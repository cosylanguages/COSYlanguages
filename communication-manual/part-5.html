Part 5 · Pragmatics

Invitations, Plans & Suggestions

Propose outings, accept warmly, or decline graciously without causing offense.

💬 Communicative Goal

Learn natural invitation structures ("Fancy grabbing a coffee?", "How about...") and cushion refusals with a polite thank-you and brief explanation.

Communicative Act Recommended Expression Usage Notes
Making a Suggestion "Why don't we go for a walk?" / "How about Friday?" Casual and collaborative way to propose a plan.
Accepting Warmly "That sounds great! Count me in." Enthusiastic and clear acceptance.
Declining Politely "I'd love to, but I'm afraid I'm busy then." Expresses appreciation before providing a soft refusal.

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

When declining an invitation, suggest an alternative time (e.g., "Rain check for next week?") to show genuine interest in connecting.

🔗 Cross-Manual Connections

Combine suggestions with hobby/free time vocabulary in Book 2 Part 11 and future plans in Book 1 Part 3.

📍 Can you use this in real life?

I can propose an activity using "How about...".
I can decline an invitation politely with a soft reason.
