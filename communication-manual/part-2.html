Communication Question: How do I keep a conversation going without awkward silences?
System 2: Keeping Conversations Alive (Sustaining the Flow)

Conversations stay alive when you combine active listening tokens with open follow-up questions to pass the turn back and forth.

Why this matters: Beginners often give one-word answers (e.g. "Yes" or "Good") which kills the conversation flow. Using active reaction tokens and returning open questions keeps the dialogue moving naturally.
🧩 System 2 Building Blocks
Right! That makes sense. Ah, interesting! Speaking of that, I actually... How about you? What do you think? What happened next?
🗺️ Conversation Flow Map
1. Listen & React Acknowledge statement ("Ah, nice!", "Right!")
2. Add Detail Share a short related personal detail ("I love that too!")
3. Pass Turn Return an open question ("How about you?")

👀 Notice

Notice how small active listening tokens like "Right", "Totally", or "Makes sense" reassure the speaker that you are engaged before you ask your next question.

🧠 Think in conversation

Think of conversation like playing catch with a ball. Giving a one-word answer drops the ball; adding a reaction and throwing back a question keeps the ball in play.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Textbook-Stiff (Dead End): Person A: "I like football." Person B: "Okay."
✅ Natural Spoken (Active Flow): Person A: "I like football." Person B: "Oh cool! Who's your favorite team?"

💡 Memory Trick

Remember the RAP Rule: React ("Nice!") + Add a thought ("I enjoy that too") + Pass the floor ("And you?").

💬 Mini Dialogue (Keeping the Catch Ball Moving)

Person A: I spent the whole weekend reading a new book.
Person B: Oh nice! I love reading on rainy days. What kind of book was it?
Person A: A mystery novel. It was super exciting! How about you, read anything good lately?
