Part 7 · Learning Context

Classroom & Speaking Club Interaction

Participate actively in speaking club sessions, pass the microphone smoothly, and ask for guidance.

💬 Communicative Goal

Thrive in speaking club sessions and group classes by passing turns ("What do you think, Alex?"), asking for feedback, and jumping into group discussions confidently.

Interaction Context Recommended Expression Goal
Passing the Turn "What's your take on this, [Name]?" Invites quiet group members to join the conversation.
Interrupting Politely "Can I jump in here for a second?" Enters the floor without coming across as rude.
Asking the Host / Teacher "How would you express this naturally?" Requests vocabulary or structural guidance during practice.

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

In speaking club rounds, aim to speak for 30–45 seconds per turn and conclude by passing the question to a peer to keep energy high.

🔗 Cross-Manual Connections

Practice classroom directions with Book 2 Part 14 and question formation in Book 1 Part 4.
