Part 3 · Pragmatics

Clarification & Conversational Repair

Keep conversations on track when you don't understand, miss a word, or need a speaker to slow down.

💬 Communicative Goal

Never pretend to understand or panic when speech is too fast. Use clear, polite repair strategies to check meaning and maintain confidence.

Clarification Need Recommended Expression Context & Purpose
Too Fast / Unclear "Could you say that again a bit slower, please?" Politely asks speaker to adjust pace without stopping the conversation.
Missing Word "What does 'x' mean in this context?" Targets the specific unknown word directly.
Double-Checking Meaning "So if I understand correctly, you mean..." Summarizes to confirm mutual agreement before proceeding.

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

Saying "Sorry, I didn't quite catch that last part" sounds much more natural than simply repeating "What?" or "Repeat!".
