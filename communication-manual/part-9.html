Part 9 · Discourse Mastery

Discourse Markers & Conversational Flow

Connect ideas logically, switch topics smoothly, and steer conversations like a native speaker.

💬 Communicative Goal

Use discourse connectors ("By the way...", "Mind you...", "Anyway...") to signal logical shifts, contrast, and topic changes seamlessly.

Discourse Function Marker / Connector Example Usage
Changing Topic "By the way..." / "Speaking of which..." "Speaking of travel, have you booked your flights?"
Returning to Main Point "Anyway..." / "As I was saying..." "Anyway, back to our weekend plans..."
Adding Nuance / Contrast "Mind you..." / "On the other hand..." "It's expensive. Mind you, the quality is top notch."

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

Discourse markers are signposts for listeners. Using "Anyway..." gently signals that you want to wrap up a tangent and move forward.
