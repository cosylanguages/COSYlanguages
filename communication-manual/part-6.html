Part 6 · Digital Speech

Digital Communication & Messaging

Navigate text messages, chat groups, casual email etiquette, and modern online registers.

💬 Communicative Goal

Master the balance between informal messaging shortcuts ("thx", "np") and clear, polite email openings/closings in professional contexts.

Channel Opening / Salutation Sign-off / Closing
Instant Chat (Casual) "Hey!" / "Quick question..." "Catch you later!" / "Cheers!"
Semi-formal Email "Hi [Name]," / "Good afternoon," "Best regards," / "Kind regards,"
Common Text Abbr. "BTW" (by the way), "IMO" (in my opinion) "TIA" (thanks in advance)

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

In text messaging, typing in ALL CAPS is perceived as yelling. Keep punctuation light and use exclamation marks sparingly to convey enthusiasm.
