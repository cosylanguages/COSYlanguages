Part 8 · Urgent Speech

Emergency, Safety & Urgent Situations

Communicate clearly, concisely, and effectively during emergencies, lost belongings, or safety hazards.

💬 Communicative Goal

Deliver immediate, direct information when safety or urgency requires dropping politeness modifiers in favour of clarity.

Emergency Context Immediate Expression Essential Information Needed
Calling for Help "Help! I need an ambulance immediately." Exact location, nature of injury or problem.
Medical Urgent Call "Is there a doctor here? He feels unwell." Symptoms, conscious state, immediate hazard.
Lost / Safety Hazard "I've lost my passport and wallet." / "Watch out!" Description of item or location of hazard.

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

In life-threatening situations or emergencies, speak short sentences starting directly with key nouns and locations (e.g., "Fire! Second floor!").

🔗 Cross-Manual Connections

Combine emergency calls with health items in Book 2 Part 2 (Body & Health) and safety phrases in Book 2 Part 14.

📍 Can you use this in real life?

I can state an emergency clearly and give my location.
I can ask for immediate medical or police assistance.
