Part 1 · Social Speech

Greetings, Introductions & Small Talk

Master entering conversations comfortably, choosing the right register, and making pleasant small talk.

💬 Communicative Goal

Learn how to open conversations smoothly using friendly greetings, introduce yourself with relevant background context, and transition into light everyday small talk.

Register Greeting & Opening Small Talk Follow-up
Casual (Friends / Peers) "Hey, how's it going?" "How was your weekend?", "Up to much today?"
Neutral (Everyday / Workplace) "Hello! How are you doing today?" "Busy day so far?", "Nice weather we're having!"
Formal (Events / Senior) "Good morning. It's a pleasure to meet you." "How has your week been treating you?"

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

When asked "How are you?", answer briefly and return the question immediately (e.g., "Good thanks! How about yourself?") to maintain conversational rhythm.

🔗 Cross-Manual Connections

Reinforce your introductions with vocabulary from Book 2 Part 1 (People & Personal Info) and simple present rules in Book 1 Part 1.
