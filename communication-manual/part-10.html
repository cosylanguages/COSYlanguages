System 10: Conversation & Story Maps (Applied Integration)

Story Maps connect openings, floor control, reactions, opinions, repair, and exits into complete, fluent conversational journeys.

Why this matters: Isolated phrases only take you so far. Real fluency comes from connecting turn roles seamlessly in multi-turn dialogues across any scenario: social parties, meetings, or casual catch-ups.
🧩 System 10 Building Blocks
Guess what happened yesterday? So basically, what happened was... No way! You're kidding! What did you do after that? Anyway, that's my crazy story!
🗺️ Complete Story Map Flow
1. Hook Story Hook ("Guess what happened?")
2. Reaction Peer Reaction Token ("No way! Really?")
3. Core Story Narrative detail ("So basically...")
4. Wrap-up Punchline & Exit ("Anyway, that was crazy!")

👀 Notice

Notice how a full story map weaves together all 5 block types: Start (Hook), React (Listener), Continue (Narrative), Ask (Follow-up), and Close (Punchline exit).

🧠 Think in conversation

Storytelling in speech is interactive, not a monologue. Listeners use reaction blocks to fuel the speaker's narrative momentum.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Textbook-Stiff (Static Script): "Yesterday I woke up. I went to store. I bought milk."
✅ Natural Spoken (Story Arc): "Guess what happened yesterday? So basically, I ran into my old teacher at the grocery store!"

💡 Memory Trick

Think of a Story Map like a roller coaster: Hook them at the top, Drive through the narrative turns, and Land softly at the end!
