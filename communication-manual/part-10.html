Part 10 · Applied Scenarios

Situational Roleplays & Real-World Dialogues

Put everything together: complete real-world conversation blueprints for everyday life.

💬 Communicative Goal

Apply greetings, requests, politeness, clarification, and discourse connectors in complete, multi-turn dialogues across common scenarios.

Scenario Roleplay Blueprint Key Speech Combination
Café / Restaurant Greeting ➔ Ordering ➔ Customizing ➔ Paying ➔ Thanks "I'd like a cappuccino, please. Could I have oat milk?"
Hotel Check-in Greeting ➔ Reservation check ➔ Special request ➔ Confirmation "Hi, I have a reservation under Smith. Is quiet room possible?"
Social Party / Gathering Opening icebreaker ➔ Opinion sharing ➔ Follow-up ➔ Friendly sign-off "Great party! How do you know Alex? We should catch up soon."

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

In real-world interactions, combining polite greetings, soft requests, and warm thanks creates an instantly positive impression.
