Part 4 · Pragmatics

Stating Opinions & Preferences

Share your ideas, agree warmly, and disagree gently without sounding aggressive.

💬 Communicative Goal

Frame opinions clearly ("In my view...", "I reckon...") and cushion disagreement ("I see your point, but...") to foster positive interaction.

Function Recommended Phrase Register & Tone
Stating Opinion "In my opinion..." / "From my perspective..." Neutral / Professional. Clearly indicates subjective view.
Soft Agreement "I completely agree!" / "That's a very good point." Warm, supportive, and encouraging.
Polite Disagreement "I see what you mean, but..." / "I'm not so sure about that." Softens conflict while presenting an alternative view.

💡 Pragmatic Rule of Thumb

Never say "You are wrong!" in social speech. Instead, validate the other person's input first before sharing your opinion (e.g., "Interesting point, though I see it slightly differently...").

🔗 Cross-Manual Connections
