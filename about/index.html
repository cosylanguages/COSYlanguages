🏡 Our Story & Philosophy

We are not a school.

There are no grades here, no falling behind, no bell that tells you when to stop. There's no pass or fail — only how far you've come since the last time you tried.

We're also not an app. No streak that guilts you, no leaderboard pretending to be a friend. Behind every lesson is a real teacher who remembers what you said last week.

What we are is closer to a small, warm place you can return to — a living room for speaking, a kitchen table for daily practice, a mailbox for a little magazine that's sometimes got your own words in it, and eventually, a radio playing something slow enough to understand.

Come in. Take your shoes off. Let's talk.
