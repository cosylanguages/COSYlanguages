How it began

COSYlanguages began as a community for language teachers: a place where teachers taught each other new languages and compared notes on methodology.

During the isolation of the covid years, we opened it to everyone. It stopped being only for teachers and became a community for anyone who wants to learn languages and be part of something.

Our founder, JY DM, kept learning more languages and kept studying how they work. The goal became a comparative system for language learning, and a special method to practise languages and actually retain them. That is where the project became what it is today.

What we do today

  • Develop methodology and learning methods that help you retain and expand a language, not just meet it once
  • Teach languages, one-to-one and in groups
  • Compare languages side by side
  • Protect languages and help them stay alive
  • Build a community for language nerds, for learners, and for anyone who wants to keep their languages in good shape
  • Run speaking clubs that help people avoid isolation, make friends, exchange ideas and have fun

What we believe

🗣️ Speak from the first lesson

Language is meant for real connection. You start speaking from lesson one, using grammar and vocabulary as tools to communicate naturally.

🌱 Genuine learning over monetisation

We focus on real student progress, transparent pricing, and zero sales pressure. Learning comes first in everything we do.

🎓 Teachers who are learners

Our teachers are active language learners themselves. We know the challenges, joys, and real feelings of learning a new language.

How we teach

We offer 1-to-1 online lessons and small group lessons in English, French, Italian and Russian designed around your real life and goals. Explore our full range of courses on our courses page.

Free tools

We build and maintain interactive study tools so you can practise speaking, vocabulary, and grammar whenever you want.

  • Language Practice: Exercises with points and daily streaks.
  • COSYgames: Interactive language games for solo and group play.
  • COSYtools Reference Hub: Conjugation, gender checking, and grammar reference tools.
