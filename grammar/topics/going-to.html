Going To

Plans, intentions & predictions

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Holiday plans
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
II am going to travel.I am not going to travel.Am I going to travel?
He / She / ItHe is going to travel.He isn't going to travel.Is he going to travel?
You / We / TheyWe are going to travel.We aren't going to travel.Are we going to travel?

Use: plans, intentions and predictions based on present evidence. Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.

∞ Connects to what you already know

Present Continuous — going to uses the exact same am/is/are + -ing pattern you already know, just add ‘going to’ after it.
