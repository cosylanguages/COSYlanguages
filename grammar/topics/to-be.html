The Verb 'To Be'

Am, is, are · affirmative, negative & question

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Jobs & Occupations →
🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can build clear, accurate sentences and communicate confidently in everyday situations.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe the examples below. Pay attention to word order, endings, and sentence patterns before reading the rule.

★ Why this matters

To be is the most common verb in English by far · and it's the building block for structures you'll meet soon, like the continuous tenses (is/are + -ing).

Use: names, nationality, age, feelings, jobs and facts. She is French. He is 25. We are happy. They are doctors.

💭 Think like a native speaker

English squeezes a lot onto one tiny irregular verb. Many languages · Russian included · skip 'to be' in the present tense and just place the two things side by side.

More examples
  • I'm a student.
  • We are from France.
  • My parents are teachers.
  • Is it Monday today?
