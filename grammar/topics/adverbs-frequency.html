Adverbs of Frequency

How often something happens

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Daily habits
never~0%
rarely~10%
sometimes~50%
often~70%
usually~80%
always~100%
Freq.WordExample
~0%NEVERI never drink coffee at night.
~10%RARELYShe rarely eats fast food.
~30%OCCASIONALLYThey occasionally visit their grandparents.
~50%SOMETIMESI sometimes walk to school.
~70%OFTENJohn often plays football with friends.
~80%USUALLYShe usually gets up at seven.
~100%ALWAYSHe always brushes his teeth before bed.

Position: adverbs of frequency go before the main verb (I often read) but after the verb to be (She is always late). These percentages are approximate · a rough guide to compare the words, not exact maths.
