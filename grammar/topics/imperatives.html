Imperatives

Instructions, rules & directions

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Classroom instructions
(NO SUBJECT):
+
VERB (base)Close
+
OBJECTthe door
Example
instructionOpen the door, please.
rule / warningDon't run in the classroom.
directionTurn left and go straight on.
recipe / orderAdd two eggs and mix well.

Form: base verb (no subject) for instructions, directions, rules and recipes. Add don't for the negative. Sit down. Don't touch that. Please wait here.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Dropping the subject entirely is unusual for English, which normally insists on a stated subject in every sentence · imperatives are one of the few exceptions.

⚠️ Common mistakes
❌You close the door.

✅Close the door, please.
(no subject in imperatives)

✅Don't touch that.

∞ Connects to what you already know

Present Simple · the base verb here is identical to the I/you/we/they form from that page, no extra -s.

More examples
  • Close the window, please.
  • Don't be late for class.
  • Listen carefully.
  • Wash your hands before dinner.
