Part 2 &middot; Verbs & Tenses

Like / Love / Hate + -ing

Talking about preferences

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Hobbies & Sports →
🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can build clear, accurate sentences and communicate confidently in everyday situations.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe the examples below. Pay attention to word order, endings, and sentence patterns before reading the rule.

hatedislike
don't mindmild
likepositive
lovestrong
strong like · LOVEI love dancing on Friday nights.
like · LIKEShe likes reading before bed.
no preference · DON'T MINDHe doesn't mind waiting a little.
strong dislike · HATEThey hate cleaning the kitchen.

Rule: after like, love, hate, don't mind, enjoy, the next verb takes the -ing form. I like swimming.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Treating a verb as a noun (swimming, reading) by adding -ing is a very English trick · many languages use a completely different infinitive-style construction here instead.
