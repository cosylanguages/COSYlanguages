Pronouns

Subject, object, possessive adjective & possessive pronoun

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: People you know

★ Why this matters

Pronouns are some of the most frequent words in English · you'll use I, you, it and their forms in almost every sentence you ever speak.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Notice pronouns still change form (I/me, he/him) even though almost no other English word does · this is a leftover from Old English, which marked grammar with endings the way Russian or German still do.

1. Choose the correct possessive pronoun: 'This bag is ___.'
Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) stand alone, without a noun after them.
2. Which is correct?
The subject pronoun is I, and it pairs with am: I am happy.
3. 'The dog wags ___ tail.' Which word fits?
'Its' (no apostrophe) is the possessive adjective for 'it'.
