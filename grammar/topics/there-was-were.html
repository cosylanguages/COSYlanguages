There was/were & There will be

Talking about the past and the future

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Travel & Trips →
Now
there was a cat
there is a cat
there will be a party
pastfuture
TenseAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
Past, oneThere was a cat in the garden.There wasn't a cat in the garden.Was there a cat in the garden?
Past, manyThere were two cats in the garden.There weren't two cats in the garden.Were there two cats in the garden?
FutureThere will be a party.There won't be a party.Will there be a party?

Use there was/were for things that existed before but may not exist now, and there will be for things that don't exist yet.

More examples
  • There was a party at my house last week.
  • There were a lot of people there.
  • There will be a test tomorrow.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Was/were still splits by number (I was / we were) even though nearly every other English verb lost that distinction long ago · 'to be' is the most stubbornly irregular survivor in the whole language.
