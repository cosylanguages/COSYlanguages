Past Simple: Irregular Verbs

Verbs that don't follow the -ed rule

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: A trip

💡 Regular vs. irregular

Regular verbs: add -ed. But common verbs like go, have, see are irregular — they change completely, with no -ed. Learn them one by one.

I went home yesterday
pastfuture
AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
She worked / She went home.She didn't work / didn't go home.Did she work / go home?

💡 Memory trick

Did is the magic word: once you use it, the main verb always goes back to its plain base form — even irregular ones. Did she go? never Did she went?

Common irregular verbs (go → went, have → had, see → saw…) don't follow the -ed rule at all — see the full grouped list in the Appendix: Irregular Verbs.
