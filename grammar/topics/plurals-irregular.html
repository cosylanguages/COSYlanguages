💡 Some words don't change at all

A few common nouns look exactly the same in the singular and the plural · the sentence tells you which one is meant: one sheep / three sheep, one fish / three fish, one deer / three deer.

💡 Memory trick

Foot → feet, tooth → teeth · say them together and hear it: the double OO sound stretches into a double EE sound.
