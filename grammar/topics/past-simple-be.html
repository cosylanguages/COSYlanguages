Past Simple of 'To Be'

Was & were

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Yesterday
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / He / She / ItI was at home.I wasn't at work.Was I late?
You / We / TheyWe were at home.We weren't at work.Were you late?

More examples
  • Where were you yesterday?
  • It wasn't sunny this morning.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Was/were is the only place in modern English where a verb still changes for singular vs plural in the past — everywhere else, the past tense form is identical for every person.

∞ Connects to what you already know

The Verb ‘To Be’ — was/were are just am/is/are moved into the past, same verb, one step back in time.
