Will

Future predictions, promises & offers

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Predictions about the future

💡 A first look : fully practised at A2

Will is included here so you can make simple offers and promises (I'll help you) · but most exam frameworks treat will as full A2 content. Don't worry if it still feels new; going to is the one to master solidly first.

AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I will help you. (I'll)I will not help you. (won't)Will you help me?

Use: quick decisions, promises, offers and predictions without evidence. I'll answer the phone. I think it will be sunny tomorrow.

🌐 Around the English-speaking world

UK Shall I…? / Shall we…? is common for offers and suggestions: Shall we go?

USCAAU Shall is rare; people prefer Should I…?, Do you want to…? or How about…?

2. Which never changes form?
Will never takes -s, even with he/she/it: she will come.
