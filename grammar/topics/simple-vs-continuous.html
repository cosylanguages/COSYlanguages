Present Simple vs Continuous

Choosing the right tense

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Free time

★ Why this matters

Mixing these two up is one of the most common mistakes at this level — choosing the right one changes whether you sound like you're describing a habit or something happening right now.

Present SimplePresent Continuous
Routines and habitsActions happening right now
Facts and general truthsTemporary situations
Permanent situationsChanging or new habits
She drinks tea every morning.She is drinking tea right now.
Now
I work every day
I'm working now
pastfuture
More examples
  • I usually walk to school, but today I'm taking the bus.
  • He plays football every Saturday. Right now he's playing basketball.
