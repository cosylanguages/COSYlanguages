Part 3 &middot; Describing Things

Adjectives

Describing people & things

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Describing people

★ Why this matters

Adjectives let you describe almost anything · people, places, feelings, objects. This is one of the most immediately useful vocabulary-grammar combinations you'll learn.

ADJECTIVEred
+
NOUNapple

Rule: in English, adjectives go before the noun and never take a plural -s. a red apple → two red apples (not reds).
size · big / smallThey live in a big house with a small garden.
feeling · happyThe children are very happy today.
price · expensive / cheapThis bag is expensive but that one is cheap.
opinion · beautifulThis is a beautiful photo.
difficulty · difficult / easyGrammar can be difficult, but practice makes it easy.
