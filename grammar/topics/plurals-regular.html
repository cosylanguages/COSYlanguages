-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x: + esbus → buses, box → boxes

vowel + y: + sday → days, toy → toys
some -f / -fe: → vesleaf → leaves, knife → knives

💡 Why -s isn't always enough

Does the word end in -s, -x, -ch or -sh? Add -es: box → boxes. Does it end in consonant + y? Change y to -ies: baby → babies.

Spelling check: say the word out loud. If adding just -s would be hard to say (busess, boxs), that's your sign you need -es instead.
