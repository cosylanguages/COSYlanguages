Part 3 &middot; Describing Things

Much / Many / A few / A little

Talking about larger and smaller quantities

📱 Vocabulary theme for practice: Shopping &amp; quantities
Noun typeWordExample
countable (plural)many / a lot ofHow many apples? · I have a lot of apples.
uncountablemuch / a lot ofHow much water? · I have a lot of water.
small quantity (countable)a fewI have a few friends here.
small quantity (uncountable)a littleThere is a little milk left.

💡 Much is rare outside questions &amp; negatives

In affirmative sentences, native speakers usually prefer a lot of over much: I have a lot of water sounds more natural than I have much water. Much is common in questions and negatives: How much…? / I don't have much…
