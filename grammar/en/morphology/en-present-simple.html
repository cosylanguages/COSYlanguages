English — Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for routines, habits, and facts.

Why is this important? You will use this grammar almost every time you speak. It forms the bedrock of basic conversations, allowing you to talk about your daily life, family, and standard routines.
Sentence Formulas & Building Blocks
Subject + Verb(s) + Object
⭐ Affirmative Sentences (Subject + Verb + Object)
I / You / We / They I like pizza. No ending added to the verb.
He / She / It She reads books. Add -s or -es to the verb.
They They play football. No ending added to the verb.
He He drinks tea. Add -s to the verb.

👀 Notice

Which ending disappears when we make a sentence negative using "doesn't"?

💡 Memory Trick

Think of -s as the "singular supervisor". He, She, and It are solo players, so they always need the singular supervisor (-s) to help the verb.

⚠ Common Mistakes
✗ Incorrect Sentence ✓ Correct Standard Sentence
He work in London. He works in London.
She doesn't likes tea. She doesn't like tea. (no double -s!)

🧠 Think like a native speaker

English cares about word order. Since we don't have many word endings, the position of each word tells the brain who does what. Keep the Subject-Verb-Object order firm!

🔗 This prepares you for the Past Simple and the Present Continuous.
