Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Used To

Past habits and states that have changed

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss childhood habits, past routines, and old states that are no longer true today.
AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I used to play football.I didn't use to play football.Did you use to play football?

Use: repeated past habits or states that are not true anymore. I used to live in Spain (but I don't now). Only for the past — there is no present form of used to.

👀 Notice: Two different 'used to'

Don't confuse with be used to (= to be accustomed to something, not about past habits). Used to play = a past habit. I'm used to the cold = I'm accustomed to it now, a different structure entirely.

More examples
  • She used to have long hair.
  • We didn't use to like vegetables.
  • Did you use to walk to school?
