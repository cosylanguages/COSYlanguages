Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Past Continuous

An action in progress in the past

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe background scenes in stories and actions happening at specific past moments.
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / He / She / ItI was working.I wasn't working.Was I working?
You / We / TheyWe were working.We weren't working.Were we working?

Use: an action in progress at a specific moment in the past. At 8pm, I was having dinner. Also for background scenes in a story: The sun was shining and birds were singing.

More examples
  • This time yesterday, I was flying to London.
  • What were you doing at midnight?
