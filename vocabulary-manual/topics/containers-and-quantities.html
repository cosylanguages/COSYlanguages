Containers & Quantities

How to talk about amounts of food and drink

WordMeaningExample
a cup of tea / coffeeamount held by one cupWould you like a cup of tea?
a glass of water / juiceamount held by one glassI'd like a glass of water.
a bottle of water / wineliquid stored in bottleWe bought a bottle of water.
a can of soda / soupsealed metal containerHe drank a can of soda.
a bag of rice / sugarquantity sold in bagWe need a bag of rice.
a box of cereal / chocolatesquantity sold in boxShe bought a box of chocolates.
a piece of cake / breadone part cut from wholeCan I have a piece of cake?
a slice of bread / pizzaflat thin piece cutHe ate a slice of pizza.
a kilo of apples / potatoesweight metric unit (1000g)I bought a kilo of apples.
a litre of milk / watervolume metric unit for liquidsWe need a litre of milk.
