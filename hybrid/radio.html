Audio Immersion Progress
From 5-minute podcasts to a live weekly conversation hour.
Phase 1 starts with slow-speech podcast episodes. Phase 2 introduces weekly live audio hours. Phase 3 evolves into a 24/7 internet radio stream.
🌙 Why this exists

I couldn't help but wonder... why do we ask learners to understand native speech before we've ever taught them to simply sit with a voice, unhurried? Somewhere between silence and full speed, there's a pace we forgot to offer. COSY Radio starts there — slow enough to lean into, familiar enough to feel like someone speaking just to you, one small episode at a time.

🎙️ Audio Roadmap

The Evolution of COSY Radio

Building an accessible, natural listening habit for foreign language learners.

1

🎙️ Phase 1: Slow-Speech Podcasts

5–10 minute digestible episodes: Word of the Day, slow-speech news recaps, and culture breakdowns.

2

📻 Phase 2: Live Weekly Hour

A live-streamed weekly conversation hour hosted by COSY teachers with guest student call-ins!

3

📡 Phase 3: 24/7 COSY Stream

A continuous internet radio stream featuring curated music, podcasts, and Speaking Club highlights.
