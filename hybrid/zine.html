🌙 Why this exists

I couldn't help but wonder... at what point does a learner stop reading for the lesson and start reading for themselves? Somewhere between the textbook and the novel, there's an empty shelf — nothing short enough, warm enough, or theirs enough. COSYzine is our attempt to fill that shelf, one folded page at a time, and to leave a little room in it for the learners' own words too — because being read is its own kind of fluency.

What's in Every Issue?

Crafted specifically for A2–B1 learners across English, French, Italian, Russian, and Greek.

1

📰 Wonder of the Month

This month's writing prompt, borrowed straight from our "I Couldn't Help But Wonder" Speaking Club. Submit your own 100-word "wondering" for a chance to see it printed.

2

🕵️ Idiom Detective

A short riddle built from three real idioms in your language. Solve it first, get named in the next issue.

3

📐 This Month's Grammar, Decoded

One tricky grammar point per language, explained the way our Conjugation & Gender Engines do: patterns, not rules to memorise.

4

🚧 False Friend of the Month

One dangerous look-alike word per language pair, illustrated as a tiny comic misunderstanding, so you never make that mistake out loud.

5

🗣️ Borrowed from the Streets

What people actually say vs. what the textbook says, plus when not to use it.

6

💌 Reader Mailbag & Learner Voices

Real reactions to last issue, and short submitted stories from students learning right now, printed with light corrections.
