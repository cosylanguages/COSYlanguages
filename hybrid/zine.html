🌙 Why this exists

I couldn't help but wonder... at what point does a learner stop reading for the lesson and start reading for themselves? Somewhere between the textbook and the novel, there's an empty shelf — nothing short enough, warm enough, or theirs enough. COSYzine is our attempt to fill that shelf, one folded page at a time, and to leave a little room in it for the learners' own words too — because being read is its own kind of fluency.

✍️ Submit Your Text to 'Learner Voices'

Are you learning English, French, Italian, Russian, or Greek with us or in your own time? Write a short text (100–250 words) about a funny misunderstanding, a breakthrough moment, or your favorite local spot. Selected texts will be published in our printed COSYzine issues with language corrections!

Guidelines for Learner Submissions:

  • Length: 100 to 250 words
  • Language: English, French, Italian, Russian, or Greek
  • Level: Any level (A1 to C1 welcome!)
  • Topic: Language journey, cultural reflection, or travel story
