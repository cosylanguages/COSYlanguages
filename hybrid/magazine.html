Editorial Standard

The 10 Recurring Magazine Desks in Every Issue

Every issue of COSYmagazine is intentionally structured around communicative production rather than passive rule retention. Here is what every edition incorporates:

📰 Front Page / Editorial Room

The monthly lead story covering language psychology, overcoming conversational freeze, or the pedagogical design of our multi-language curriculum.

🔤 Words Across 14 Languages

High-frequency active vocabulary: 10 essential verbs, 50 conversational phrase upgrades, and strong adjectives systematically mapped across 14 languages.

📐 Grammar Made Cosy

Visual timeline representations of verb aspect, tense logic, and phrasal verb schemas designed to eliminate rote memorization.

🗓️ Saturday Mistakes

Diagnostic error clinic dissecting 5 pervasive learner traps, false friends (*faux amis*), and phonological interference with direct spoken alternatives.

🗣️ Say It & Sentence Frames

Conversational scaffolding with 10 reusable sentence frames enabling learners to produce complex thoughts immediately without word-by-word mental translation.

🎙️ COSYtalks & Podcast Decks

Audio companion episodes linked to interactive 16:9 Stage Mode (?stage=1) for classrooms and Teleprompter Script Mode (?script=1) for learner recordings.

🎭 Culture, Quotes & Nuance

Linguistic insights, untranslatable cultural terms (*meraki*, *gemütlich*, *dolce far niente*), and sound shifts connecting cognate families.

💭 Learner Voices & Reflections

Real 100–250 word authentic submissions from students sharing funny cross-cultural blunders, aha moments, and speaking club stories.

🗞️ Tactile Print Studio Zine

Every issue pairs with single-sheet 8-page foldable pocket zines, broadsheet PDFs, or Leitner flashcards to take practice off-screen.

🎉 Practice & Live Events Bridge

Direct deep-links connecting each article to daily exercises on COSYpractice and upcoming Cinema Club or Speaking Club themes on COSYevents.
