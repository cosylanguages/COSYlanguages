🔗 Bridge Tools

Connect paper flashcards to digital audio & games.

Printable cards shouldn't live in isolation. Bridge Tools generate QR codes that link your physical zines and flashcards back to online audio pronunciations, interactive quizzes, and Speaking Club sessions.

Generate QR Code ↓ Printable Tools Hub ↓
The Online ↔ Offline Bridge
Scan a printed card to listen to real audio or jump into a game.
Stick generated QR codes onto your Leitner cards, pocket zines, or textbook notes to bridge your offline review with online practice.
🌙 Why this exists

I couldn't help but wonder... how many flashcards have we ever printed, only to lose them somewhere between the desk and the drawer? A card without its sound is only half a memory. Bridge Tools exist because paper deserves a way back to the voice that gave it meaning — one small scan, and a silent card remembers how to speak.
