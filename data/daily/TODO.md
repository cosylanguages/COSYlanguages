# Native-speaker review: daily fun facts

Every language now has 30 curated facts (English 41) covering history, literature, etymology,
grammar, script, major figures and language policy. They were written from reliable general
knowledge, but each still needs a check by a native speaker for wording, nuance and factual
accuracy before the dataset is considered final.

When reviewing a file, check each fact and fix or remove anything that is wrong, outdated or
unclear. Keep the `id` stable so downstream references do not break.
