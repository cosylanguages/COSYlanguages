Past Tenses Review

A fast B1 refresher

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can accurately structure narrative backgrounds and earlier past actions.
TenseJobExample
Past Simplethe main eventsShe opened the door.
Past Continuousthe background sceneIt was raining.
Past Perfect (Simple / Continuous)the earlier pastShe had already left. / She'd been waiting for hours.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌When I arrived, the film already started.
tap to see the correct form
✅When I arrived, the film had already started.
❌She was tired because she ran all morning.
tap to see the correct form
✅She was tired because she'd been running all morning.
