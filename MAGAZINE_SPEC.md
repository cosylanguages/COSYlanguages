# COSYmagazine: Master Content & Editorial Architecture Specification

This document defines the complete content blueprint, pedagogical standards, and issue architecture for **COSYmagazine** across the COSYlanguages ecosystem.

---

## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Philosophy

COSYmagazine is a monthly speaking-first digital publication and tactile printable gazette designed for language learners. Rather than presenting isolated grammar tables or passive textbook dialogues, every issue is crafted around actionable production: **sentence frames**, **circumlocution strategies**, **lexical upgrades**, **demystified grammar timelines**, and **audio-first stage presentations**.

### Core Guiding Principles:
1. **Speaking-First from Day One**: Every article answers the question: *"What can the learner say aloud tomorrow?"*
2. **CELTA & CEFR Grounded**: Content spans CEFR A0 through C1, structured with explicit communicative goals and diagnostic rubrics.
3. **14-Language Cross-Linguistic Cohesion**: Core high-frequency verbs, sentence frames, and linguistic shifts are mapped directly across all 14 catalog languages:
   - **Indo-European**: English (`en`), French (`fr`), Italian (`it`), Spanish (`es`), Portuguese (`pt`), German (`de`), Russian (`ru`), Greek (`el`), Armenian (`hy`).
   - **Kartvelian**: Georgian (`ka`).
   - **Turkic**: Tatar (`tt`), Bashkir (`ba`), Chuvash (`cv`).
   - **Celtic**: Breton (`br`).
4. **Digital + Tactile Hybrid Integration**: Every issue pairs with:
   - Interactive web articles with **Stage Mode** (`?stage=1`) and **Teleprompter Script Mode** (`?script=1`).
   - Tactile printable companion formats (8-page foldable pocket zines, broadsheet gazettes, Leitner flashcards).
   - Direct bridges to interactive drills in **COSYpractice** and live speaking clubs in **COSYevents**.

## 2. The 10 Recurring Magazine Desks

Every COSYmagazine edition contains a curated selection from the following 10 editorial desks:

| # | Desk | Key & Icon | Purpose & Pedagogical Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Front Page / Editorial Room** | `Front Page` 📰 | The monthly lead story. Focuses on language learning psychology, neuroplasticity, overcoming plateaus, or curriculum architecture. |
| 2 | **Words (14 Target Languages)** | `Words` 🔤 | High-frequency foundational vocabulary: 10 essential verbs, 50 conversational upgrades, 50 strong adjectives, and precise synonym grids mapped across all 14 languages. |
| 3 | **Grammar Made Cosy** | `Grammar Made Cosy` / `Grammar` 📐 | Visual timeline guides, aspect vs. tense clarity, the underlying logic of phrasal verbs, and deconstructing structural rules without dry drills. |
| 4 | **Saturday Mistakes** | `Saturday Mistakes` 🗓️ | Diagnostic breakdown of 5 common learner traps, false friends (*faux amis* / *falsi amici*), phonological interference, and natural conversational fixes. |
| 5 | **Say It & Sentence Frames** | `Say It` 🗣️ | Ready-to-use conversational scaffolding, circumlocution tools ("What to say when you don't know the word"), and conversational bridge phrases. |
| 6 | **COSYtalks & Podcast Decks** | `COSYtalks` / `The Podcast` 🎙️ | Accompanying audio episodes with dual modes: interactive 16:9 presentation slides (`?stage=1`) for classrooms and teleprompter scripts (`?script=1`) for self-recording. |
| 7 | **Culture, Quotes & Nuances** | `Culture & Quotes` 🎭 | Idiomatic expressions, untranslatable cultural concepts (*gemütlich*, *meraki*, *dolce far niente*), literary micro-sparks, and historical sound shifts. |
| 8 | **Learner Voices & Community** | `Reflections` 💭 | 100–250 word authentic student submissions, funny misunderstandings, breakthrough milestones, and reflections from the global COSY community. |
| 9 | **Tactile Print Studio Companion** | `Back Issues` / `Zine` 🗞️ | Single-sheet fold-out pocket zines (via `print-studio/print-zine.html`), broadsheet PDFs, and double-sided Leitner flashcard sheets. |
| 10 | **Practice & Live Events Bridge** | `Cosy Events` 🎉 | Deep-linked interactive exercises on COSYpractice matching the issue's vocabulary/grammar, plus upcoming Cinema Club and Speaking Club themes on COSYevents. |

## 4. Front-End Technical Specifications

1. **Procedural Generative Visuals**:
   - Every article includes custom SVG header art generated by `renderCover()` using algorithmic motifs (*riso-print*, *gingham-knit*, *notebook-handwritten*, *linocut*).
2. **Interactive Presentation Modes**:
   - `?stage=1`: Launches 16:9 dark-mode classroom presentation slides.
   - `?script=1`: Formats the article into a scrolling teleprompter script for learner speech practice and podcast recordings.
3. **Accessibility & Responsive Typography**:
   - Self-hosted variable typography (`Fraunces` for headlines, `Commissioner` and `DM Sans` for body).
   - High-contrast, dark-mode compliant palette tokens (`--ink`, `--sage`, `--cream`, `--terracotta`).
