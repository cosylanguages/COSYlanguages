## Executive Overview & Architectural System

This master specification defines the elementary curriculum for COSYlanguages A2 English (combining **A2.1** and **A2.2** sub-levels). At the A2 level, learners transition from survival phrases to connected functional communication across social, travel, work, and narrative contexts.

## PART 1 — PERSONAL LIFE, RELATIONSHIPS & CHARACTER (A2.1 & A2.2)

### UNIT 1: Detailed Personal Profiles & Life History (A2.1)
- **1.1 Life History & Biography**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Biography, background, upbringing, childhood, youth, education, degree, career, milestone, memory, experience.
  - **Essential Verbs:** Be born, grow up, move, graduate, start work, retire, change, remember, recall.
  - **Collocations:** Early childhood, family background, graduate from university, career milestone.
  - **Set Phrases:** Where were you born? / I was born in 1998 in a small town. / I grew up in Chicago. / When did you graduate?
  - **Micro-Dialogue:**
    - *A:* Where did you grow up?
    - *B:* I grew up in Manchester, but we moved to London when I was twelve.
- **1.2 Personality Traits & Character Descriptions**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Personality, character, trait, quality, flaw, behavior, attitude.
  - **Adjectives:** Reliable, hard-working, lazy, generous, selfish, patient, impatient, stubborn, flexible, moody, cheerful, shy, outgoing, polite, rude, honest, dishonest.
  - **Collocations:** Warm personality, reliable friend, hard-working student, stubborn attitude.
  - **Set Phrases:** What is she like? / She is very hard-working and reliable. / He can be a bit stubborn sometimes.

### UNIT 2: Social Circles, Friendships & Networking (A2.2)
- **2.1 Social Circles & Friendship Dynamics**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Circle of friends, acquaintance, childhood friend, close friend, social gathering, party, invitation, host, guest.
  - **Verbs:** Make friends, get along with, hang out, catch up, invite, accept, decline, keep in touch, lose touch.
  - **Set Phrases:** How did you meet your best friend? / We've known each other since school. / Do you get along with your neighbours?
- **2.2 Conflict, Apologies & Resolutions**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Argument, disagreement, mistake, misunderstanding, apology, forgiveness, peace.
  - **Verbs:** Argue, fight, disagree, apologize, forgive, resolve, make up, regret.
  - **Set Phrases:** I'm so sorry about that. / I didn't mean to hurt you. / Don't worry about it. / Let me make it up to you.

## PART 2 — HOUSING, NEIGHBOURHOOD & LIVING CONDITIONS (A2.1 & A2.2)

### UNIT 3: Renting, Buying & Housing Features (A2.1)
- **3.1 Property Listings & Rental Terms**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Property, advert, rent, deposit, landlord, landlady, tenant, contract, lease, agency, agent, rent payment, utilities, bills.
  - **Verbs:** Rent, let, lease, sign, pay, move in, move out, share.
  - **Set Phrases:** How much is the monthly rent? / Are bills included in the price? / When is the flat available?

  - **Core Vocabulary:** Problem, repair, maintenance, leak, crack, noise, heating, radiator, pipe, plumber, electrician, lock, key.
  - **Verbs & Adjectives:** Break, fix, repair, leak, overflow, replace, broken, noisy, cold.
  - **Set Phrases:** The heating isn't working. / There is a leak in the bathroom. / Could you send someone to fix it?

### UNIT 4: Neighbourhoods & City Life (A2.2)
- **4.1 Local Neighbourhood Amenities**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Suburb, district, area, convenience store, dry cleaner, recycling point, playground, pedestrian zone.
  - **Set Phrases:** Is it a safe neighbourhood? / Everything is within walking distance. / There is a great park nearby.
- **4.2 City vs. Country Living**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** City center, countryside, nature, fresh air, traffic jam, noise pollution, lifestyle, pace of life.
  - **Set Phrases:** City life is fast and exciting. / I prefer the peace and quiet of the countryside.

# SECTION 2: COSYlanguages COMPLETE ENGLISH GRAMMAR A2 (GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE)

---

## PART 1 — TENSES & ASPECT (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Past Simple vs. Past Continuous:** Actions in progress interrupted by past events (*I was sleeping when the alarm went off*).
- **Present Perfect Simple:** Life experiences with *ever/never*, duration with *for/since*, recent events with *just/already/yet*.
- **Future Expressions Contrast:** *Be going to* (intentions) vs *Present Continuous* (fixed arrangements) vs *Will* (predictions/offers).

## PART 2 — MODAL VERBS & EXPRESSING OBLIGATION (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Have to / Don't have to:** External obligation vs absence of obligation.
- **Must / Mustn't:** Rules and personal obligation vs strict prohibition.
- **Should / Shouldn't:** Advice and recommendations.
- **Could / Would you mind:** Polite requests and permission.

## PART 3 — CONDITIONALS & COMPARISONS (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Zero Conditional:** General truths and scientific facts (*If you freeze water, it turns to ice*).
- **First Conditional:** Real future possibilities (*If it rains tomorrow, we will stay inside*).
- **Second Conditional Intro:** Hypothetical present/future situations (*If I had more money, I would travel more*).
- **Extended Comparatives:** *much more expensive*, *a bit cheaper*, *as... as*, *too / not enough*.

## PART 4 — CLAUSES, CONNECTORS & REPORTED SPEECH (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Defining Relative Clauses:** *who, which, that, where, whose*.
- **Discourse Connectors:** *although, however, because, so, in addition, for example*.
- **Basic Reported Speech:** Tense shift in simple statements (*He said he was tired*).
