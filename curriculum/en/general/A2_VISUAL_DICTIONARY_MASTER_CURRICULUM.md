# SECTION 2: COSYlanguages COMPLETE ENGLISH GRAMMAR A2 (GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE)

## PART 1 — TENSES & ASPECT (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Past Simple vs. Past Continuous:** Ongoing actions interrupted by past events (*I was sleeping when the phone rang*).
- **Present Perfect Simple:** Experience (*ever/never*), duration (*for/since*), recent events (*just/already/yet*).
- **Future Contrast:** *Be going to* (intentions) vs *Present Continuous* (fixed arrangements) vs *Will* (predictions/promises).

## PART 2 — MODAL VERBS & EXPRESSING OBLIGATION (A2.1 & A2.2)
- *Have to / Don't have to* (external obligation vs lack of obligation).
- *Must / Mustn't* (rules vs prohibition).
- *Should / Shouldn't* (advice & recommendations).
- *Could / Would you mind* (polite requests).

## PART 3 — CONDITIONALS & COMPARISONS (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Zero Conditional:** General truths (*If you heat ice, it melts*).
- **First Conditional:** Real future possibilities (*If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home*).
- **Second Conditional Intro:** Imagined situations (*If I had more time, I would travel*).
- **Extended Comparatives & Superlatives:** *much more expensive*, *by far the best*, *as... as*, *too / not enough*.

## PART 4 — CLAUSES, CONNECTORS & REPORTED SPEECH (A2.1 & A2.2)
- **Relative Clauses:** *who, which, that, where, whose*.
- **Discourse Connectors:** *although, however, so, because, as a result, in addition*.
- **Basic Reported Speech:** *He said that..., She told me that...*.
