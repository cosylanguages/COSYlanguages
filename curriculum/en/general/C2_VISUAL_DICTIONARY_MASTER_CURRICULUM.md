## PART 1 — NATIVE PROFICIENCY, STYLISTIC NUANCE & REGISTER (C2.1 & C2.2)

### UNIT 1: Literary Registers, Archaic Lexicon & Poetics (C2.1)
- **1.1 High Literary & Archaic Vocabulary**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Quintessential, ephemeral, ubiquitous, ostentatious, sycophant, taciturn, fastidious.
  - **Set Phrases:** Her prose possesses an ephemeral, almost poetic beauty. / His fastidious attention to detail was legendary.
- **1.2 Subtleties of Idiom, Wit & Irony (C2.2)**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Sarcasm, irony, double entendre, understatement, hyperbole, satire, wit.
  - **Set Phrases:** The remark was delivered with a subtle touch of dry irony. / The article is a brilliant piece of political satire.
