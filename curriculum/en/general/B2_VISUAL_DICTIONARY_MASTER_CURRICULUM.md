## Executive Overview & Architectural System

This master specification defines the upper-intermediate curriculum for COSYlanguages B2 English (**B2.1** and **B2.2** sub-levels). At B2, learners achieve operational fluency, debating abstract issues, analyzing screenplays/literature, professional writing, and nuanced register control.

# SECTION 1: B2 ENGLISH VISUAL DICTIONARY & VOCABULARY KNOWLEDGE MAP

---

## PART 1 — SOCIETY, POLITICS & ENVIRONMENT (B2.1 & B2.2)

### UNIT 1: Global Issues, Climate & Sustainability (B2.1)
- **1.1 Climate Change & Ecology**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Greenhouse gas, global warming, carbon neutrality, fossil fuels, renewable energy, eco-friendly, conservation.
  - **Set Phrases:** Achieving carbon neutrality requires global cooperation. / We must reduce our reliance on fossil fuels.
- **1.2 Social Justice, Law & Human Rights**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Legislation, human rights, discrimination, equality, justice, court, legal system, reform.
  - **Set Phrases:** Everyone has the right to a fair trial. / Legal reforms are necessary to promote social justice.

### UNIT 2: Culture, Cinema & Literary Analysis (B2.2)
- **2.1 Film Deconstruction & Screenplay Terms**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Protagonist, antagonist, character arch, conflict, cinematography, screenplay, subtext, motif, genre.
  - **Set Phrases:** The protagonist faces an ethical dilemma in the second act. / The director uses lighting to create atmosphere.
- **2.2 Literary Criticism & Debate**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Narrative structure, metaphor, symbolism, allegory, thesis, argument, counter-argument, rebuttal.
  - **Set Phrases:** The novel serves as an allegory for political corruption. / Her argument was supported by strong evidence.

# SECTION 2: COSYlanguages COMPLETE ENGLISH GRAMMAR B2 (GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE)

---

## PART 1 — ADVANCED TENSES & CONDITIONALS (B2.1 & B2.2)
- **Mixed Conditionals:** Combining past cause with present result (*If I had taken that job, I would be living in Paris now*).
- **Future Perfect & Future Continuous:** *By next year, I will have graduated / This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Tokyo*.
- **Inversion & Emphasis:** *Rarely have I heard such inspiring words / Not only did she win, but she also set a record*.

## PART 2 — PARTICIPLE CLAUSES & ADVANCED PASSIVE (B2.1 & B2.2)
- **Participle Clauses:** Shortening clauses using `-ing` and `-ed` forms (*Having finished his homework, he went out*).
- **Advanced Passive Structures:** Passive report structures (*He is said to be... / The monument is believed to have been built...*).
