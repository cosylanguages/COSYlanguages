# PILLAR II — GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE

---

## PART 1 — ADVANCED SYNTAX & MODAL MECHANICS

### UNIT 1: Advanced Modal Nuance & Deduction
- **Modal Deduction & Speculation:** *Must have been, can't have been, might have been, bound to be, highly likely to*.
- **Modal Criticism & Expectation:** *Should have known better, ought to have submitted, was supposed to start*.

### UNIT 2: Ellipsis & Substitution Techniques
- **Ellipsis in Clauses:** Omitting repeated verb phrases or clauses (*She passed the exam, and so did her brother*).
- **Substitution via Pronouns & Auxiliaries:** *one / ones*, *do so*, *so / not* (*Will it rain? I hope not / I think so*).

### UNIT 3: Advanced Determiners & Quantifiers
- *Little vs a little*, *few vs a few*, *far fewer vs far less*, *plenty of*, *a great deal of*, *a vast amount of*.

### UNIT 4: Nominalization & Academic Register Shifts
- Transforming active verb structures into academic noun phrases:
  - Active: *We investigated the problem thoroughly.*
  - Nominalized: **A thorough investigation into the problem was conducted.**

### UNIT 5: Hedging, Nuance & Stance Devices
- *Apparently, arguably, relatively, tend to, appear to, be likely to, it is suggested that*.

### UNIT 6: Fronting & Cleft Sentences
- **Fronting for Emphasis:** *This proposal, I absolutely support.*
- **It-Clefts & Wh-Clefts:** *It was the CEO who approved the plan.* / *What we need is a clear strategy.*

### UNIT 7: Advanced Relative Clauses & Reduced Structures
- Relative pronouns: *whereby, wherein, whose, of which*.
- Reduced Relative Clauses: *Active (-ing)* (*The lawyer representing the firm...*) and *Passive (-ed)* (*The report submitted yesterday...*).

### UNIT 8: Advanced Reporting Verbs & Distancing
- *According to, it is alleged that, it is claimed that, the report suggests that, sources indicate that*.

### UNIT 9: Advanced Discourse Markers & Linking Masterclass
- *Transitioning across contrast, concession, emphasis, addition, and conclusion.*
