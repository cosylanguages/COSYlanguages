# PILLAR II — GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE

---

## PART 1 — ADVANCED HEDGING, NUANCE & MODALITY

### UNIT 1: Advanced Hedging & Academic Stance Devices
- **Devices:** *It appears that, it would seem that, it is conceivable that, arguably, presumably, tend to, appear to, be likely to, might suggest*.
- *The data would seem to indicate a gradual shift in consumer behavior.*

### UNIT 2: Modality for Speculation, Deduction & Certainty
- *Must have been, cannot have been, might conceivably be, is bound to occur, is open to question*.

---

## PART 2 — INFORMATION PACKAGING, COHESION & SENTENCE VARIETY

### UNIT 3: Theme, Rheme & Information Packaging
- **Theme/Rheme Alignment:** Placing known information in the Theme position (sentence start) and new/important information in the Rheme position (sentence end) to optimize readability.
- **Fronting & End-Weight:**
  - *Fronting:* **In no small measure** did the policy succeed.
  - *End-Weight:* Placing heavy noun phrases at the end of the sentence.

### UNIT 4: Cohesion, Ellipsis & Lexical Substitution
- Cohesive devices: *accordingly, consequently, by contrast, in light of this, subsequently*.
- Ellipsis & Substitution: *do so, such, so, not, former/latter*.

### UNIT 5: Sentence Variety (Periodic, Loose & Balanced Sentences)
- **Periodic Sentence:** Main clause delayed until the very end for dramatic effect.
  - *Having considered the financial risks, reviewed the market analysis, and consulted board members, **the CEO approved the merger**.*
- **Balanced Sentence:** Parallel structures balancing two opposing or complementary ideas.

### UNIT 6: Advanced Punctuation Mechanics
- Precision usage of semicolons (`;`), colons (`:`), em dashes (`—`), and parentheses for stylistic register control.
