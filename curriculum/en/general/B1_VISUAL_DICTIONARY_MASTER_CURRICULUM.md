## PART 1 — TENSES & ASPECT IN DEPTH (B1.1 & B1.2)

### UNIT 1: Present Perfect Continuous vs. Present Perfect Simple
- **Formula:** *Continuous:* `have/has been + V-ing` vs *Simple:* `have/has + V3`.
- *Continuous* emphasizes duration/ongoing process (*I have been studying for 3 hours*).
- *Simple* emphasizes completion/result (*I have finished 3 chapters*).

### UNIT 2: Past Perfect Simple & Past Perfect Continuous
- *Past Perfect Simple:* `had + V3` (*When we arrived, the movie had started*).
- *Past Perfect Continuous:* `had been + V-ing` (*He was tired because he had been driving all night*).

### UNIT 3: Future in the Past (Was going to / Was about to / Was due to)
- Used to describe plans or intentions that were made in the past (whether fulfilled or unfulfilled).
- *I was going to call you, but my battery died.*
- *The train was about to leave when she ran onto the platform.*
- *The meeting was due to start at 9 AM.*

---

## PART 2 — PASSIVES, CAUSATIVES & IMPERSONAL STRUCTURES

### UNIT 4: Passive Voice Across Tenses
- Present/Past/Future/Present Perfect Passive (*The car is being repaired / The decision was made / The report will be published*).

### UNIT 5: Causatives (Have / Get Something Done)
- **Formula:** `Subject + HAVE / GET + Object + Past Participle (V3)`
- Used when someone else performs a service for you.
- *I had my car repaired yesterday.* (= A mechanic fixed it).
- *She is getting her hair cut tomorrow.*

---

## PART 3 — CONDITIONALS, WISH & MODALS OF DEDUCTION

### UNIT 6: Second & Third Conditionals + Mixed Conditionals Recognition
- *Second Conditional (Unreal Present):* `IF + Past Simple, WOULD + Verb` (*If I had time, I would travel*).
- *Third Conditional (Unreal Past Regret):* `IF + Past Perfect, WOULD HAVE + V3` (*If I had studied, I would have passed*).
- *Mixed Conditional Recognition (Past Cause ➔ Present Result):* `IF + Past Perfect, WOULD + Base Verb` (*If I had taken that job, I would be in London now*).

### UNIT 7: Expressing Wishes & Regrets (Wish + Past / Would)
- `I wish + Past Simple` (Present regret): *I wish I had a car.*
- `I wish + Past Perfect` (Past regret): *I wish I hadn't said that.*
- `I wish + WOULD + Base Verb` (Annoyance about someone else's behavior): *I wish you would stop tapping your pen!*

### UNIT 8: Modals of Deduction & Speculation (Present & Past)
- Present: *must be, can't be, might be*.
- Past: `Must have + V3`, `Can't have + V3`, `Might have + V3`.

---

## PART 4 — CLAUSES, RELATIVES & CLEFT STRUCTURES

### UNIT 9: Relative Clauses (Defining, Non-defining, Whose & Reduced Relatives)
- *Defining:* Essential info (*The man who lives next door is a doctor*).
- *Non-defining:* Extra info surrounded by commas (*My brother, who lives in Paris, is an architect*).
- *Whose:* Possession (*I met a writer whose book won an award*).
- *Reduced Relative Clauses (Recognition):*
  - *Active (`V-ing`):* The girl standing by the door is my sister. (= who is standing...)
  - *Passive (`V3`):* The cars made in Japan are reliable. (= which are made...)

### UNIT 10: Basic Cleft Sentences & Structural Emphasis
- `It was + [Object/Person] + THAT / WHO...`
  - *It was John who solved the problem.*
- `What I + [Verb] + IS / WAS...`
  - *What I need right now is a good rest.*
- **Emphasis with auxiliary DO:**
  - *I do hope you can come to the party!* / *She does work hard.*

## PART 5 — DISCOURSE CONNECTORS & LINKING DEVICES MASTERCLASS

### UNIT 11: Masterclass in Discourse Connectors
- **Addition:** *Furthermore, moreover, in addition, besides, also*.
- **Contrast:** *However, although, even though, in spite of, despite, on the other hand, whereas*.
- **Cause & Reason:** *Because, since, as, due to, owing to, on account of*.

- **Purpose:** *In order to, so as to, so that*.

# SECTION 4: PRONUNCIATION, INTONATION & CONNECTED SPEECH

---

- **Sentence Stress & Rhythm:** Content words vs structure words in English stress-timed rhythm.
- **Thought Groups & Pausing:** Chunking sentences into logical tone units.
- **Weak Forms:** Pronouncing auxiliary verbs and prepositions with schwa /ə/ (*can /kən/, to /tə/, for /fə/*).
- **Connected Speech Mechanics:**
  - *Linking:* Consonant-to-vowel linking (*check it out ➔ /tʃekɪtaʊt/*).
  - *Assimilation:* /t/ + /j/ ➔ /tʃ/ (*don't you ➔ /dəʊntʃu/*).
  - *Elision:* Disappearance of /t/ or /d/ between consonants (*next day ➔ /neksdeɪ/*).
- **Intonation for Nuance:** Rising intonation for polite check-ins, falling intonation for firm statements.
