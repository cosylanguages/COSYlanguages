## PART 1 — PERSONALITY, PSYCHOLOGY & EMOTIONS (B1.1 & B1.2)

### UNIT 1: Complex Character Traits & Identity (B1.1)
- **1.1 Complex Personality Nuances**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Ambition, empathy, self-confidence, insecurity, temperament, charisma, reliability.
  - **Adjectives:** Ambitious, compassionate, insecure, charismatic, level-headed, unpredictable, conscientious, arrogant, humble.
  - **Collocations:** Highly ambitious, level-headed leader, charismatic personality, quiet confidence.
  - **Set Phrases:** She strikes me as very level-headed. / What makes him so charismatic?
- **1.2 Interpersonal Relationships & Boundaries**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Dynamic, boundary, trust, betrayal, intimacy, mutual respect, compromise.
  - **Verbs:** Establish boundaries, compromise, betray, rebuild trust, resolve differences.
  - **Set Phrases:** Relationships require mutual respect and compromise. / It takes time to rebuild trust after an argument.

### UNIT 2: Emotional Wellbeing & Mental Health (B1.2)
- **2.1 Anxiety, Burnout & Coping Mechanisms**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Anxiety, burnout, depression, resilience, coping mechanism, mindfulness, therapy.
  - **Set Phrases:** I've been feeling burnt out lately. / Mindfulness helps manage daily stress.
- **2.2 Personal Growth & Goal Setting**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Motivation, self-discipline, goal, milestone, progress, obstacle, mindset.
  - **Set Phrases:** Setting clear milestones helps maintain motivation. / Overcoming obstacles builds resilience.

## PART 2 — WORK, CAREER & BUSINESS (B1.1 & B1.2)

### UNIT 3: Employment, Interviews & Career Mobility (B1.1)
- **3.1 Job Applications & Resumes/CVs**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Cover letter, CV, portfolio, reference, background check, candidate, applicant, recruiter.
  - **Set Phrases:** Please find my updated CV attached. / I am writing to express my interest in the position.
- **3.2 Job Interviews & Salary Negotiation**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Interviewer, panel, strength, weakness, salary expectation, benefits package, probation period.
  - **Set Phrases:** What are your main strengths and weaknesses? / I am looking for a competitive salary package.

### UNIT 4: Project Management & Customer Relations (B1.2)
- **4.1 Teamwork & Project Milestones**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Collaboration, milestone, feedback, bottleneck, deadline, delegate, efficiency.
  - **Set Phrases:** We need to delegate tasks to meet the deadline. / Constructive feedback improves team performance.
- **4.2 Customer Service & Complaint Handling**
  - **Core Vocabulary:** Customer satisfaction, complaint, refund, apology, resolution, client relationship.
  - **Set Phrases:** We apologize for the inconvenience and will issue a full refund. / Our priority is customer satisfaction.

# SECTION 2: COSYlanguages COMPLETE ENGLISH GRAMMAR B1 (GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE)

---

## PART 1 — TENSES & ASPECT (B1.1 & B1.2)
- **Present Perfect Continuous:** Duration of ongoing activity (*I have been studying for three hours*).
- **Past Perfect Simple:** Action completed before another past event (*When we arrived at the cinema, the film had already started*).
- **Passive Voice Across Tenses:** Present, Past, and Future Passive forms (*The report is prepared by the manager / The decision was made yesterday*).

## PART 2 — CONDITIONALS & MODALS OF DEDUCTION (B1.1 & B1.2)
- **Second Conditional:** Hypothetical present/future (*If I had more free time, I would learn Spanish*).
- **Third Conditional Intro:** Imagined past events (*If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam*).
- **Modals of Deduction:** Present deduction (*must be, can't be, might be*) & Past deduction (*must have been, couldn't have been*).

## PART 3 — REPORTED SPEECH & INDIRECT STRUCTURES (B1.1 & B1.2)
- **Reported Statements & Questions:** Tense shifts, pronoun changes, and time word shifts (*He asked where I lived / She said she was working*).
- **Gerunds vs Infinitives:** Verb patterns with subtle meaning changes (*remember to do vs remember doing*).
