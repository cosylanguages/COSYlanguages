# SECTION 2: COSYlanguages COMPLETE ENGLISH GRAMMAR B1 (GRAMMAR KNOWLEDGE TREE)

## PART 1 — TENSES, ASPECT & PASSIVE (B1.1 & B1.2)
- **Present Perfect Continuous:** Duration of recent activity (*I have been studying for 3 hours*).
- **Past Perfect Simple:** Earlier past events (*When I arrived, the train had left*).
- **Full Passive Voice:** Present, Past, and Future Passive (*The house was built in 1990*).

## PART 2 — CONDITIONALS & MODALS OF DEDUCTION (B1.1 & B1.2)
- **Second Conditional:** Imagined present/future (*If I won the lottery, I would buy a house*).
- **Third Conditional Intro:** Imagined past (*If I had known, I would have helped*).
- **Modals of Deduction:** Present (*must be, can't be, might be*) & Past (*must have been, couldn't have been*).
