💬 4. Top 100 Essential English Phrases & Collocations (A, B, C Patterns)

100 Items
Preferences & Choice
I like A but I don't like B. ↔ I dislike A and prefer B.
"I like coffee but I don't like tea."
Desire & Need
I want A, not B. ↔ I refuse A and need B.
"I want water, not soda."
Ability & Skill
I can speak A, but I can't speak B. ↔ I am unable to speak A, but fluent in B.
"I can speak English, but I can't speak French."
Opinion & Agreement
I agree with A, but I disagree with B. ↔ I oppose A and support B.
"I agree with rule A, but disagree with B."
Existence & State
There is A in B. ↔ There is no A in B.
"There is water in the glass."
