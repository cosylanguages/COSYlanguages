🧬 Nostratic & Eurasiatic First Row

In comparative linguistics (Illich-Svitych, Dolgopolsky, Greenberg), Nostratic branches into four primary clades: Afroasiatic, Kartvelian, Dravidian, and Eurasiatic.

The Eurasiatic clade encompasses Indo-European, Uralic, and Turkic (Altaic).

🗣️ Reconstructed Pronoun Cognates

A primary argument for Eurasiatic and Nostratic unity is the conservative stability of first-person (*m*) and second-person (*t*) pronominal stems:

Language / Family 1st Person ('I') 2nd Person ('You')
Proto-Nostratic **mi* **ti*
English (IE) Me / My Thou / Thee
Finnish (Uralic) Minä / Me Sinä (*t- > s-*) / Te
Turkish (Turkic) Ben (*m- > b-*) / Biz Sen (*t- > s-*) / Siz

💡 Pedagogical Transfer Insights

Understanding macro-family connections transforms language learning. When moving from English or French to Finnish or Turkish, recognizing shared Eurasiatic underlying structures (like agglutinative case markers and SOV/SVO word order shifts) speeds up mental mapping.
