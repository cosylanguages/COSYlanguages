TenseJobExample
Past Simplethe main eventsShe opened the door.
Past Continuousthe background sceneIt was raining.
Past Perfect (Simple / Continuous)the earlier pastShe had already left. / She'd been waiting for hours.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Notice final -ed pronunciation: /t/ after voiceless sounds (e.g. worked), /d/ after voiced sounds (e.g. lived), and /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (e.g. wanted).
