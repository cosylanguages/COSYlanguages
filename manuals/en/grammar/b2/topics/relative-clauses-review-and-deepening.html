Core Rule: Advanced relative clauses can use prepositions + relative pronouns (whom / which), sentential relative clauses (which modifying a whole clause), and reduced participle clauses (verb-ing / past participle).

🗣️ Pronunciation note

In natural speech, the -ing suffix ends in a nasal velar sound /ɪŋ/. Avoid pronouncing a hard hard /g/ sound at the end.
