Core Rule: Advanced relative clauses can use prepositions + relative pronouns (whom / which), sentential relative clauses (which modifying a whole clause), and reduced participle clauses (verb-ing / past participle).

Which advanced relative clause structure matches your context?

Whole-Sentence Modifier
, which + verb...
He passed the exam, which surprised us.
Reduced Active Participle

The man sitting over there...
Reduced Passive Participle
past participle
