Present Simple vs Present Continuous

A fast A2 refresher

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can accurately distinguish between permanent habits and temporary trends or present events.
FeaturePresent SimplePresent Continuous
Useroutines, habits, facts, permanent situationsnow, temporary situations, changing trends
Signal wordsalways, usually, every daynow, at the moment, these days, currently
ExampleShe works in a bank.She's working from home this month.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌She is working in a bank for ten years.
tap to see the correct form
✅She has worked in a bank for ten years.
❌I am understanding the problem now.
tap to see the correct form
