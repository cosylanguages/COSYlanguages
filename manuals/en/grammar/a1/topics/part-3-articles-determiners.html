Articles & Determiners

Indefinite articles 'a'/'an', definite article 'the', zero article rules & master article choice flowchart.

Units 11–15: Article System

Use a before consonant sounds (a car, a university); an before vowel sounds (an apple, an hour). Use the for specific items or second mention. Zero article for general plurals and uncountable nouns.
