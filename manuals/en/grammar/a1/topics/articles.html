Articles: A / An / The

Choosing the right article

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can build clear, accurate sentences and communicate confidently in everyday situations.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe the examples below. Pay attention to word order, endings, and sentence patterns before reading the rule.

Is the noun specific and known to both speakers?
YES (Specific)
Use 'THE'
Pass me the salt.
NO (Singular Countable)
Use 'A / AN'
I need an apple.
NO (Plural / Uncountable)
Use 'SOME / Ø'
I want some water.
Example
consonant sound · AI have a dog and a cat.
vowel sound · ANShe has an apple and an egg.
specific thing · THEThe sun is hot. Close the door, please.
general / plural · no articleI like cats. Children love music.

💡 Listen, don't just look

Say the next word. Vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u)? Use an: an apple, an hour. Any other sound? Use a: a dog, a university (starts with a 'y' sound, not a vowel sound).

NEW thinga dog
SAME thing, now knownthe dog

I saw a dog. The dog was brown. · once mentioned, it becomes 'the'.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Articles barely exist in many languages · Russian has none at all. If your language doesn't have them, this repetition (a dog… the dog…) is exactly how English marks 'a new thing' vs 'the thing we both know'.

💡 Memory trick

The extra N in an exists just to stop two vowel sounds crashing into each other. Say “a apple” out loud · it's awkward! The n smooths the join: “an apple.”
