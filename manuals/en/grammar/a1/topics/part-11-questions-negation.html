👀 What do you notice?

Notice question structure: Question Word + Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb? (Where do you live?). Notice 'not' follows the auxiliary verb in negatives.

Question WordTarget InformationExample Question
WhoPerson / IdentityWho is your teacher?
WherePlace / LocationWhere do you live?
When / What timeTime / DateWhen does the train leave?
WhyReason / CauseWhy are you studying English?
What / WhichThing / ChoiceWhat is your name? Which dress?
HowManner / MeansHow do you travel to work?

🧠 Think like a native speaker

In English, main verbs (except 'to be') CANNOT make questions or negatives by themselves. They always recruit an auxiliary helper (do, does, did, is, are, can).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Wh- questions end with FALLING intonation (⤵ Where do you live?). Yes/No questions end with RISING intonation (⤴ Do you like tea?).

Key examples
  • Where did you go yesterday?
  • What time does the supermarket open?
  • I don't understand this word.
