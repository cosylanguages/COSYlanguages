Connecting Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, and Going To Future

👀 What do you notice?

Compare time frames: 'I work' (routine), 'I am working' (now), 'I worked' (yesterday), 'I am going to work' (tomorrow).

TenseTime FocusFormulaTime Markers
Present SimpleRoutines / FactsSubject + V1 (-s)every day, usually
Present ContinuousAction Right NowSubject + am/is/are + V-ingnow, at the moment
Past SimpleFinished PastSubject + V2 (-ed)yesterday, last night
Going To FutureFuture PlanSubject + am/is/are going to + V1tomorrow, next week

🧠 Think like a native speaker

English relies heavily on time markers to lock down time context. Choosing the right auxiliary (do, be, have) determines the tense logic.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Notice how auxiliary verbs shift stress depending on tense: 'I WAS working' vs 'I AM working' vs 'I WILL work'.

Key examples
  • Every day I walk to work, but today I am taking the bus.
  • Yesterday we visited the museum.
  • Next weekend we are going to relax at the beach.

💡 Memory trick

PRESENT SIMPLE = Habits. CONTINUOUS = Right now. PAST = Finished then. GOING TO = Future plan!
