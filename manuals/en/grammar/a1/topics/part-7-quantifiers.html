Quantifiers

Some vs any, much vs many, a lot of, a few vs a little, each, every & all.

Focus on word stress and sentence rhythm: content words (nouns, main verbs) carry key stress, while structural items remain unstressed.

Unit 24: Quantifiers & Determiners

Some in affirmative sentences and polite offers/requests; Any in negative sentences and general questions. Much + uncountable; Many + countable plural.
