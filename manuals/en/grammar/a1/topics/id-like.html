I'd Like / Would You Like…?

Polite requests & offers

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can build clear, accurate sentences and communicate confidently in everyday situations.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe the examples below. Pay attention to word order, endings, and sentence patterns before reading the rule.

★ Why this matters

This is the phrase you'll use in every café, shop, and restaurant · it's often the very first 'polite' structure learners get right.

SUBJECTI
=
MODALwould
FunctionExample
Polite requestI'd like a coffee, please. (= I want, but more polite)
OfferWould you like some tea?
Positive answerYes, please. I'd love some.
Negative answerNo, thank you. I'm fine.

I'd = I would. Use I'd like (not I want) to sound polite in shops, cafés and restaurants.

⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I like a coffee, please.
tap to see the correct form
✅I'd like a coffee, please.
❌Do you like some tea?
tap to see the correct form
✅Would you like some tea?
(as an offer)
More examples
  • I'd like a sandwich, please.
  • Would you like to come to my house?
  • I'd like two tickets, please.
