Nouns & Possession

Common/proper nouns, singular vs plural rules, irregular plurals, countable/uncountable nouns & possessive 's.

Units 4–10: Nouns & Possessives

Regular plurals (-s, -es, -ies, -ves) and irregular plurals (man/men, child/children). Uncountable container phrases (a glass of water, a loaf of bread).

Possessive 's Rules:
  • Singular: John's car, my mother's house.
  • Regular Plural: my parents' house, the students' books.
  • Irregular Plural: the children's toys, men's clothes.
