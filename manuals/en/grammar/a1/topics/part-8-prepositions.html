Prepositions of Place & Time

In, on, at for locations, dates, times, and movement

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can pinpoint locations, schedule appointments, and give directions using in, on, at, and movement prepositions.

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Answer these quick questions to verify meaning:

1. Sentence: "Exact times, holidays (at 3 PM, at night)" — Does the preposition show spatial position or relationship?
Prepositions link nouns to express location, direction, or time.
2. Sentence: "Exact times, holidays (at 3 PM, at night)" — Is 'on' used because the book is touching a surface?
'On' indicates contact with a surface.

👀 What do you notice?

Notice time pyramid: AT specific times (at 5 PM), ON days/dates (on Monday), IN long periods (in July, in 2026).

PrepositionTime UsagePlace / Location Usage
ATExact times, holidays (at 3 PM, at night)Specific points (at the door, at the bus stop)
ONDays, dates (on Monday, on May 5th)Surfaces, floors (on the desk, on the 2nd floor)
INMonths, years, seasons (in July, in 2026)Enclosed spaces, cities, countries (in London, in a room)

🧠 Think like a native speaker

Think of IN/ON/AT as a pyramid of general to specific. IN = big general areas/periods. ON = medium surfaces/days. AT = specific targets/times!

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Prepositions are weak function words in speech: 'at' /ət/, 'in' /ɪn/, 'on' /ɒn/. They blend directly into the noun phrase that follows.

Key examples
  • We will meet at 8:00 on Friday.
  • She is studying in her room at the university.
  • There is a picture on the wall.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌❌ I was born in 15th May.

✅✅ I was born on 15th May.
❌❌ She lives at France.

💡 Memory trick

AT specific points & times. ON days & surfaces. IN enclosed places & long time spaces!
