General past ability, possibility, and polite asking

FunctionStructureExample
Past Abilitysubject + could + verbWhen I was five, I could swim.
Polite RequestCould you + verb...?Could you pass the salt, please?
Possibilitysubject + could + verbWe could go to the cinema tonight if you like.

Rule: Could is the past form of can for general abilities (I could swim). In present requests, Could you...? is more polite than Can you...?

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Notice final -ed pronunciation: /t/ after voiceless sounds (e.g. worked), /d/ after voiced sounds (e.g. lived), and /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (e.g. wanted).

Examples
  • My grandfather could speak four languages.
  • Could you open the window, please?
