Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Past Perfect

The earlier of two past actions

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can clarify the exact order of events when two things happened in the past.
AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I had finished before she arrived.I hadn't finished before she arrived.Had you finished before she arrived?

Use: the earlier of two past actions — it happened before another past action or time. Structure: had + past participle.

👀 Notice: Which action came first?

Two past actions, one before the other: the earlier one is past perfect (had + past participle), the later one is past simple. The train had left when we arrived. (leaving happened first)

More examples
  • When I arrived, the film had already started.
  • She had never seen snow before that trip.
