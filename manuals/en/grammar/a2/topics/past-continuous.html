Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Past Continuous

Actions in progress at a specific moment in the past

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe background scenes and ongoing activities at exact past moments.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Answer these quick questions to verify the meaning:

Sentence: 'I was sleeping when you called.'

Q1: Was I in the middle of sleeping when the phone rang? (Yes)

Sentence: 'They were driving home at 8 PM.'

Q2: Was the drive finished before 8 PM, or in progress at 8 PM? (In progress at 8 PM)

🎯 Practice this →
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / He / She / ItHe was sleeping.He wasn't sleeping.Was he sleeping?
You / We / TheyThey were playing.They weren't playing.Were they playing?

Form: was/were + verb -ing. Use it for an action that was already in progress at a specific time in the past (e.g., At 8 PM yesterday, I was studying).

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Notice final -ed pronunciation: /t/ after voiceless sounds (e.g. worked), /d/ after voiced sounds (e.g. lived), and /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (e.g. wanted).

Examples
  • What were you doing at midnight? I was watching a film.
  • It was raining heavily when we woke up.
