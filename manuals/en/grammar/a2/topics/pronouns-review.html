Pronouns Review: Subject, Object, Possessive

Mastering personal, object, possessive adjectives and pronouns

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use all forms of personal pronouns, possessive adjectives, and possessive pronouns without confusion.

we / theyus / themour / their (car)ours / theirs

Rule: Subject pronouns do the action (Subject + Verb). Object pronouns receive the action (Verb + Object). Possessive adjectives need a noun (my book). Possessive pronouns stand alone (The book is mine).

Examples
  • She called him yesterday, but he didn't answer her.
  • Is this pen yours or mine? It's my pen, so it's mine.
