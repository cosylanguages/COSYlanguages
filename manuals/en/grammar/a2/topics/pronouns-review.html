Pronouns Review

Subject, object, possessive adjective & pronoun

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can correctly use subject, object, and possessive pronouns without confusion.

👀 Notice: Four jobs, one family of words

Subject does the action (She calls him). Object receives it (She calls him). Possessive adjective sits before a noun (her phone). Possessive pronoun stands alone (It's hers).

More examples
  • He gave her his number.
  • Is this bag yours or theirs?
  • We invited them to our house.
