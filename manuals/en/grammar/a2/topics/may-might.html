Expressing present and future possibility

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express uncertainty and talk about possible future outcomes.
ModalMeaningExample
might / mayPossibility (maybe yes, maybe no)It might rain later. Take an umbrella.
might not / may notPossible negative outcomeShe might not come to the party tonight.

Rule: Use may or might + base verb infinitive to express 30–50% possibility. Might is extremely common in spoken English.

Examples
  • We might go to Italy this summer, but we haven't decided yet.
  • Be careful, the floor may be slippery.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌It might to snow tomorrow.
tap to see the correct form
✅It might snow tomorrow.
