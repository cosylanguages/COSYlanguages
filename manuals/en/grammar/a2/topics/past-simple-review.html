Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Past Simple (Review)

A finished action at a finished time

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can talk clearly about completed past events, trips, and historical facts.
AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I worked / went yesterday.I didn't work / didn't go yesterday.Did you work / go yesterday?

👀 Notice: A finished moment in the past

The past simple needs a finished time in the past, even if you don't say it out loud: yesterday, last week, in 2020, two days ago, when I was young.

Common time expressions
  • yesterday
  • last week
  • two days ago
  • in 2020
More examples
  • She visited Rome last summer.
  • We moved here three years ago.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I saw her yesterday at 3 o'clock in the past.
