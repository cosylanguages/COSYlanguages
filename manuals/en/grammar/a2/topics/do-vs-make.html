Do vs Make

Mastering high-frequency collocations with do and make

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can correctly pair activities, tasks, creations, and decisions with 'do' or 'make'.

VerbGeneral MeaningCommon Collocations
DOTasks, jobs, general unstated activitiesdo homework, do housework, do research, do a favor, do your best
MAKECreating, producing, decisions, speakingmake coffee, make a mistake, make a decision, make money, make a noise

Rule: DO = actions, obligations, routines. MAKE = creating something new or producing an effect/result.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Focus on word stress and sentence rhythm: content words (nouns, main verbs) carry key stress, while structural items remain unstressed.

Examples
  • I need to do my homework before I make dinner.
  • Don't worry if you make a mistake.
