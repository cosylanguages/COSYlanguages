Part 4 · Modals & Verb Patterns

Have To, Must, Mustn't, Don't Have To

Obligation, prohibition, and lack of necessity

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express mandatory rules, strict prohibitions, and options where something is not necessary.
ModalMeaningExample
have to / mustObligation (necessary)You have to wear a seatbelt. I must call my doctor.
mustn'tProhibition (forbidden / don't do it!)You mustn't smoke here.
don't have toNo obligation (optional)You don't have to come if you are busy.

Crucial Distinction: Mustn't = 100% forbidden (Do NOT do it!). Don't have to = unnecessary (You can do it if you want, but it's not required).

Examples
  • In football, you mustn't touch the ball with your hands.
  • It's Sunday, so I don't have to get up early.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌You mustn't pay, it's free!
tap to see the correct form
✅You don't have to pay, it's free!
