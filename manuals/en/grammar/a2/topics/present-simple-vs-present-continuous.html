Present Simple vs Present Continuous

A fast A1 refresher

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can distinguish between habits/routines and actions happening right now.

New to this book? This page is a fast refresher of two tenses from A0-A1 level. If you already know them well, skip straight to Part 1 · Talking About the Past.

FeaturePresent SimplePresent Continuous
Formbase (+s for he/she/it)am/is/are + verbing
Useroutines, habits, factsactions happening right now, temporary situations
Signal wordsalways, usually, every daynow, at the moment, today, Look!
ExampleShe works in a bank.She's working late today.

👀 Notice: Routine, or happening now?

Ask: is this a repeated routine (present simple), or is it happening right now, at this exact moment (present continuous)? I read the news every day. vs. I'm reading the news right now.
