Present Continuous for Future Arrangements

Fixed plans with a specific time and place

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss confirmed social plans, doctor appointments, and travel arrangements.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.

Sentence: 'She is cooking dinner now.'

Sentence: 'He is staying with us this week.'

🎯 Practice this →

Rule: Use Present Continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing) for future arrangements that have been organized with another person, booked, or confirmed on a calendar.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

In natural speech, the -ing suffix ends in a nasal velar sound /ɪŋ/. Avoid pronouncing a hard hard /g/ sound at the end.

Examples
  • I am meeting Sarah at 3 PM tomorrow.
  • We are flying to Rome on Friday morning.
