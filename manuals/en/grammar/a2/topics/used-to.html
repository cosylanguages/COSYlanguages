Part 1 · Talking About the Past

Used To: Past Habits & Past States

Things that were true in the past but are not true now

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss long-term past habits, routines, and states that have since changed.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →
FormStructureExample
Affirmativeused to + verbI used to live in Madrid.
Negativedidn't use to + verbShe didn't use to like spinach.
QuestionDid ... use to + verb?Did you use to play the piano?

Key Rule: used to + infinitive is ONLY for past habits and states that are NO LONGER true today. For negative forms and questions, drop the '-d': didn't use to, Did you use to...?

Examples
  • I used to have long hair when I was a teenager.
  • We didn't use to travel much, but now we travel every year.
