Future Time Clauses: When, As Soon As, Before, After, Until

Present simple after time conjunctions referring to the future

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can link future actions chronologically using time connectors without repeating 'will'.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →
Time ConjunctionRuleExample
when / as soon as / before / after / untilUse Present Simple in the time clause (after the conjunction)I will call you when I arrive.
Main ClauseUse Will / Going to in the main clauseAs soon as the meeting finishes, we will leave.

Golden Rule: Never use will immediately after time conjunctions (when, as soon as, before, after, until). Use the Present Simple instead!

Examples
  • I'll wait here until you come back.
  • Before she leaves tomorrow, she will sign the contract.
⚠️ Common mistakes
