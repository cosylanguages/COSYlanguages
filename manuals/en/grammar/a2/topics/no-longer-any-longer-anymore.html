No Longer, Any Longer, Anymore

Expressing actions and states that have ceased

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can clearly express that a past situation or habit has stopped occurring.
Word / PhraseSentence TypePositionExample
no longerAffirmative verbBefore main verb / after 'be'He no longer works here.
any longer / anymoreNegative verbAt the end of the sentenceHe doesn't work here anymore.

Rule: Use no longer with affirmative verbs (She no longer lives here). Use anymore or any longer with negative verbs (She doesn't live here anymore). Do NOT double-negative!

Examples
  • I no longer drink coffee in the evening.
  • They don't speak to each other any longer.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌He doesn't work here no longer.
tap to see the correct form
✅He doesn't work here anymore.
