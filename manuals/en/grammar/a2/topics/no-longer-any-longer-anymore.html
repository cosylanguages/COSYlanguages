Part 1 · Talking About the Past

No Longer, Any Longer, Anymore

Saying something has stopped

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can explain that a past action or state has ceased.
PatternStyleExample
no longer + affirmative verbneutral / a little formalI no longer live there.
negative verb + any longerneutral, everydayI don't live there any longer.
negative verb + anymoreneutral, everyday (very common)I don't live there anymore.

All three mean the same thing: something was true before, but isn't true now. No longer goes straight before the verb and needs no extra negative; any longer and anymore go at the end of a negative sentence.

More examples
  • She no longer works here.
  • We don't talk anymore.
  • He doesn't smoke any longer — he stopped last year.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I no longer don't smoke.
tap to see the correct form
✅I no longer smoke.
❌She doesn't no longer live here.
tap to see the correct form
✅She no longer lives here. / She doesn't live here anymore.
