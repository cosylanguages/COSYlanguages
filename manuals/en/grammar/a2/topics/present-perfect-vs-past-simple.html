Part 2 · Present Perfect

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Unfinished time & experience vs finished past time

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can smoothly alternate between introducing life experiences and specifying exact past details.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →
FeaturePresent PerfectPast Simple
Time FrameUnfinished time / no specific time mentionedFinished time (yesterday, in 2020, ago)
FocusResult now or life experienceFinished event in the past
ExampleI have visited Paris. (in my life)I visited Paris in 2019. (finished year)

Gold Standard Conversation Pattern: Start with Present Perfect to introduce an experience (Have you ever seen a bear?), then switch to Past Simple to ask for specific details (Where did you see it?).

Examples
  • I have broken my leg! (result now: it's in a cast).
  • I broke my leg last winter while skiing.
