Mastering Second Conditional

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can accurately apply Second Conditional in spoken and written English.

Core Rule: Use the Second Conditional (If + Past Simple, WOULD + verb) for imaginary, unreal, or hypothetical situations in the present or future.

Is the situation hypothetical or unlikely now/future?
If-clause (Unreal Condition)
If + Past Simple
If I won the lottery...
Result-clause (Hypothetical Result)
would + verb
...I would travel the world.
