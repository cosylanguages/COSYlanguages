Describing Symptoms & Asking for Help

Explaining a physical problem to a doctor or asking emergency help.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can explain physical symptoms to healthcare staff and request help in urgent situations.

🧩 Building Blocks: Medical Dialogue Chips

I've got a terrible stomach ache. It hurts when I press here. How long have you felt like this? Could you help me, please?

🗺️ Doctor's Consultation Flow

1
Symptom Description: "Doctor, I've had a bad cough and fever since Tuesday."
2
Clarifying Duration: "Does it hurt when you breathe deeply?" — "Yes, a little."
3
Advice / Prescription: "Take these pills twice a day after meals."
More Examples
  • I feel dizzy and weak.
  • Where is the nearest pharmacy open on Sunday?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌My arm hurts me since 3 days.
tap to see the correct form
✅My arm has hurt for three days.
