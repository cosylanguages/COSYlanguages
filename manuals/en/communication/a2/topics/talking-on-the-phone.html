A2 Everyday Spoken Interaction

Talking on the Phone

Starting/holding/ending a phone call, and leaving simple messages.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can open telephone calls, hold line connection, and pass on short messages.

🧩 Building Blocks: Phone Dialogue Chips

Hello, this is [Name] speaking. Could I speak to [Name], please? Hold on a moment, please. Can I take a message?

🗺️ Phone Conversation Flow

1
Greeting & Identity: "Hello, this is David speaking. Could I speak to Anna?"
2
Status: "I'm afraid she's away from her desk right now. Can I take a message?"
3
Message & Close: "Could you ask her to call me back?" — "Will do! Bye."
More Examples
  • Thanks for calling. Speak to you soon!
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat your number?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I am David on the phone. (Use 'This is David speaking'!)
tap to see the correct form
✅Hello, this is David speaking.
