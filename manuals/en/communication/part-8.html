Communication Question: What do I say when I miss a word, don't understand, or need a speaker to slow down?
System 8: Clarifying & Repairing (Conversational Maintenance)

Conversational repair keeps speech flowing smoothly when you miss a word, need a slower pace, or want to double-check understanding.

Why this matters: Pretending to understand or panicking when speech is too fast breaks down communication. Targeted repair phrases allow you to stay in control and maintain confidence.
🧩 System 8 Building Blocks
Could you say that again a bit slower, please? Sorry, I didn't quite catch that last word. What does 'x' mean in this context? So if I understand correctly, you mean... Let me double-check...
🗺️ Clarification & Repair Map
1. Spot Issue Missed word, fast speech, or unclear meaning
2. Target Repair Ask targeted repair ("Didn't catch that last word...")
3. Confirm Confirm meaning ("So you mean...")

👀 Notice

Notice how saying "Sorry, I didn't catch that last word" sounds much more natural and specific than just repeating "What?" or "Repeat please".

🧠 Think in conversation

Asking for clarification isn't a sign of weakness; native speakers do it constantly in noise, cross-talk, or complex discussions.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Textbook-Stiff (Blunt): "What? Repeat sentence."
✅ Natural Spoken (Specific): "Sorry, could you say that again a bit slower?"

💡 Memory Trick

Think of repair phrases like windshield wipers: a quick wipe clears the view so you can keep driving together safely!

💬 Mini Dialogue (Targeted Repair in Action)

Person A: We need to finalize the venue for the symposium by Tuesday.
Person B: Sorry, I didn't quite catch that last word. What does 'symposium' mean?
Person A: In other words, the big conference!
Person B: So if I understand correctly, we need to choose the hotel room by Tuesday?
