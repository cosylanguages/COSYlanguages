Asking for Clarification

Seeking detailed explanations and checking understanding without causing confusion.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can request further explanation or elaboration politely when instructions or ideas are unclear.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Could you elaborate on what you mean by...? If I understand correctly, you are saying that... Could you clarify the distinction between X and Y? Am I right in thinking that...?

🗺️ Asking for Clarification Dialogue Flow

1
Request Elaboration: "Could you elaborate on what you mean by 'streamlining the process'?"
2
Paraphrase Meaning: "If I understand correctly, you are saying that we should automate testing first."
3
Confirm Accuracy: "Exactly. Am I right in thinking that this will reduce manual errors?"
More Authentic Examples
  • Could you clarify the distinction between the standard and premium tiers?
  • I didn't quite catch that last point; could you explain it once more?
