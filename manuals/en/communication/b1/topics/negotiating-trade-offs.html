Negotiating Trade-Offs

Balancing concessions, finding compromises, and agreeing conditional terms.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can offer trade-offs and reach mutually beneficial conditional agreements in negotiations.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

If you can meet us halfway on X, we could offer Y... We would be willing to compromise provided that... On the condition that you extend the deadline, we can... What if we agree to X in exchange for Y?

🗺️ Negotiating Trade-Offs Dialogue Flow

1
Propose Trade-Off: "If you can meet us halfway on the pricing, we could offer extended support."
2
Counter-Condition: "We would be willing to accept those terms provided that delivery is guaranteed by Friday."
3
Closing Deal: "That sounds like a fair compromise. Let's draft the agreement."
More Authentic Examples
  • On the condition that payment is upfront, we can offer a 5% discount.
  • What if we adjust the scope slightly in exchange for a lower overall cost?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Take it or leave it, no changes.
tap to see the conditional negotiation frame
