Giving Constructive Feedback

Balancing positive praise with actionable areas for growth.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can deliver balanced performance feedback that motivates and guides improvement.

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

What worked really well was X; one area for growth is Y. I really appreciated your effort on..., and next time... To build on your success, you might want to consider... One suggestion for future projects would be to...

🗺️ Giving Constructive Feedback Dialogue Flow

1
Praise Strengths: "What worked really well was your thorough research on the market analysis."
2
Identify Growth Area: "One area for growth is presentation timing; we went slightly over schedule."
3
Actionable Advice: "To build on your success, you might want to rehearse with a timer next time."
More Authentic Examples
  • I really appreciated your proactive communication throughout the sprint.
  • One suggestion for future reports would be including a brief executive summary at the start.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Your presentation was bad and too long.
tap to see the balanced feedback sandwich
