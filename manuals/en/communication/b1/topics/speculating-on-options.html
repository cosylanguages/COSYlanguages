Speculating on Options

Exploring possibilities, hypothetical scenarios, and potential choices.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can speculate on potential options and evaluate hypothetical scenarios during discussions.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

One possibility might be to... Assuming that X happens, we could potentially... It's conceivable that we might need to... What if we were to explore...?

🗺️ Speculating on Options Dialogue Flow

1
Propose Possibility: "One possibility might be to partner with a local distribution agency."
2
Hypothetical Outcome: "Assuming that market demand increases, we could potentially double our orders."
3
Evaluate Scenario: "It's conceivable that initial costs might be higher, but long-term gains look promising."
More Authentic Examples
  • What if we were to explore alternative suppliers in Europe?
  • There's a strong chance that regulations might change next year.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌We will do X 100% no doubt.
