PET/FCE Discussion Frame

Discussing Advantages & Disadvantages

Weighing both sides of an issue before forming a balanced conclusion.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can present balanced two-sided discussions weighing benefits against drawbacks.

🧩 Building Blocks: Balance Chips

One major advantage is... On the flip side, a drawback is... Weighing both sides,... All things considered,...

🗺️ Advantage / Disadvantage Discussion Map

1
Introduce Pros: "Living in a large city has clear benefits. One major advantage is public transport access."
2
Introduce Cons: "On the flip side, a significant drawback is high rent costs and pollution."
3
Balanced Conclusion: "All things considered, I think the cultural opportunities outweigh the cost."
More Examples
  • A positive aspect of online learning is flexibility.
  • However, the downside is lack of direct social interaction.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌On the other hand... (Without first saying 'On the one hand' or setting up the contrast!)
tap to see the correct form
✅On the one hand X, on the other hand Y. / Alternatively: On the flip side...
