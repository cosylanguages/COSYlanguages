FCE Topic: Philosophy & Ethics

Philosophy, Ethics & Moral Logic

Utilitarianism vs deontology, moral dilemmas, free will, epistemology, and ethical decision frameworks.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can debate philosophical frameworks (*utilitarianism, deontology, moral dilemma*) and ethical logic.
DomainVocabularyExample
Philosophical Logicutilitarianism, deontology, moral dilemma, free willEngineers face a moral dilemma in autonomous driving design.
Principlesepistemology, ethical framework, fundamental principlesUtilitarianism seeks the greatest good for the greatest number.
Verbsdeliberate, justify, uphold, deduce, ponderThe ethics committee deliberated for several hours.
More Examples
  • Deontology emphasizes duty over consequences.
  • He spent years pondering the nature of free will and human agency.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌They justified for their decision morally.
tap to see the correct form
✅They justified their decision morally. (No 'for' after transitive verb justify!)
