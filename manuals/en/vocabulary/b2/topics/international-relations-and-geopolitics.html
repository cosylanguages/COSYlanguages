FCE Topic: International Relations

International Relations & Geopolitics

Diplomatic immunity, ratifying treaties, multilateral agreements, economic sanctions, and peacekeeping missions.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can analyze international diplomacy (*multilateral treaty, diplomatic immunity, economic sanctions*).
DomainVocabularyExample
Diplomacy & Treatiesdiplomacy, multilateral treaty, diplomatic immunity, ambassadorBoth delegates met to sign a multilateral treaty.
Global Securityinternational sanctions, peacekeeping, summit, bilateralThe UN Security Council voted to impose economic sanctions.
Verbsnegotiate, ratify, impose, mediate, executeAll parliament members voted to ratify the treaty.
More Examples
  • An international peacekeeping mission was dispatched to monitor the ceasefire.
  • The prime minister attended the international climate summit.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌They imposed economic sanctions for the country.
tap to see the correct form
✅They imposed economic sanctions on / against the country.
