FCE Topic: Arts & Heritage

Culture, Arts & Heritage

Fine arts, architectural landmarks, symphonic orchestration, sculpture, aesthetics, and heritage sites.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can critique architectural design (*landmark, aesthetic*), fine arts exhibitions, and cultural heritage sites.
DomainVocabularyExample
Fine Arts & Designsculpture, choreography, aesthetics, curatorial designThe architect focused on clean lines and modern aesthetics.
Heritage & Performancearchitectural landmark, cultural heritage site, symphonyThe ancient temple was designated a UNESCO heritage site.
Verbsexhibit, compose, sculpt, choreograph, preserveThe orchestra performed a newly composed symphony.
More Examples
  • Efforts were made to preserve the city's architectural landmarks.
  • The museum features an outstanding collection of contemporary sculptures.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌The monument is an aesthetic landmark structure.
tap to see the correct form
✅The monument is an architectural landmark.
