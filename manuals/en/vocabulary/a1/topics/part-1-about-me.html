  • First name, last name, full name, spell your name, say hello, wave goodbye.
  • Be, call, spell, say, meet, introduce, greet, wave.

Unit 3: The Human Body & Physical Appearance

3.1 Head, Facial Features & Body Parts (15 terms)

head noun

The top part of your body that has your eyes, mouth, and brain.

"I have a hat on my head."

hair noun

The mass of things like threads that grows on your head.

"He has short brown hair."

eye noun

The part of your body that you use for seeing.

"Close your eyes."

nose noun

The part of the face that you use for smelling.

"He has a small nose."

mouth noun

The part of the face that you use for eating and speaking.

"Open your mouth."

shoulder noun

The part of the body where the arm joins the trunk.

"She carried the bag on her shoulder."

arm noun

The part of the body from the shoulder to the hand.

"She broke her arm."

hand noun

The part of your arm that has fingers.

"Wash your hands before dinner."

finger noun

One of the five long parts at the end of your hand.

"He pointed his finger at the map."

leg noun

The long part of the body that you use for walking.

"I have two legs."

knee noun

The part in the middle of your leg where it bends.

"He hurt his knee playing football."

foot noun

The part of the body at the bottom of the leg.

"I have a pain in my foot."

toe noun

One of the five parts at the end of your foot.

"She stubbed her toe on the table."

heart noun

The organ in your chest that sends blood around your body.

"My heart is beating fast."

stomach noun

The part of your body where food goes after you eat it.

"I have a stomach ache."
