Unit 7: Food Categories & Pantry

7.1 Fruits, Berries & Vegetables (11 terms)

apple noun

A round fruit that can be red or green.

"She eats an apple."

banana noun

A long yellow fruit.

"Monkeys like bananas."

orange adjective

Having the colour of an orange fruit.

"He bought an orange jacket."

lemon noun

A yellow fruit that tastes sour.

"I like tea with lemon."

strawberry noun

A small red fruit.

"I love strawberry ice cream."

"This is a key example sentence for raspberry."

blueberry noun

Essential A1 vocabulary term for blueberry.

"This is a key example sentence for blueberry."

potato noun

A round white vegetable with a brown skin that grows underground.

"I love roast potatoes."

tomato noun

A soft red fruit with a lot of seeds, eaten as a vegetable.

"Put some tomatoes in the salad."

onion noun

A round vegetable with a strong smell and taste.

"Chop the onion finely."

garlic noun

A vegetable with a very strong smell and taste.

"Use garlic in the pasta sauce."

"I eat lunch at one o'clock."

"Dinner is at eight o'clock."

"I like healthy snacks like fruit."
