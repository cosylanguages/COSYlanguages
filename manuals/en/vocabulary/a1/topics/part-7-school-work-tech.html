Unit 13: Education, Work & Digital Devices

13.1 Classroom Objects & School Subjects (16 terms)

desk noun

A type of table that you sit at to write or work.

"The pen is on the desk."

chair noun

A piece of furniture for one person to sit on.

"Please sit on the chair."

whiteboard noun

A large smooth white board for writing on with dry-erase markers.

"The teacher wrote today's vocabulary on the whiteboard."

textbook noun

A book that contains information about a subject that people study.

"Open your English textbooks to page ten."

notebook noun

A book with empty pages that you write in.

"I write my lessons in a notebook."

pencil noun

A long thin object used for writing or drawing.

"Write your name with a pencil."

pen noun

An object used for writing with ink.

"Can I borrow your pen?"

backpack noun

A bag that you carry on your back.

"I put my books in my backpack."

maths noun

The study of numbers, shapes, and quantities.

"Maths is my favorite subject."

science noun

The study of the natural world based on facts learned through experiments.

"We have science class on Tuesdays."

English adjective

From or connected with England.

"I am learning English."

history noun

The study of past events.

"I like learning about history."

geography noun

The study of the systems and processes that involve the Earth's surfaces.

"We use maps in geography class."

art noun

The creation of works such as paintings or sculptures.

"I love modern art."

music noun

The sound of people singing or playing instruments.

"I like listening to music."

pe noun

Physical education classes taught in schools.

"We played volleyball during PE class this morning."

13.2 Professions & Workplace (14 terms)

teacher noun

A person who helps you learn new things.

"The teacher is in the classroom."

doctor noun

A person who helps people when they are sick.

"The doctor is at the hospital."

nurse noun

A person whose job is to care for people who are ill or injured, especially in a hospital.

"The nurse gave him some medicine."

engineer noun

A person whose job involves designing and building machines, systems, or structures.

"He is a software engineer."

manager noun

A person who is in charge of a team or business.

"My manager works very long hours."

programmer noun

A person who writes code to create software and computer programs.

"An experienced programmer built our company website."

designer noun

A person who plans the look or structure of objects, clothing, or graphics.

"The graphic designer created a stylish new logo."

lawyer noun

A qualified professional who advises clients on legal matters.

"Our lawyer reviewed the lease contract carefully."

office noun

A room or building where people work.

"He is in the office."

laptop noun

A small computer that you can carry.

"I use my laptop for work."

printer noun

A machine used for printing documents from a computer.

"We need more paper for the printer."

meeting noun

A time when people come together to talk about something.

"We have a meeting every Monday."

email verb

To send a message using the internet.

"Email me the details."

salary noun

A fixed regular payment made by an employer to an employee.

"Employees receive their monthly salary on the last Friday."

13.3 Digital Devices & Software (13 terms)

smartphone noun

A mobile phone that can also connect to the internet.

"I use my smartphone every day."

tablet noun

A portable touch-screen computer.

"She reads digital textbooks on her tablet."

laptop noun

A small computer that you can carry.

"I use my laptop for work."

computer noun

An electronic machine that can store and find information.

"I use a computer at work."

screen noun

The flat part of a television or computer where you see the pictures.

"Look at the computer screen."

keyboard noun

A set of keys on a computer or piano.

"Type your name on the keyboard."

mouse noun

A small hand-held device used to control the cursor on a computer screen.

"Click the link using the computer mouse."

headphones noun

A pair of small speakers worn over or inside the ears.

"He listened to music through his wireless headphones."

charger noun

A device used to put electricity into a battery.

"Can I borrow your phone charger?"

website noun

A set of pages on the internet with information about a subject.

"This is a useful website for learning English."

app noun

A software program designed for mobile devices or computers.

"Download the transit app to view live bus schedules."

password noun

A secret string of characters used to log into an account.

"Never share your secret account password with anyone."

video call noun

A telephone call transmiting real-time video and audio.

"We scheduled a video call with our overseas colleagues."
