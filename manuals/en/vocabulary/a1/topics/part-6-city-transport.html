Unit 11: City Infrastructure & Places

11.1 Shops & Public Services (11 terms)

supermarket noun

A large shop selling food and other household goods.

"I go to the supermarket on Saturdays."

bakery noun

A shop where bread, cakes, and pastries are made or sold.

"I buy fresh croissants at the bakery every morning."

pharmacy noun

A shop where medicines are prepared and sold.

"She went to the pharmacy to buy aspirin."

bookshop noun

A shop where you can buy books.

"I bought a dictionary at the bookshop."

bank noun

A place where people keep money and do financial transactions.

"The bank is near my office."

post office noun

A public building where stamps are sold and letters are mailed.

"I am going to the post office to send a parcel."

police station noun

The local office of a police force in a town or city.

"Report the missing passport at the central police station."

hospital noun

A place where sick or injured people receive medical care.

"He went to the hospital by ambulance."

library noun

A place where you can read or borrow books.

"I go to the library to study."

school noun

A place where children go to learn.

"I go to school by bus."

town hall noun

A public building used for local government administration.

"The mayor gave a speech at the town hall."

11.2 Entertainment & Leisure Places (12 terms)

park noun

A public garden where people go to walk and relax.

"Let's walk in the park."

square noun

An open public area in a city or town, usually surrounded by buildings.

"We met in the town square near the fountain."

cinema noun

A place where you go to watch films.

"Let's go to the cinema tonight."

theatre noun

A building with a stage where people go to watch plays.

"We are going to the theatre tonight."

museum noun

A building where you can look at important objects.

"The museum is very interesting."

art gallery noun

A building or room where paintings and sculptures are exhibited.

"We visited an impressive art gallery on Saturday."

restaurant noun

A place where you pay to eat meals.

"We go to a restaurant on Fridays."

cafe noun

A place where you can buy drinks and simple meals.

"Let's meet at the cafe."

gym noun

A room or building with equipment for doing physical exercise.

"I go to the gym three times a week."

swimming pool noun

An artificial basin filled with water for swimming.

"She swims twenty laps in the indoor swimming pool."

stadium noun

A large sports ground surrounded by tiers of seats for spectators.

"Thousands of fans gathered at the football stadium."

zoo noun

A place where many kinds of wild animals are kept so that people can look at them.

"Let's go to the zoo."

Unit 12: Transport & Travel

12.1 Means of Transport (11 terms)

car noun

A vehicle with four wheels and an engine.

"His car is red."

bus noun

A large vehicle that carries many people.

"I take the bus to work."

train noun

A vehicle that runs on rails.

"The train is fast."

metro noun

An underground railway system in a major city.

"Riding the metro is the fastest way across town."

subway noun

An underground urban railway network.

"Take the subway to reach the museum quickly."

tram noun

A passenger vehicle powered by electricity that runs on rails along city streets.

"The tram stopped right outside our hotel."

taxi noun

A car with a driver that you pay to take you somewhere.

"Let's take a taxi."

bicycle noun

A vehicle with two wheels that you sit on and move by turning pedals.

"He goes to work by bicycle."

plane noun

A vehicle with wings that flies through the air.

"The plane lands at nine o'clock."

boat noun

A vehicle used for traveling on water.

"We took a boat across the lake."

ferry noun

A boat or ship that transports passengers and vehicles across water.

"We boarded the ferry to cross the bay."

12.2 Travel Infrastructure & Tickets (13 terms)

train station noun

A place along a railway line where trains stop for passengers.

"I will meet you outside the central train station."

bus stop noun

A designated spot along a road where buses pause for passengers.

"Wait at the bus stop for the number twelve bus."

airport noun

A place where planes land and take off.

"I am going to the airport now."

terminal noun

A main building at an airport or transport hub.

"Our flight departs from terminal two."

platform noun

The raised structure beside railway tracks where passengers board trains.

"The express train departs from platform four."

ticket noun

A piece of paper that shows you have paid for a journey.

"I need a train ticket."

single ticket noun

A ticket valid for a journey in one direction only.

"A single ticket to the city center costs three pounds."

return ticket noun

A ticket valid for travel to a destination and back.

"Buying a return ticket is cheaper than two single tickets."

boarding pass noun

A card or digital pass giving a passenger permission to board an aircraft.

"Please show your passport and boarding pass at the gate."

luggage noun

Bags, trunks, and suitcases containing personal belongings for travel.

"The porter helped us load our heavy luggage."

suitcase noun

A large case that you use to carry your clothes and other things when you are traveling.

"I am packing my suitcase for the trip."

delay noun

A period of time during which an event or service is delayed.

"Bad weather caused a one-hour flight delay."

flight noun

A journey made by flying in an aircraft.

"Our flight to Barcelona was smooth and pleasant."
