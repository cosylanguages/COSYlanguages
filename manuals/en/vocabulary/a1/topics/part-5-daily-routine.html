Unit 9: Daily Routines (Contextual Progression)

9.1 Morning Routine (10 terms)

wake up verb

To stop sleeping; to become conscious after sleep.

"I wake up at six thirty every morning."

get up verb

To rise from bed after waking.

"He gets up at seven and makes coffee immediately."

shower noun

A piece of equipment that you stand under to wash your whole body.

"I have a shower every morning."

brush verb

To clean something with a brush.

"I brush my teeth every morning."

breakfast noun

The first meal of the day.

"Breakfast is at seven o'clock."

coffee noun

A hot brown drink made from coffee beans.

"I drink coffee in the morning."

eat verb

To put food in your mouth and swallow it.

"We eat dinner at seven."

drink verb

To take liquid into your mouth and swallow it.

"She drinks coffee every morning."

dress verb

To put clothes on yourself or someone else.

"I get dressed at seven o'clock every morning."

wash verb

To clean something with water.

"He washes his car every Sunday."

9.2 Commute & Workday Routine (10 terms)

leave verb

To go away from a place.

"I leave the house at eight fifteen. She leaves work at six."

bus noun

A large vehicle that carries many people.

"I take the bus to work."

train noun

A vehicle that runs on rails.

"The train is fast."

drive verb

To operate a vehicle.

"She drives to work."

work verb

To do tasks as part of a job.

"She works from home three days a week."

arrive verb

To reach a destination.

"He arrives at the office at nine. The train arrives on time."

email verb

To send a message using the internet.

"Email me the details."

meeting noun

A time when people come together to talk about something.

"We have a meeting every Monday."

lunch noun

A meal eaten in the middle of the day.

"I eat lunch at one o'clock."

finish verb

To complete something.

"He finishes work at five. She finishes the report by noon."

9.3 Evening & Weekend Routines (10 terms)

home noun

The place where you live.

"I go home at six o'clock."

relax verb

To stop working and rest; to become less tense.

"I relax at the weekend and never check emails."

cook verb

To prepare food by heating it.

"She cooks dinner every evening. I do not cook — I order food."

dinner noun

The main meal of the day, usually in the evening.

"Dinner is at eight o'clock."

watch verb

To look at or observe attentively over a period of time.

"We watch a movie on TV in the evening."

read verb

To look at and understand written words.

"She reads the news every morning."

book noun

A written work published in print or electronic form for reading.

"I read a chapter of my book before going to sleep."

sleep noun

The natural rest your body needs each night.

"I need eight hours of sleep."

clean verb

To remove dirt, dust, or mess from something.

"I clean the flat every Saturday morning."

friend noun

A person you like and know well.

"She is my friend."

Unit 10: Time, Days, Months & Seasons

10.1 Clock Time & Parts of the Day (15 terms)

clock noun

A device that shows the time.

"The clock on the wall says ten o'clock."

watch verb

To look at or observe attentively over a period of time.

"We watch a movie on TV in the evening."

hour noun

A period of sixty minutes.

"I work eight hours a day."

minute noun

A period of sixty seconds.

"Wait a minute, please."

second noun

One of the sixty parts that a minute is divided into.

"The light changes every thirty seconds."

o'clock adverb

Used after a number from one to twelve to say what time it is.

"School starts at nine o'clock in the morning."

half past phrase

Thirty minutes past the hour.

"We have a team meeting at half past two."

quarter past phrase

Fifteen minutes past the hour.

"The train arrives at quarter past three."

quarter to phrase

Fifteen minutes before the next hour.

"It is quarter to four right now."

morning noun

The part of the day before 12 o'clock.

"I start work in the morning."

afternoon noun

The part of the day from noon until the evening.

"I finish work at five in the afternoon."

evening noun

The part of the day after afternoon and before night.

"We eat dinner in the evening."

night noun

The period of darkness between evening and morning.

"I sleep eight hours every night."

midnight noun

Twelve o'clock at night.

"The movie screening finished near midnight."

time noun

What is measured in minutes, hours, days, etc.

"What time is it?"

10.2 Days, Months & Seasons (23 terms)

Monday noun

The first day of the week.

"I start work on Monday."

Tuesday noun

The second day of the week.

"We have a meeting on Tuesday."

Wednesday noun

The third day of the week.

"Is it Wednesday today?"

Thursday noun

The fourth day of the week.

"I am busy on Thursday."

Friday noun

The fifth day of the week.

"I love Fridays!"

Saturday noun

The sixth day of the week.

"I relax on Saturday."

Sunday noun

The seventh day of the week.

"The shop is closed on Sunday."

January noun

The first month of the year.

"It is cold in January."

February noun

The second month of the year.

"February is a short month."

March noun

The third month of the year.

"Spring begins in March."

April noun

The fourth month of the year.

"It rains a lot in April."

May noun

The fifth month of the year.

"The weather is beautiful in May."

June noun

The sixth month of the year.

"Summer starts in June."

July noun

The seventh month of the year.

"We go on holiday in July."

August noun

The eighth month of the year.

"It is very hot in August."

September noun

The ninth month of the year.

"Children go back to school in September."

October noun

The tenth month of the year.

"October is in autumn."

November noun

The eleventh month of the year.

"It is often grey in November."

December noun

The twelfth month of the year.

"Christmas is in December."

spring noun

The season between winter and summer.

"Flowers grow in spring."

summer noun

The hottest season of the year.

"I love swimming in summer."

autumn noun

The season between summer and winter.

"Leaves fall in autumn."

winter noun

The coldest season of the year.

"It snows in winter."
