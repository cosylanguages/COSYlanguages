Unit 9: Daily Routines (Contextual Progression)

9.1 Morning Routine

Wake up, get up, turn off the alarm, make the bed, wash face, brush teeth, take a shower, get dressed, eat breakfast, drink coffee.

9.2 Commute & Workday Routine

Leave the house, catch the bus/train, drive to work, arrive at work, check emails, attend meetings, have a lunch break, finish work.

9.3 Evening & Weekend Routines

Get home, relax, cook dinner, watch TV, read a book, go to bed, sleep in on Saturday, clean the house, meet friends.

Unit 10: Time, Days, Months & Seasons

10.1 Clock Time & Parts of the Day

Clock, watch, hour, minute, second, o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to, morning, afternoon, evening, night, midnight, noon.

10.2 Days, Months & Seasons

Monday–Sunday, January–December, Spring, Summer, Autumn/Fall, Winter.
