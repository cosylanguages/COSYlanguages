Unit 16: Primitive Core Boards

16.1 Numbers 0–20, Tens, Hundreds & Thousands (14 terms)

zero noun

The numerical symbol 0.

"Water freezes at zero degrees Celsius."

one noun

Essential A1 vocabulary term for one.

"This is a key example sentence for one."

two noun

Essential A1 vocabulary term for two.

"This is a key example sentence for two."

three noun

Essential A1 vocabulary term for three.

"This is a key example sentence for three."

twenty noun

Essential A1 vocabulary term for twenty.

"This is a key example sentence for twenty."

thirty noun

The number 30.

"There are thirty days in April."

forty noun

The number 40.

"He works forty hours a week."

fifty noun

The number 50.

"Fifty percent of the group agreed."

hundred noun

The number 100.

"One hundred years make a century."

thousand noun

The number 1000.

"A thousand people attended the concert."

million noun

The number 1,000,000.

"Over one million tourists visit each year."

first adjective

Coming before all others in time or order.

"He was the first person to arrive."

second noun

One of the sixty parts that a minute is divided into.

"The light changes every thirty seconds."

third adjective

Coming immediately after the second.

"She finished in third place."

16.2 Colours & Shades (14 terms)

red adjective

Having the colour of blood or a ripe tomato.

"She has a red bag."

blue adjective

Having the colour of a clear sky.

"His car is blue."

yellow adjective

Having the colour of the sun or a lemon.

"She has a yellow umbrella."

green adjective

Having the colour of grass or leaves.

"She wears a green dress."

orange adjective

Having the colour of an orange fruit.

"He bought an orange jacket."

purple adjective

A color between blue and red.

"I have a purple shirt."

pink adjective

Having a pale red colour.

"She prefers pink to red."

brown adjective

Having the colour of wood or earth.

"She has brown eyes and dark hair."

black adjective

Having the darkest colour, like the night sky.

"He wears a black coat."

white adjective

Having the colour of snow or milk.

"The walls are white."

grey adjective

Having a colour between black and white.

"He has grey hair and a kind face."

light blue adjective

Having a pale shade of blue.

"She wore a light blue shirt."

dark green adjective

Having a deep or dark shade of green.

"The forest was dark green in summer."

bright red adjective

Having a vibrant, vivid red colour.

"A bright red sports car drove past."

16.3 Geometry & Basic Shapes (15 terms)

circle noun

A completely round flat shape.

"Draw a perfect circle on the page."

square adjective

Having four equal sides and four 90-degree angles.

"The box is square."

triangle noun

A geometric shape with three straight sides and three angles.

"A slice of pizza looks like a triangle."

rectangle noun

A shape with four straight sides and four right angles.

"The computer monitor is a rectangle."

oval adjective

Shaped like an egg.

"The mirror is oval."

heart noun

The organ in your chest that sends blood around your body.

"My heart is beating fast."

star noun

A shape with five or six points.

"Draw a yellow star on the chart."

diamond noun

A shape with four equal straight sides forming two acute angles.

"The road sign was shaped like a diamond."

cube noun

A solid three-dimensional square shape with six equal faces.

"Roll the six-sided ice cube."

sphere noun

A round three-dimensional shape like a ball.

"The Earth is a massive sphere."

cylinder noun

A solid geometric shape with straight parallel sides and circular ends.

"The tin can is a cylinder."

cone noun

A solid object that tapers smoothly from a circular base to a point.

"He ate an ice cream cone."

round adjective

Shaped like a circle.

"The table is round."

straight adjective

Not curving or bending.

"He has straight black hair."

curved adjective

Having a bending line without straight angles.

"The road follows a curved path."
