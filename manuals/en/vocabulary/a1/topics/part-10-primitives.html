Unit 16: Primitive Core Boards

16.1 Numbers 0–20, Tens, Hundreds & Thousands (14 terms)

zero noun

The numerical digit or symbol 0.

"The temperature dropped to zero degrees overnight."

one noun

The number or digit 1.

"She bought one red apple."

two noun

The number or digit 2.

"Two passenger trains arrived at the station simultaneously."

three noun

The number or digit 3.

"We waited three hours for our flight."

twenty noun

The number 20.

"There are twenty students in our English class."

thirty noun

The number 30.

"April has thirty days."

forty noun

The number 40.

"A standard work week consists of forty hours."

fifty noun

The number 50.

"Fifty percent of the participants answered correctly."

hundred noun

The number 100.

"One hundred years make one century."

thousand noun

The number 1000.

"More than a thousand people attended the festival."

million noun

The number 1,000,000.

"The city has a population of two million residents."

first adjective

Coming before all others in time or order.

"He was the first person to arrive."

second noun

One of the sixty parts that a minute is divided into.

"The light changes every thirty seconds."

third adjective

Coming immediately after second in order or rank.

"She finished in third position in the race."

16.2 Colours & Shades (14 terms)

red adjective

Having the colour of blood or a ripe tomato.

"She has a red bag."

blue adjective

Having the colour of a clear sky.

"His car is blue."

yellow adjective

Having the colour of the sun or a lemon.

"She has a yellow umbrella."

green adjective

Having the colour of grass or leaves.

"She wears a green dress."

orange adjective

Having the colour of an orange fruit.

"He bought an orange jacket."

purple adjective

A color between blue and red.

"I have a purple shirt."

pink adjective

Having a pale red colour.

"She prefers pink to red."

brown adjective

Having the colour of wood or earth.

"She has brown eyes and dark hair."

black adjective

Having the darkest colour, like the night sky.

"He wears a black coat."

white adjective

Having the colour of snow or milk.

"The walls are white."

grey adjective

Having a colour between black and white.

"He has grey hair and a kind face."

light blue adjective

Having a pale or light shade of blue.

"She wore a light blue cotton shirt."

dark green adjective

Having a deep or dark shade of green.

"Pine needles are dark green in color."

bright red adjective

Having a vivid, vibrant red color.

"A bright red sports car drove past the office."

16.3 Geometry & Basic Shapes (15 terms)

circle noun

A perfectly round two-dimensional geometric shape.

"Draw a circle on the paper with a compass."

square noun

An open public area in a city or town, usually surrounded by buildings.

"We met in the town square near the fountain."

triangle noun

A two-dimensional geometric shape with three straight sides and three angles.

"A slice of pizza resembles a triangle."

rectangle noun

A four-sided geometric shape with four right angles.

"The computer monitor screen is a rectangle."

oval adjective

Shaped like an egg.

"The mirror is oval."

heart noun

The organ in your chest that sends blood around your body.

"My heart is beating fast."

star noun

A conventional geometric symbol with five or six pointed arms.

"The teacher placed a gold star on her test."

diamond noun

A quadrilateral geometric shape with four equal sides placed obliquely.

"Road signs in the area are shaped like a diamond."

cube noun

A solid three-dimensional object with six equal square faces.

"Drop an ice cube into the glass of lemonade."

sphere noun

A perfectly round three-dimensional solid ball shape.

"The Earth is an oblique spherical planet."

cylinder noun

A solid or hollow geometric object with straight parallel sides and circular ends.

"Soup comes stored in a metal cylinder can."

cone noun

A solid or hollow object tapering smoothly from a circular base to a point.

"He enjoyed a chocolate ice cream cone."

round adjective

Shaped like a circle.

"The table is round."

straight adjective

Not curving or bending.

"He has straight black hair."

curved adjective

Having a bending or non-straight line or surface.

"The scenic mountain road follows a curved path."
