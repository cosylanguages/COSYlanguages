Unit 15: Hobbies, Physical Activity & Entertainment

15.1 Hobbies & Leisure (11 terms)

music noun

The sound of people singing or playing instruments.

"I like listening to music."

reading noun

The action or skill of reading books and written material.

"Reading daily improves your language skills."

drawing noun

A picture or diagram produced with pencils or pens.

"She submitted a detailed charcoal drawing."

painting noun

A picture made with paint.

"There is a beautiful painting on the wall."

gardening noun

The activity of cultivating and caring for a garden.

"Gardening is her favorite weekend hobby."

fishing noun

The activity or sport of catching fish.

"He spent Sunday morning fishing by the riverbank."

camping noun

The activity of spending holidays outdoors in a tent.

"We packed sleeping bags for our weekend camping trip."

travelling noun

The activity of visiting different places and foreign countries.

"Travelling opens your mind to diverse world cultures."

gaming noun

The activity of playing interactive video games.

"Online gaming with friends is popular among teens."

dancing noun

The movement of the body in a rhythmic way, usually to music.

"Dancing salsa is a fun way to stay active."

singing noun

The act or vocal performance of producing musical sounds with the voice.

"She practices singing in the local choir every Tuesday."

15.2 Sports & Physical Exercise (18 terms)

football noun

A game played by two teams of eleven players who kick a ball.

"They are playing football in the park."

soccer noun

A game played by two teams of eleven players kicking a spherical ball.

"Kids play soccer on the school field during recess."

basketball noun

A game where two teams try to throw a ball through a high net.

"He plays basketball every Wednesday."

volleyball noun

A game in which two teams hit a ball back and forth over a high net.

"We played beach volleyball during our summer holiday."

tennis noun

A game played by two or four players with rackets and a ball.

"Do you want to play tennis?"

swimming noun

The sport or activity of moving through water using limbs.

"Swimming is a great low-impact full-body exercise."

running noun

The activity or sport of running on foot as exercise.

"He goes running in the park every morning at six."

cycling noun

The sport or activity of riding a bicycle.

"Cycling to work is healthy and environmentally friendly."

skiing noun

The sport or activity of sliding over snow on skis.

"We go skiing in the Swiss Alps every winter."

yoga noun

A Hindu spiritual and physical discipline involving posture and breath control.

"Practicing morning yoga helps stretch muscles and relax."

gym noun

A room or building with equipment for doing physical exercise.

"I go to the gym three times a week."

pitch noun

An outdoor playing area marked for sports such as football.

"The referee stepped onto the football pitch."

court noun

A marked rectangular area for playing games such as tennis or basketball.

"They reserved the indoor tennis court for two hours."

match noun

A contest or game in which people or teams compete.

"Our local team won the championship match."

team noun

A group of players forming one side in a competitive game.

"All eleven players worked together as a cohesive team."

win verb

To be the best in a game or competition.

"We want to win the game."

lose verb

To fail to win a game, match, or competition.

"Our team played hard but lost the match by one goal."

score a goal phrase

To kick or strike a ball into the opponent's net to score a point.

"The striker scored a goal in the final minute of play."
