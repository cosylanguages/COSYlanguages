Unit 14: Nature, Climate & Animals

14.1 Natural Landscapes (17 terms)

nature noun

The physical world including plants, animals, landscapes, and natural forces.

"Taking walks in nature helps reduce daily stress."

mountain noun

A large natural elevation of the earth's surface rising abruptly.

"Snow covered the top of the high mountain peak."

hill noun

A naturally raised area of land, smaller than a mountain.

"They walked up the green hill to enjoy the view."

valley noun

A low area of land between hills or mountains.

"A sparkling river winds through the fertile valley."

forest noun

A large area of land covered densely with trees and undergrowth.

"Birds sing in the quiet pine forest."

jungle noun

An area of land overgrown with dense tropical vegetation.

"Monkeys and parrots live in the tropical jungle."

river noun

A large natural stream of water flowing towards the sea or a lake.

"Boats sail along the wide river."

lake noun

A large body of water surrounded entirely by land.

"We rented a small wooden boat on the lake."

sea noun

The expanse of salt water that covers most of the earth's surface.

"Ships travel across the open sea."

ocean noun

A vast body of salt water covering major areas of the globe.

"Whales migrate across the deep ocean."

beach noun

A pebbly or sandy shore beside a body of water.

"Children love building sandcastles on the sunny beach."

island noun

A piece of land surrounded by water on all sides.

"We took a ferry to visit the picturesque island."

desert noun

A dry barren region of land with very little rainfall.

"Camels can travel long distances across the hot desert."

field noun

An area of open land, especially one used for pasture or crops.

"Yellow sunflowers bloom across the farmer's field."

tree noun

A tall perennial plant with a wooden trunk and spreading branches.

"Apples grow on the large tree in our garden."

flower noun

The colorful blossom of a plant.

"She placed a vase of fresh red flowers on the table."

grass noun

Low green vegetation growing on lawns and pastures.

"Cows graze peacefully on the green grass."

14.2 Weather & Climate (14 terms)

sun noun

The star that gives light and heat during the day.

"The sun is very strong today."

cloud noun

A visible mass of condensed water vapor floating in the atmosphere.

"White fluffy clouds drifted across the blue sky."

rain noun

Water that falls from clouds in drops.

"There is a lot of rain in autumn."

snow noun

Soft white pieces of frozen water that fall from the sky when it is very cold.

"We have a lot of snow in winter."

wind noun

Air that moves, sometimes strongly.

"The wind is very strong today."

fog noun

A thick mist of tiny water droplets hanging low near the ground.

"Dense morning fog reduced visibility on the highway."

sunny adjective

Having a lot of light from the sun.

"It is a sunny day."

rainy adjective

Having a lot of rain.

"It is a rainy day."

snowy adjective

With a lot of snow.

"We have snowy weather in January."

windy adjective

With a lot of wind.

"It is very windy today."

warm adjective

Slightly hot; pleasantly heated.

"The office is warm and comfortable."

hot adjective

Having a high temperature.

"The coffee is hot."

cold adjective

Having a low temperature.

"The water is cold."

freezing adjective

Extremely cold, near or below the freezing point of water.

"Dress warmly because it is freezing outside today."

14.3 Animals & Wildlife (17 terms)

dog noun

An animal that is often called 'man's best friend'.

"I walk my dog in the park."

cat noun

A small animal with fur that many people keep at home.

"My cat is sleeping."

rabbit noun

A small animal with long ears.

"The rabbit is eating a carrot."

cow noun

A large farm animal that gives milk.

"The cow is in the field."

horse noun

A large animal that people can ride.

"She can ride a horse."

sheep noun

A farm animal with thick wool.

"The sheep are eating grass."

pig noun

A pink farm animal.

"Pigs are intelligent animals."

chicken noun

A common type of meat from a bird.

"I like roast chicken."

lion noun

A large wild cat that lives in Africa.

"The lion is the king of animals."

tiger noun

A large wild Asian cat with distinctive black stripes on orange fur.

"The tiger stalked quietly through the forest shadow."

elephant noun

A very large animal with a long trunk.

"I saw an elephant at the zoo."

bear noun

A large strong wild animal with thick fur.

"I saw a bear in the forest."

monkey noun

A wild animal that can climb trees and has a long tail.

"Monkeys like bananas."

dolphin noun

A very intelligent sea animal that looks like a large fish with a pointed mouth.

"Dolphins like to jump out of the water."

shark noun

A large fish that has sharp teeth and a pointed fin on its back.

"The shark is swimming in the ocean."

bird noun

An animal that has feathers and wings, and usually can fly.

"A bird is singing."

butterfly noun

An insect with four large, usually brightly colored wings.

"Look at that beautiful butterfly."
