Environmental TermSustainability Goal
circular economySystem eliminating waste through continuous reuse, repair, and recycling.
carbon offset schemeReduction in GHG emissions made to compensate for emissions made elsewhere.
climate adaptationAdjusting natural or human systems in response to actual/expected climate shocks.
Examples
  • Investing in green technology is essential for long-term climate adaptation.
  • A circular economy model decouples economic growth from finite resource consumption.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ Circular economy encourages single-use disposable manufacturing.
tap to see the correct form
