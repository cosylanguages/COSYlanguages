Food, Health & the Body

Symptoms, doctor appointments, pharmacy terms, diets, and emergency vocabulary.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe illness symptoms to a doctor or pharmacist and discuss dietary needs.
DomainVocabularyExample
Symptoms & Bodyheadache, fever, sore throat, cough, allergyI have a sore throat and a mild fever.
Medical Servicesprescription, painkillers, appointment, doctorThe pharmacist gave me painkillers.
Diet & Foodvegetarian, ingredients, calories, balanced dietAre there any nut ingredients in this dish?
