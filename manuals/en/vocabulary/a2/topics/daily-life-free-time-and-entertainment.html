A2 Key Topic: Leisure

Daily Life, Free Time & Entertainment

Hobbies, cinema, music, streaming, and describing past leisure experiences.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss entertainment choices, streaming habits, and recount leisure experiences.
CategoryVocabularyExample
Media & Streamingseries, episode, playlist, stream, channelI watched three episodes of that new series.
Hobbies & Sportscycling, photography, gym, board gamesHe took up photography during his free time.
Past Experience"It was the first time I...", "I've never tried..."It was the first time I visited an art gallery.
More Examples
  • What kind of music playlists do you listen to when working?
  • I've never tried windsurfing, but I'd love to try it.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌It was the first time I go to cinema.
tap to see the correct form
✅It was the first time I went to the cinema.
