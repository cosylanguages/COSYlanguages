A2 Key Topic: Shopping & Services

Shopping & Services

Comparing prices, quality, returns, complaints, bank, post office, and online shopping.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can manage retail purchases, request refunds, handle bank transactions, and shop online.
ContextKey TermsExample
Retail & Salesreceipt, refund, discount, special offer, fitting roomI would like a refund, here is my receipt.
Services (Bank/Post)account, parcel, postage stamp, transferI need to send this parcel to Germany.
Online Shoppingdelivery, basket, checkout, track orderYou can track your order using this code.
More Examples
  • Is there a discount for students?
  • The fitting room is over there on the left.
⚠️ Common mistakes

tap to see the correct form
✅Can I have a receipt for this shirt? (Recipe = cooking instructions!)
