A2 Key Topic: Places & Nature

Home, Neighbourhood & Environment

Renting, neighbourhood features, basic environmental problems, and weather phenomena.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss housing options, renting terms, neighbourhood amenities, and weather events.
TopicKey TermsExample
Renting & Housingflat, landlord, rent, deposit, balconyThe landlord requested a one-month deposit.
Neighbourhoodsuburb, pedestrian area, park, local shopWe live in a quiet suburb outside the city.
Environmentpollution, litter, recycling, climateRemember to put plastic bottles in the recycling bin.
Weatherstorm, flood, drought, fog, heavy rainThe flight was delayed due to thick fog.
More Examples
  • Our new apartment has a spacious balcony facing south.
  • The heavy storm caused a minor flood on our street.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌The climate today is very rainy.
tap to see the correct form
✅The weather today is very rainy. (Weather = today; Climate = long term)
