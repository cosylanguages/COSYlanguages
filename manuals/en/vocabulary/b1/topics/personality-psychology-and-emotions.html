Personality, Psychology & Emotions

Character nuances, temperament, emotional wellbeing, burnout resilience, and growth mindset.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe personality traits (*conscientious, level-headed*), emotional wellbeing (*mindfulness, resilience*), and personal growth milestones.

DomainVocabularyExample
Character Traitsconscientious, level-headed, charismatic, empatheticShe is a conscientious student who never misses deadlines.
Wellbeing & Growthresilience, burnout, mindfulness, coping mechanismPracticing mindfulness helps prevent workplace burnout.
Phrasal Verbsbounce back, cope with, come across as, open upIt took him a few months to bounce back after losing his job.
More Examples
  • He comes across as quiet, but he has a great sense of humour.
  • Building resilience is essential for navigating challenging career transitions.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌She is very sensible about other people's feelings.
tap to see the correct form
✅She is very sensitive to other people's feelings. (Sensible = reasonable/practical; Sensitive = empathetic/tender!)
