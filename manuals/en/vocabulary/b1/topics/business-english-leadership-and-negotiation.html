PET/FCE Topic: Business & Negotiation

Business English, Leadership & Negotiation

Corporate strategy, key performance indicators (KPIs), executive leadership, trade-offs, and negotiating deals.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can manage business negotiations (*trade-off, compromise*), corporate strategy, and KPI performance tracking.
DomainVocabularyExample
Corporate Strategycorporate strategy, KPI, stakeholder, executive leadershipWe reviewed quarterly KPIs during the board meeting.
Negotiation Skillstrade-off, compromise, agenda, chair a meeting, contractFinding a fair trade-off resolved the contract dispute.
Phrasal Verbswork out, deal with, bring forward, put off, close downWe managed to work out a mutually beneficial deal.
More Examples
  • The CEO presented the corporate strategy for international expansion.
  • She was appointed to chair the meeting on Monday morning.
