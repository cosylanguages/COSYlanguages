PET/FCE Topic: Arts & Heritage

Arts, Culture & Performing Arts

Art exhibitions, architectural heritage, live symphonies, theatrical productions, and cultural curation.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can critique performing arts (*theatrical play, live symphony*), architectural heritage, and museum curation.
DomainVocabularyExample
Performing Artssymphony, theatrical play, choreography, soundtrack, venueThe orchestra performed a breathtaking symphony.
Arts & Curationart gallery, exhibition, architectural heritage, aestheticsThe cathedral is a masterpiece of architectural heritage.
Phrasal Verbsput on, show off, act out, draw on, compose ofThe theater group will put on a new play in May.
More Examples
  • The museum curator organized an impressive art exhibition.
  • Her choreography combined traditional dance with modern movement.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌They made a new theatrical play.
tap to see the correct form
✅They put on / staged a new theatrical play.
