PET Topic: Media & Tech

Media, Digital Life & Cybersecurity

News journalism, media bias, fact-checking, social media algorithms, and cybersecurity encryption.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can evaluate news credibility (*media bias, fact-checking*), manage digital footprints, and secure sensitive data.
DomainVocabularyExample
Journalismmedia bias, objective, broadcast, fact-check, sensationalAlways verify sources to avoid falling for media bias.
Digital Networksalgorithm, digital footprint, engagement, subscriberSocial media algorithms prioritize high engagement posts.
Cybersecurityencryption, data breach, malware, firewall, backupEnd-to-end encryption keeps messaging secure.
More Examples
  • Always fact-check breaking news stories before sharing them online.
  • A major data breach forced the company to reset user passwords.
⚠️ Common mistakes
