turn down

Phrasal Verb 🧩 Transitive (VT) Separable down Level: B1
📖 Definition: To reject an offer or invitation, or to reduce volume.
💡 Structure & Pattern: turn down [something] OR turn [something / pronoun] down
📌 Grammar, Particle & Separability Rule: Separable phrasal verb. Noun objects can go before or after particle, but pronoun objects MUST go in between.
⚠️ Common Pitfall & Mistake: ❌ He turned down it. ➔ ✅ He turned it down.
