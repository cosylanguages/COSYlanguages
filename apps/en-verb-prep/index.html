depend

Intransitive (VI) Which preposition goes with this verb?
"Our plans depend [ ? ] the weather."

depend

Intransitive (VI) on / upon Level: A2
📖 Definition: To be controlled, decided, or influenced by someone or something else.
💡 Structure & Pattern: depend on [someone / something]
📌 Grammar & Transitivity Rule: Always requires the preposition 'on' (or formal 'upon') before a noun or gerund object.
⚠️ Common Pitfall & Mistake: ❌ It depends of the situation. ➔ ✅ It depends on the situation.
