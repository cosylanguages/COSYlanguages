influence

Phrasal Verb 🧩 Transitive (VT) Separable No Preposition (Direct Object) Level: B1
📖 Definition: To have an effect on the character, development, or behavior of someone or something.
💡 Structure & Pattern: influence [someone / something] (direct object)
⚡ Verb vs. Noun Preposition Contrast: Verb: 'It influences me' (no prep) ➔ Noun: 'It has an influence ON me' (uses ON)
📌 Grammar, Particle & Separability Rule: Direct transitive verb without preposition! Do NOT use 'on' after the verb 'influence'. However, the noun 'influence' DOES take 'on'.
⚠️ Common Pitfall & Mistake: ❌ It influences on my decision. ➔ ✅ It influences my decision.
