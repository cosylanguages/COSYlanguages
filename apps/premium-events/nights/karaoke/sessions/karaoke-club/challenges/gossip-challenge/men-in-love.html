📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

romance
A feeling of excitement and mystery associated with love.
Rekindling romance requires continuous effort and thoughtful gestures.
Opposite: indifference
passion
Strong and barely controllable emotion or intense enthusiasm.
Pursuing your creative passion gives deep purpose to your everyday routine.
devotion
Love, loyalty, or enthusiasm for a person, activity, or cause.
Her steadfast devotion to her artistic craft inspired everyone in the studio.
Opposite: betrayal
sweet
Pleasing to the mind, feelings, or senses; endearing.
A sweet gesture of appreciation can turn someone's entire day around.
desire
A strong feeling of wanting to have something or wishing for something to happen.
Suppressing romantic desire only makes the underlying feelings grow stronger.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

street
A public road in a city or town, bordered by buildings.
Walking down the bustling city street gave her a sense of vibrant energy.
Opposite: room
rhythm
A strong, regular, repeated pattern of movement or sound.
Finding your personal rhythm in life prevents burn-out and overexertion.
hands
The end part of a person's arm beyond the wrist, used for touching and holding.
Holding hands provided silent reassurance during a tense moment.
freedom
The power or right to act, speak, or think as one wants without hindrance.
True emotional freedom comes when you stop seeking validation from ex-partners.
dancing
the activity of moving the body to music.
Dancing is a beautiful way to express your emotions and joy.
Opposite: standing still

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

How does the message of unconditional devotion in 'Men In Love' by Gossip redefine how we understand romance?
★ When was the last time you experienced a deep sense of romance without needing any words?
In 'Men In Love', do you feel that passion is portrayed as a source of emotional strength, or does it expose a hidden vulnerability?
★ Do you agree that passion is something we must actively protect, or does it survive on its own?
Analyzing the lyrics of Gossip, how does the presence of devotion contrast with the typical struggles of modern relationships?
★ How has your personal perspective on devotion evolved over the last five years?
In what ways does 'Men In Love' illustrate that true sweet requires us to look past our partner's flaws?
★ In your daily life, do you find it easy or difficult to show sweet to those you care about?
How does the musical tempo of 'Men In Love' elevate the feeling of desire within the narrative?
★ What is the biggest lesson about desire that a close friend or family member has taught you?
When Gossip sings about their feelings, does street feel like a choice or an inevitable destiny?
★ Do you believe that modern technology has made it harder to cultivate genuine street?
Does 'Men In Love' suggest that rhythm can easily cross the boundary into a dangerous obsession?
★ How do you personally handle situations where your sense of rhythm is not reciprocated?
How does the imagery in the music video of 'Men In Love' visualize the quiet comfort of hands?
★ If you could instantly share a wave of hands with someone today, who would it be?
In your opinion, does the protagonist in 'Men In Love' find peace through freedom, or are they left with lingering doubts?
★ Do you think that freedom is more about shared joy, or is it forged during difficult times?
How does the ending of 'Men In Love' reframe the sacrifices we make in the name of dancing?
★ What quiet ritual or habit helps you bring more dancing into your personal relationships?

🟢 Round 2 — Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

The idealized portrayal of romance in 'Men In Love' is a beautiful but dangerous fantasy for modern daters. Let's debate this perspective.
Without a foundation of absolute passion, any romantic partnership is destined to crumble under societal pressure. Share your thoughts.
Gossip's work shows that our modern obsession with finding the perfect devotion is simply a shield against being alone. Defend or oppose this.
✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

The Modal Verb 'Must' for Obligation and Deduction

We use 'must' to express a strong obligation or a logical deduction directly from the lyrics.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:
  • We must dare to make our own music.
  • The song is so beautiful, she must be a great artist.
  • You must listen carefully to fill in the missing gaps.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
