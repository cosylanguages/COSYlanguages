🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

How does the journey of self-discovery in 'Real Power' by Gossip redefine what it means to achieve strength?
★ What is a personal goal or dream that has driven your own strength over the past year?
In 'Real Power', does the pursuit of voice feel like a struggle against outer rules or an inner conflict?
★ Have you ever had to step out of your comfort zone to find your own voice?
How does Gossip use the lyrics of 'Real Power' to highlight the importance of personal truth?
★ Do you find that quiet reflection or active action helps you grow in truth?
Does 'Real Power' suggest that power is something we can find alone, or do we need others to grow?
★ What is the most difficult challenge you have faced that ultimately built your power?
How does the musical style of 'Real Power' enhance the message of strength and freedom?
★ Who is the mentor or teacher who has influenced your personal freedom the most?
In 'Real Power', is town presented as a sudden breakthrough or a slow, painful process?
★ Do you believe that mistakes are essential for our personal town, or can they be avoided?
How does Gossip challenge traditional views of success and force in this song?
★ How do you personally balance your desire for force with your daily responsibilities?
What role does vulnerability play in allowing the main character to achieve unity in 'Real Power'?
★ If you could write a letter to your younger self about unity, what advice would you give?
How do the lyrics of 'Real Power' show that stand requires us to confront our deepest fears?
★ Does your current environment support your personal stand, or does it restrict it?
In your opinion, is noise the ultimate goal of the character's journey in 'Real Power'?
★ What daily habit or practice helps you maintain your focus on personal noise?

🟢 Round 2 — Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

The search for absolute strength in 'Real Power' is an exhausting quest that often leads to isolation. Discuss.
Without a willingness to embrace our own flaws, true personal voice is completely impossible. Share your thoughts.
Gossip's song suggests that society's expectations are the greatest obstacle to our personal truth. Defend or oppose.
✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

The Modal Verb 'Must' for Obligation and Deduction

We use 'must' to express a strong obligation or a logical deduction directly from the lyrics.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:
  • We must dare to make our own music.
  • The song is so beautiful, she must be a great artist.
  • You must listen carefully to fill in the missing gaps.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
