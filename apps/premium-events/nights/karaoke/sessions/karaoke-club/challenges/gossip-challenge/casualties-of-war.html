✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Present Participles as Durative Modifiers

In the song Casualties Of War, the repetitive lyric chunk "We're casualties of war... carrying the scars" highlights the use of Present Participles as Durative Modifiers. This structure supports: Describing lingering inner struggles and trauma.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:
  • Practice using "We're casualties of war... carrying the scars" or related forms actively during your discussion rounds today.
  • Notice how the lyric chunk "We're casualties of war... carrying the scars" builds rhythmic familiarity and emotional weight.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
