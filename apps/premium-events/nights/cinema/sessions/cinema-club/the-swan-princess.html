🎬 Final Challenge — You're the Director!▲

You have been hired to remake this classic film! You must alter ONE key element. Choose your directorial pivot:

1. The Setting 🌲 Move the story from a classic fairy tale kingdom and Swan Lake to a completely different location, universe, or era. How does this shift the atmosphere?
2. The Ending 🔚 Alter the final twist or resolution of the central conflict of defeating dark curses and proving true romantic devotion. How does this affect the audience's emotional payoff?
3. The Soundtrack 🎵 Swap the current score for something radically different (e.g., electronic synth-wave, classical silence, or heavy rock) to completely redefine the tone.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I have seen that movie yesterday→I saw that movie yesterdayUse the simple past for actions completed in the past.
She is more older than him→She is older than himDo not double comparatives; 'older' is already comparative.
He gave to her a beautiful book→He gave her a beautiful bookDirect object doesn't require 'to' after 'give' in English.
