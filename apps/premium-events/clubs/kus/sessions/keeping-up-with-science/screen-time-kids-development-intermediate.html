🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • How many hours of screen time did you have as a child, and how does that compare to kids today?
  • What daily activities in your childhood replaced digital entertainment?
  • Do you think digital devices help or hinder early communication skills?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • If an 8-year study shows screen time impacts attention, should schools limit digital tablet use in class?
  • How can parents teach children healthy digital boundaries without completely banning screens?
  • What strategies help adults reduce their own screen time when working long hours?

⚡ Expressing Cause & Result (B1)

Use connectors like due to, as a result, and leads to to describe cause and effect relationships clearly.

  • Screen exposure increased, as a result children spent less time playing outdoors.
  • Poor sleep patterns are often due to late-night device usage.
  • Consistent reading leads to stronger vocabulary development over time.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Guide B1 learners to discuss screen habits neutrally without judging parenting choices. Encourage using cause-and-effect connectors during Round 2.
