Multilingual People May Have Brains Up to 13 Years Younger

Discover how learning and speaking multiple languages keeps your brain active, flexible, and healthy.

Duration

60 mins

Languages

English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Source Article

Reference Link 🔗

🔬 Field Journal Research Log

New neurological research shows that speaking more than one language acts as a natural exercise for the brain. Multilingual individuals demonstrate better memory skills and maintain youthful brain health up to 13 years longer than monolinguals.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Comparatives & Superlatives (younger, healthiest) (A2))

Use comparatives to describe brain benefits: "Multilingual brains stay younger than monolingual ones." / "Learning languages is one of the best ways to keep your mind active."

🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)
1. According to the scientific study, how does speaking multiple languages help keep the brain younger?
★ How many languages can you speak or understand?
2. Why is practicing a second language considered great cognitive exercise?
★ How long have you been studying English?
3. What is the biggest mental benefit you have noticed since learning new vocabulary?
★ Do you feel smarter or more creative when speaking another language?
4. How does keeping an active mind protect your memory as you get older?
★ What games or hobbies do you play to exercise your mind?
5. Can being multilingual make traveling to new countries more enjoyable?
★ Where would you like to travel next using your English skills?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)
6. Should schools encourage children to become multilingual from a very early age?
★ At what age did you start learning your second language?
7. How does switching between two languages offer natural protection against mental fatigue?
★ Is it hard for you to switch between English and your native language?
8. Can adult learners achieve the same brain health benefits as childhood language learners?
★ Do you believe it is ever too late to start a new language?
9. What daily routine helps you keep your English skills active?
★ Do you listen to podcasts, watch movies, or read books in English?
10. How can future scientific research inspire more adults to study new subjects?
★ What new skill would you like to learn next year?
