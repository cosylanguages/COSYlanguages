🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • What languages do you speak or want to learn in the future?
  • When did you start learning your second language?
  • Do you feel your brain working hard when you speak English?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • If speaking two languages keeps your brain 13 years younger, why do many people wait to start learning?
  • What simple daily habit helps you remember new vocabulary words?
  • How does learning a new language change how you view other cultures?

⚡ Comparative Adjectives & Modifiers (A2)

Use younger than or much more active to compare brain function and habits.

  • Multilingual brains stay younger than monolingual brains.
  • Learning words is much more effective when practiced daily.
  • Her memory is stronger today than it was two years ago.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Keep language simple for A2 students. Focus on personal motivation for learning languages and daily habits.
