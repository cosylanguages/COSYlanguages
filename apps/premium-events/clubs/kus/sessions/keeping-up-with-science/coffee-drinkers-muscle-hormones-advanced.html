🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • What role does coffee or tea play in your daily productivity routine?
  • How do health news headlines about coffee shift public perception over decades?
  • Do you notice physiological changes when consuming caffeine regularly?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • How should nutritional science distinguish between correlation and causation in dietary studies?
  • Given metabolic variability, is it ethical for wellness brands to promote coffee as a therapeutic supplement?
  • What implications do biomarker studies have for personalized medicine and biohacking trends?

⚡ Advanced Subjunctive & Hypothesis Structures (C1)

Use inverted conditionals (e.g., Were caffeine to be...) and advanced hypothetical phrases to debate scientific findings.

  • Were caffeine to be classified as a metabolic modulator, regulations might tighten.
  • Had researchers isolated sweetener variables, results might have proven even clearer.
  • It is essential that dietary recommendations be tailored to individual metabolic profiles.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Facilitate a high-level academic debate on nutritional science methodologies, biomarker interpretation, and correlation vs causation.
