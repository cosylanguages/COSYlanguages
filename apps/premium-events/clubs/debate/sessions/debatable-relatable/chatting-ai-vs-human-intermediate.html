🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • In what roles (tutor, assistant, sounding board) do you currently find AI most helpful?
  • Have you ever felt relieved to talk to an AI because it won't judge you?
  • What is the biggest difference in feeling when receiving a message from a friend vs an AI?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • If someone uses an AI as a therapist or sounding board, is that a healthy coping tool or a risk?
  • Can AI conversations help lonely people build confidence, or will it isolate them further?
  • What boundaries should we establish when interacting with conversational AI in daily life?

⚡ Concession & Contrast Structures (B1)

Use although, whereas, and on the one hand / on the other hand to weigh both sides of the debate.

  • Although AI provides immediate responses, it cannot offer genuine empathy.
  • AI is available 24/7, whereas human friends have limited time.
  • On the one hand AI is convenient; on the other hand human bonds are deeper.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Encourage B1 learners to weigh nuanced roles (tutor vs therapist vs friend) using contrasting linking words.
