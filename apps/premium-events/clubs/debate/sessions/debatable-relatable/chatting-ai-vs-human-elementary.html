🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • Have you ever chatted with an AI chatbot like ChatGPT?
  • Do you prefer asking a friend for advice or searching with AI?
  • What is your favorite thing about chatting with a real human friend?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • Is chatting with an AI helper better for learning English than chatting with a real person?
  • Can an AI chatbot ever be a true friend, or is human contact always necessary?
  • What should you never share when chatting with an online chatbot?

⚡ Expressing Opinions with 'I think' & 'In my opinion' (A2)

Use simple opinion phrases to state your preference clearly.

  • In my opinion, chatting with AI is fast and easy.
  • I think human friends understand our feelings better.
  • I believe both AI and humans can help us learn languages.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Help A2 students express pros and cons using basic opinion phrases. Keep tone open and light.
