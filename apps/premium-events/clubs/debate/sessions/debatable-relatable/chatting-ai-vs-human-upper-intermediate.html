🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • Why are increasing numbers of adults seeking emotional companionship from artificial intelligence?
  • How does chatting with an entity that never gets tired or angry alter our expectations of friends?
  • Where do you draw the line between using AI as a productive tool vs an emotional substitute?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • Should regulatory bodies place warnings on AI applications designed to mimic human affection?
  • Will future generations raised alongside hyper-realistic AI companions view human relationships as inconvenient?
  • Does AI therapy democratize mental health support or pose unacceptable psychological risks?

⚡ Complex Hypotheticals & Mixed Conditionals (B2)

Use mixed conditionals (e.g., If AI hadn't advanced... we wouldn't be questioning...) to discuss technological shifts.

  • If conversational AI had not advanced so rapidly, we would not be questioning human attachment today.
  • If users understood the underlying code, they would have been less likely to form emotional dependencies.
  • Had developers implemented stricter guards, fewer vulnerable users would be reliance-prone.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Prompt B2 students to debate the ethical and psychological ramifications of synthetic intimacy and algorithmic therapy.
