🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • Whose ideas or books have had a profound positive effect on your mental outlook?
  • How does hearing calm, thoughtful perspectives change your reaction to stressful news?
  • Do you find it easy to practice compassion toward people with whom you disagree?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • Why does hearing positive, compassionate thinking make human minds happier?
  • How can individuals protect their emotional balance in an era of constant online noise?
  • In what ways can we cultivate a habit of offering uplifting dialogue to those around us?

⚡ Gerunds as Subjects & Prepositional Objects (B1)

Use gerunds (-ing forms) as noun concepts when discussing psychological states and actions.

  • Hearing positive perspectives helps quiet anxious thoughts.
  • They spoke about cultivating emotional balance through daily reflection.
  • Practicing compassion benefits the giver as much as the receiver.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Encourage B1 participants to reflect on how philosophy and mindfulness influence daily decision-making.
