Dalai Lama: Happier Minds

"Number of people have said to me, after hearing your thinking, their mind becomes much more happier." — Dalai Lama

Duration

60 mins

Languages

English

Level

Intermediate (B1)

Format

Interactive Discussion

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Number of people have said to me, after hearing your thinking, their mind becomes much more happier." — Dalai Lama

An intermediate exploration into how mindset transformation, ethical wisdom, and mental discipline build sustainable happiness.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Mindset /ˈmaɪnd.set/

[n.]

an established set of attitudes held by someone.

"Adopting a growth mindset encourages resilience."

≠ Antonym: Fixed belief

Perspective /pəˈspek.tɪv/

[n.]

a particular attitude toward or way of regarding something.

"A broader perspective reduces anxiety over minor issues."

≠ Antonym: Narrowness

Serenity /səˈren.ə.ti/

[n.]

the state of being calm, peaceful, and untroubled.

"Meditation cultivates lasting inner serenity."

≠ Antonym: Turmoil

Altruism /ˈæl.tru.ɪ.zəm/

[n.]

disinterested and selfless concern for the well-being of others.

"Practicing altruism enhances personal psychological well-being."

≠ Antonym: Egoism

Equanimity /ˌek.wəˈnɪm.ə.ti/

[n.]

mental calmness, composure, and evenness of temper.

"Maintaining equanimity during crises enables clear decisions."

≠ Antonym: Agitation

Empathy /ˈem.pə.θi/

[n.]

the ability to understand and share the feelings of another.

"Genuine empathy builds deep interpersonal trust."

≠ Antonym: Indifference

Contemplation /ˌkɒn.təmˈpleɪ.ʃən/

[n.]

deep reflective thought.

"Quiet contemplation helps clarify complex values."

≠ Antonym: Distraction

Resilience /rɪˈzɪl.jəns/

[n.]

the capacity to recover quickly from difficulties.

"Emotional resilience allows people to thrive under pressure."

≠ Antonym: Fragility

Benevolence /bəˈnev.əl.əns/

[n.]

the quality of being well meaning; kindness.

"Acts of benevolence create positive ripple effects."

≠ Antonym: Malevolence

Fulfillment /fʊlˈfɪl.mənt/

[n.]

satisfaction or happiness as a result of fully developing one's abilities.

"True fulfillment comes from meaningful contribution."

≠ Antonym: Frustration

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Modal Verbs of Possibility & Advice (can, could, should) (B1))

Use modals to discuss mental wellness strategies: "Practicing gratitude can transform your outlook." / "We should cultivate empathy to build better relationships."

🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)
1. How can shifting your mindset transform daily stress into mental clarity?
★ What personal belief has helped you overcome difficult moments?
2. Why does adopting a broader perspective make personal challenges feel less daunting?
★ How do you step back when you feel consumed by a problem?
3. Is inner serenity a natural trait, or can it be systematically cultivated through practice?
★ What daily habit contributes most to your mental tranquility?
4. How does practicing genuine altruism impact an individual's psychological health?
★ When did helping someone else give you a strong sense of joy?
5. Why is maintaining equanimity during conflicts essential for constructive dialogue?
★ How do you keep your composure when someone challenges your ideas?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)
6. How does deep empathy allow leaders to build more supportive communities?
★ Do you find it easy to connect emotionally with people from different backgrounds?
7. What role does quiet contemplation play in a hyper-connected modern world?
★ How much silent time do you set aside each day for reflection?
8. How can individuals strengthen their emotional resilience against unexpected setbacks?
★ What lesson did you learn from a past failure?
9. Why does public benevolence inspire trust across diverse social groups?
★ Who is a public figure you admire for their humanitarian efforts?
10. In what ways does finding inner fulfillment differ from seeking external validation?
★ What does a truly successful life look like to you?
☕ Café Philosophical Debate

Debate whether happiness is primarily an internal discipline or a product of external circumstances.
