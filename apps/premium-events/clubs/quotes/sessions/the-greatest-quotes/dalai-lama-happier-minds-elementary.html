Dalai Lama: Happier Minds

"Number of people have said to me, after hearing your thinking, their mind becomes much more happier." — Dalai Lama

Duration

60 mins

Languages

English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Format

Interactive Discussion

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Number of people have said to me, after hearing your thinking, their mind becomes much more happier." — Dalai Lama

This session explores how positive thinking, kindness, and mental clarity bring peace to daily life.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Peaceful /ˈpiːs.fəl/

[adj.]

quiet and calm; free from disturbance.

"A peaceful mind helps you sleep well."

≠ Antonym: Restless

Kindness /ˈkaɪnd.nəs/

[n.]

the quality of being friendly, generous, and considerate.

"Small acts of kindness make everyone happy."

≠ Antonym: Cruelty

Thinking /ˈθɪŋ.kɪŋ/

[n.]

the process of using your mind to consider something.

"Positive thinking changes your daily mood."

≠ Antonym: Thoughtlessness

Calm /kɑːm/

[adj.]

not showing or feeling nervousness, anger, or excitement.

"Deep breathing keeps your heart calm."

≠ Antonym: Anxious

Joy /dʒɔɪ/

[n.]

a feeling of great happiness.

"Spending time with good friends brings joy."

≠ Antonym: Sorrow

Compassion /kəmˈpæʃ.ən/

[n.]

sympathy and concern for the sufferings of others.

"Showing compassion makes communities friendly."

≠ Antonym: Indifference

Mindful /ˈmaɪnd.fəl/

[adj.]

conscious or aware of something in the present moment.

"Being mindful helps you enjoy your food."

≠ Antonym: Mindless

Gratitude /ˈɡræt.ɪ.tjuːd/

[n.]

the quality of being thankful.

"Expressing gratitude every morning creates a happy day."

≠ Antonym: Ingratitude

Gentle /ˈdʒen.tl̩/

[adj.]

having or showing a mild, kind, or tender character.

"Speak in a gentle voice when helping others."

≠ Antonym: Harsh

Contentment /kənˈtent.mənt/

[n.]

a state of happiness and satisfaction.

"True contentment comes from feeling peaceful inside."

≠ Antonym: Dissatisfaction

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Comparative Adjectives (much more happier / happier) (A2))

Use comparative adjectives to compare states: "My mind is happier now." In natural spoken English, people often add much for emphasis: "much happier" or "much more relaxed".

🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)
1. What makes your mind feel peaceful and happy during a busy week?
★ What is one small thing that always puts a smile on your face?
2. How does showing kindness to strangers improve your own mood?
★ When did someone show unexpected kindness to you?
3. Can changing your daily thinking make you feel much happier?
★ Do you try to stay positive when facing small problems?
4. What activities help you stay calm when you feel stressed?
★ Do you prefer walking in nature or reading a book to relax?
5. Where do you find the most joy in your daily routine?
★ What morning habit gives you energy for the day?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)
6. Why is compassion important when talking to friends who are sad?
★ How do you help a friend when they are having a bad day?
7. How can being mindful during meals make life more pleasant?
★ Do you turn off your phone when eating dinner?
8. What role does daily gratitude play in building a positive mind?
★ What three things are you thankful for today?
9. Why is a gentle response better than an angry reaction during a misunderstanding?
★ How do you keep your voice quiet when you feel upset?
10. How do you define personal contentment in simple words?
★ Does success or inner peace matter more to you?
✨ Quote Reflection

Share one piece of advice you would give to someone who wants a happier mind.
