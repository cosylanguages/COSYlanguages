🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • What kind words made you feel happy this week?
  • Who in your life gives you calm and positive advice?
  • What do you do when you want your mind to relax?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • How does listening to positive ideas change your mood during a busy day?
  • Can one person's happy thinking help a whole group of people feel better?
  • What simple daily act helps you keep your mind peaceful?

⚡ Comparative Forms for Feelings (A2)

Use much happier, calmer, or more peaceful to describe emotional changes.

  • After listening to peaceful music, my mind becomes much happier.
  • Walking in nature makes her feel calmer than watching news.
  • Positive thoughts make daily work more enjoyable.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Encourage A2 students to share simple personal stories about positive thinking, kindness, and feeling happy.
