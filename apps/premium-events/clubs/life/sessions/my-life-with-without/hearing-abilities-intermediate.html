🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • What is your favorite sound in nature or daily life that instantly makes you feel happy?
  • How do you feel when you spend an entire day in complete silence?
  • Have you ever experienced temporary hearing loss (e.g., during a cold or loud concert)? How did it feel?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • If you had to live without hearing for a month, what daily habit or activity would be hardest to adapt?
  • How do environmental sounds shape your memory of specific places and trips?
  • In what ways can cities improve acoustic design so environments are less overwhelming?

⚡ Describing Sensory Experiences with 'Looks like', 'Sounds like', & 'Feels like' (B1)

Use sensory verbs paired with prepositions to evoke vivid descriptions.

  • Walking through a forest sounds like soft rustling leaves.
  • A world without noise feels like stepping into a peaceful dream.
  • Navigating a bustling market without hearing looks like a silent movie.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Encourage B1 participants to use descriptive sensory language to contrast sound-filled daily routines with moments of quiet.
