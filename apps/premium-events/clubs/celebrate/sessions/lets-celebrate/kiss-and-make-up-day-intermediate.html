🎙️ Discussion Structure

Round 1: Personal Observations

  • What does 'making up' look like in your culture after a small argument?
  • Is it easier for you to apologize first or wait for the other person to reach out?
  • Why do minor misunderstandings sometimes become bigger than they need to be?

Round 2: Deep Analysis & Reflection

  • Why is admitting fault often difficult, even when we value the relationship?
  • What role does active listening play in repairing trust after a disagreement?
  • Can dedicated holidays like Kiss and Make Up Day encourage people to take positive action?

⚡ Second Conditional for Hypothetical Reconciliation (B1)

Use if + past simple, would + infinitive to discuss hypothetical peaceful resolutions.

  • If I were in your position, I would send a quick message to clear the air.
  • If people talked more openly, fewer grudges would persist over time.
  • She would offer an olive branch if she knew how he felt.

💡 Facilitator & Teacher's Note

Maintain a warm, empathetic group atmosphere. Focus on constructive communication skills and language for apologizing and making peace.
