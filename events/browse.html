Speaking Club Quick-Start Guide

New to our Speaking Clubs? Follow our level-calibrated roadmap to build confidence, expand vocabulary, and achieve conversational fluency with native teachers.

🌱 Beginner (A1–A2)

Build Foundation

Listen to guided dialogues, practice key phrases, and gain conversational confidence in small, friendly groups.

🚀 Intermediate (B1–B2)

Expand Fluency

Engage in real-world discussions, express complex opinions, and refine natural sentence structures.

🔥 Advanced (C1–C2)

Master Nuance

Debate abstract philosophical topics, master idioms, and articulate high-level persuasive arguments.
