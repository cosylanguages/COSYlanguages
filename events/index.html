Speaking Club Quick-Start Guide

New to our Speaking Clubs? Follow our level-calibrated roadmap to build confidence, expand vocabulary, and achieve conversational fluency with our native teachers.

🌱 Beginner Friendly (A1–A2)

Start with Let's Celebrate or The Greatest Quotes. Topics are tailored to everyday life, cultural rituals, and core vocabulary.

Show these sessions ➔
🚀 Intermediate (B1–B2)

Join My Life With & Without or Debatable & Relatable. Unpack personal choices, express regrets, and debate mild controversies.

Show these sessions ➔
🧠 Advanced (B2–C2)

Challenge yourself in Keeping Up with Science, Mind Matters, or I Couldn't Help But Wonder. Discuss biology, psychology, and abstract philosophy.

What we do

Four ways to connect

Whether you want to debate deep topics, enjoy a movie, sing along, or play interactive games, we have a session for you.

Speaking Club

A small group session (4–6 people) on a real-world topic chosen in advance. The teacher facilitates, corrects lightly, and keeps everyone speaking.

  • Languages English, French, Italian, Russian, Greek
  • Duration 60 min
  • Frequency Weekly
🎙️ Enter Speaking Clubs
🎬

Cinema Night

Watch a short film or scene in the target language, then discuss it as a group with the teacher. Focus on listening comprehension and natural reaction vocabulary.

  • Languages English, French, Italian, Russian, Spanish
  • Duration 90 min
  • Frequency Monthly
🍿 Enter Cinema Club
🎤

Karaoke Night

Learn languages through the power of music! Sing along to timeless songs, analyze rich slang words, master native accents, and discuss cultural contexts with a native COSYlanguages teacher.

  • Languages English, French, Russian, Greek
  • Duration 60 min
  • Frequency Bi-weekly
🎶 Enter Karaoke Club
🎮

Game Evening

Connect, compete, and speak! Play interactive, multi-player vocabulary, debate, charade, and listening games—crafted beautifully for offline couch and online hybrid play together.

  • Languages English, French, Russian, Spanish, German, Greek, Italian
  • Duration 60 - 90 min
  • Frequency Bi-weekly
