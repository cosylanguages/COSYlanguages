🔬 Field Journal Research Log

Specimen / Topic

Anthropogenic Thermal Dynamics

Primary Hypothesis

Self-censorship occurs among clima-researchers to avoid alarmism.

Experimental Findings

Over 70% of polled meteorologists admit to softening report titles.

Linguistic Focus

Reported speech & softening devices (e.g. He claimed that, it is believed that)

This session analyzes a highly controversial climate science debate involving a leading scientist who previously "proved" human-induced warming. This researcher now claims that a major government climate report has misinterpreted the scientific data. We will discuss the integrity of scientific models, public trust, and the complexity of predicting global climate patterns.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Self-censorship
the withholding of one's true findings due to external pressure.
The researcher admitted to practicing self-censorship to preserve funding.
Alarmism
the exaggerated raising of public fear regarding potential disasters.
Critics accused the meteorologists of unscientific climate alarmism.
Softening
the deliberate dilution of strong statements to make them palatable.
The softening of report summaries was criticized by younger researchers.
Socio-political
relating to the combination of social and political factors.
Climate data is inevitably scrutinized through a socio-political lens.
Mitigation
the action of reducing the severity, seriousness, or painfulness of something.
Global mitigation strategies require unprecedented international cooperation.
Discrepancy
an illogical difference or inconsistency between two sets of data.
They found a glaring discrepancy between the raw readings and the summary.
Consensus
a general agreement among a group of scientific experts.
An overwhelming scientific consensus exists regarding anthropogenic warming.
Dilution
the action of making something weaker in force, content, or value.
Politicians demanded a dilution of the strict emission regulations.
Scepticism
a doubting or questioning attitude towards popular dogmas.
Healthy scientific scepticism is vital, but climate denialism is dangerous.
Policy-maker
a person responsible for formulating governmental strategies.
The team presented their carbon-cycle modeling directly to policy-makers.

⚡ Grammar Practice — Reported Speech & Softening Devices▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Upper-Intermediate (B2))

Reported speech shifts tenses back in time. Softening devices (e.g., 'It is believed that...') make claims sound less direct or aggressive.

📝 Task 1 — Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: When reporting opinions, present simple usually shifts to _____. Softening devices use _____ constructions like 'it is said' to reduce directness.

📝 Task 1 — Part B: Structured Practice:

  2. It is widely _____ that self-censorship is becoming common in laboratories.
  3. The meteorologist reported that the glacier _____ entirely by 2025.
  4. Many policy-makers suggested that carbon taxes _____ increased immediately.
  5. It has been _____ that the report title was softened to prevent panic.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The scientifics did a controversy→The scientists had a controversy(Use noun 'scientists', verb 'have a controversy')
I am agree with the report→I agree with the report(Agree is a verb)
It depend on computer models→It depends on computer models(Depends + on, singular verb s)
