Specimen / Topic

Senior Neuroplasticity

Primary Hypothesis

Hippocampal neurogenesis persists into late senescent decades.

Experimental Findings

Complex visual stimuli trigger dendritic branching in nonagenarians.

Linguistic Focus

Gerunds as subjects and objects (e.g., Staying active is beneficial)

This session explores exciting scientific research showing that the human brain can continue to improve and adapt even into a person's 90s. We will discuss the concept of neuroplasticity and the cognitive exercises that help maintain brain health in later life. Participants will reflect on the lifestyle choices, physical activities, and mental challenges that support healthy aging.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Plasticity
the ability of the brain to change and adapt over time.
Adult neurogenesis is clear evidence of brain plasticity.
Improve
to make or become better in quality, strength, or ability.
Reading daily is an easy way to improve your memory.
Elder
an older person, especially one who is highly respected.
Elders play an essential role in preserving family history.
Cognitive
relating to the mental processes of perception and memory.
Solving puzzles helps maintain strong cognitive functions.
Stimulus
something that causes a reaction or activity in an organism.
A complex visual stimulus keeps the brain active.
Memory
the faculty by which the mind stores and recalls information.
Her grandmother has an exceptionally sharp memory.
Habit
a settled or regular tendency or practice.
Learning a language is a wonderful mental habit for seniors.
Active
participating in activities or physical exercise.
Seniors who stay active have much better brain health.
Science
the intellectual and practical study of the physical world.
Modern science shows that our brains can grow new cells.
Capacity
the maximum amount that something can contain or produce.
Our mental capacity does not have to decline as we age.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
My grandfather is 90 years→My grandfather is 90 years old(Specify 'old' or omit)
I hope to live until 100→I hope to live to 100(Use 'to' for age limits)
It depend of your genes→It depends on your genes(Preposition 'on' after depend)
