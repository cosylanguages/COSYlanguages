Theme

Gratitude, Unsung Heroes, and Kindness

National Someone Day is a beautiful opportunity to thank that special 'someone' who has made a difference in your life. Whether it is a helpful neighbor, a dedicated teacher, or a supportive coworker, this session is all about recognizing unsung kindness. We will discuss the positive impact of showing gratitude and how simple gestures can strengthen our daily connections. Join us for a lively B2 debate.

🔞
Sensitive Topic Note: This session focuses on sensitive or challenging personal and philosophical themes. Discussion is intended to be handled with extreme respect, empathy, and academic focus.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Gratitude
the feeling of being thankful and showing appreciation.
Cultivating deep gratitude in our daily lives has been shown to improve mental well-being.
Appreciation
the recognition of the good qualities of someone or something.
We must show real appreciation for the people who work silently to keep our communities clean.
Unsung
not praised, celebrated, or praised publicly despite doing valuable work.
Cleaners, delivery riders, and social workers are the unsung heroes of our modern cities.
Acknowledge
to accept or admit the truth, existence, or value of something.
It is vital to acknowledge the contributions of every single team member before finishing a project.
Kindness
the quality of being friendly, generous, and considerate.
A small act of kindness can start a positive chain reaction throughout the workplace.
Supportive
providing encouragement, help, or emotional assistance to someone.
Working in a highly supportive environment makes it much easier to handle stressful deadlines.
Apathy ≠ Empathy
a lack of enthusiasm, interest, or concern ≠ the ability to understand and share the feelings of another.
We must actively fight social apathy by teaching empathy and community values in schools.
Reciprocate
to respond to a gesture or action by making a corresponding one.
When someone does you a favor, it is natural to want to reciprocate their kindness in the future.
Altruistic
showing a disinterested and selfless concern for the well-being of others.
His decision to donate all his prize money to the local children's hospital was purely altruistic.
Nurture
to care for and encourage the growth or development of someone or something.
We need to nurture our close relationships by spending quality time together on a regular basis.
