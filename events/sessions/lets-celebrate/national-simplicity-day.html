This session is about National Simplicity Day and the beauty of simple living. We will talk about why people choose to have fewer things and live a quiet life. We will also learn simple words to describe our daily routines and hobbies.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Simple ≠ Difficult
easy to do or understand ≠ not easy to do; needing effort.
The basic idea is simple and easy to understand.
Quiet
with very little noise; calm.
I like to read a book in a quiet room.
Reduce
to make something smaller or less.
We can reduce our screen time on our phones.
Nature
the physical world with trees, plants, and wild animals.
I love walking in nature on sunny days.
Routine
your normal plan or habits for every day.
Eating breakfast is part of my morning routine.
Celebrate
to do something special for a fun day.
We celebrate this holiday with a small family dinner.
Tidy
to clean a room and put things in their correct places.
You should tidy your room every weekend.
Peaceful
calm, quiet, and with no stress.
He found a peaceful spot in the park to rest.
Hobby
an activity that you do for fun in your free time.
Gardening is a very relaxing and simple hobby.
Thankful
feeling or showing thanks; grateful.
I am thankful for my healthy life.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
Short introduction to daily schedules.
  • What is the most relaxing part of your day?
  • Do you have too many things in your bedroom or house?
🔵 Round 1 — General Discussion▲
Questions
Simple discussion about simple living and habits.
What does it mean to live a simple life?
★ What is one simple thing that makes you very happy?
Why is it important to spend time in a quiet place?
★ Where do you go when you want a quiet moment?
How can we reduce our monthly expenses easily?
★ What monthly expense did you reduce recently?
Why do people feel more relaxed when they are in nature?
★ How often do you walk in a forest or park?
What is your morning routine?
★ Is your daily routine busy or relaxed?
How does your family celebrate traditional holidays?
★ How did you celebrate your last birthday?
Why is it satisfying to tidy your wardrobe?
★ Do you need to tidy your bedroom desk today?
What makes a neighborhood feel peaceful?
★ Is your current neighborhood peaceful or noisy?
Is having a physical hobby better than a digital one?
★ What hobbies do you practice in your free time?
Why should we feel thankful for simple things like fresh coffee?
★ What simple thing are you most thankful for today?

🟢 Round 2 — Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Predictions
Simple future predictions about modern lifestyle trends.
In the future, hyper-connectivity will become so overwhelming that people will legally demand a 'right to disconnect' and reduce screen time on all digital networks.
★ Have you ever tried to reduce your social media use and go off-grid?
The rise of smart homes and AI assistants will make our daily routine so automated that we will lose the simple joy of physical chores.
★ Do you find doing simple tasks like washing dishes to be a good daily routine?
Governments will eventually introduce mandatory simplicity taxes to force citizens to lead a simple lifestyle with fewer possessions.
★ Do you think a simple life with only 100 personal possessions is possible?
As urban areas grow, access to quiet, undisturbed nature will become an expensive luxury reserved only for the wealthy.
★ How much would you pay to spend a weekend in beautiful nature with absolute silence?
A slow, traditional hobby like gardening or woodworking will completely disappear as virtual reality becomes more immersive.
★ Would you prefer a physical hobby like growing real tomatoes to a virtual reality game?
We will celebrate 'quiet quitting' and prioritizing rest over professional career success.
★ Would you choose to celebrate a low-paying job if it gave you more free time?
Minimalist micro-housing will become the standard way of living, forcing people to tidy up their homes permanently.
★ Could you happily tidy your life and live in a tiny, minimalist home?
In a hyper-commercialized future, we will feel thankful for simplicity only when it is sold to us as an expensive lifestyle brand.
★ Have you ever felt thankful for buying an expensive minimalist product?
Future school systems will replace academic grading with classes in mindfulness to help children live a peaceful life.
★ Do you think your childhood would have been more peaceful if you spent more time doing nothing?
The desire for a quiet life will eventually lead to a massive reverse-migration from modern megacities back to small, self-sustaining villages.
★ If you could move to a quiet country cottage, would you leave the city behind?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I prefer a life more simple→I prefer a simpler life(Comparative form of 'simple' is 'simpler')
I am work in a peaceful place→I work in a peaceful place(Simple present structure)
She tidies always her room→She always tidies her room(Adverbs of frequency come before the main verb)
