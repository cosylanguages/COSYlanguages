Celebrate Urban Beekeeping Day! Learn about how busy bees live in our modern towns and cities. Let's discuss why we need to protect bees and how we can grow wild plants on our balconies. This is an exciting session with simple questions for elementary learners.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Urban
relating to a town or city.
We need to build more green parks in urban areas.
Protect
to keep someone or something safe from harm or danger.
We must protect bees because they help grow our food.
Hive
a container or box where bees live and make honey.
The beekeeper opened the wooden hive very carefully.
Environment
the natural world, including the air, water, land, and plants.
City pollution is very bad for the environment.
Community
a group of people living in the same place or having a common interest.
Our local community works together to clean the park.
Wild
living or growing in nature; not kept or controlled by humans.
We can see beautiful wild animals in the national forest.
Beekeeper
a person who owns and takes care of bees.
The beekeeper wears a white protective suit to avoid stings.
Hobby
an activity that you do for pleasure when you are not working.
My favorite hobby is taking photos of nature.
Plant
a living thing that grows in the earth and has a stem, leaves, and roots.
I bought a small green plant for my living room.
Useful
helping you to do or achieve what you want; having a practical use.
A dictionary is a very useful tool for language students.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Would you like to have your own honey garden?
  • Do you find bees interesting or dangerous?
🔵 Round 1 — General Discussion▲
Quiz / Discussion
What green spaces do you have in your urban area?
★ Do you prefer living in urban cities or quiet villages?
How can we protect bees from city pollution?
★ Do you buy eco-friendly products to help protect the earth?
Would you put a wooden hive on your balcony?
★ Have you ever seen a real hive with active bees?
How does city noise impact our local environment?
★ Do you try to recycle your plastic bottles to keep the environment clean?
How can a local community build a green garden together?
★ Are you active in your local neighborhood community?
Do you prefer to see wild flowers or neat city parks?
★ When was the last time you walked in a wild forest?
What skills does a modern beekeeper need to learn?
★ Would you like to try the work of a beekeeper for one day?
Is growing green vegetables a popular hobby in your country?
★ What is your favorite outdoor hobby?
How often do you water a delicate plant at home?
★ Do you prefer green houseplants or colorful flowers?
Why are insects like honey bees so useful for humans?
★ What is the most useful tip you learned about saving electricity?

🟢 Round 2 — Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Predictions
In the future, every urban school will have a honey hive for students to learn biology.
★ Would you support having a bee hive at your local school?
Governments will pay citizens to protect rare wild flowers on their balconies.
★ Would you grow more flowers if the government paid you?
Humans will destroy the natural environment completely and live in underground virtual cities.
★ Would you want to live in a clean virtual city if the real world became too polluted?
Every local community will grow its own organic food on vertical city walls.
★ Do you think vertical walls are a useful way to grow food?
Being a beekeeper will become the most popular and fashionable outdoor hobby.
★ Would you tell your friends if your new hobby was keeping bees?
We will build gigantic artificial trees to help clean the urban air instead of planting real trees.
★ Do you think artificial trees are useful or a waste of money?
A robotic plant will clean the dust in our apartments better than natural houseplants.
★ Would you buy a robotic plant or a real green fern?
City laws will make it illegal to use chemical sprays in order to save wild insects.
★ Do you actively avoid chemical sprays in your household?
Every citizen will have to spend one weekend a year helping their neighborhood community clean local parks.
★ Would you enjoy spending a weekend cleaning parks with your neighbors?
A tiny tracking chip will be placed on every queen bee to help scientists protect hives.
★ Do you think tracking queen bees is useful for science?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The bee have beautiful wings. → The bee has beautiful wings. (Subject-verb agreement: 'The bee' is singular, use 'has'.)
I saw a bee on yesterday. → I saw a bee yesterday. (Time adverb: do not use the preposition 'on' before 'yesterday'.)
He is afraid to bees. → He is afraid of bees. (Preposition check: the adjective 'afraid' is followed by the preposition 'of'.)
