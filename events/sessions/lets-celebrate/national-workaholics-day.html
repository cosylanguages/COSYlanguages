Full Info 📖

This session is about National Workaholics Day and the balance between work and life. We will talk about why people work too much and how to rest. We will also learn easy words to describe our daily job and hobbies.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Balance
a state where different things are equal.
A good balance between work and sleep is important.
Career
a job that you do for a long time.
She wants a long career as a school teacher.
Overtime
extra hours that you work at your job.
He works overtime to get more money.
Relaxation
time to rest and feel calm.
Reading a book is my favorite relaxation.
Stress
a feeling of worry or pressure.
Too much work can cause a lot of stress.
Busy
having a lot of work or things to do.
I am very busy with work today.
Holiday
a time when you do not work and can travel.
They are going to Spain for their summer holiday.
Free time
time when you do not have to work or study.
In my free time, I like to play football.
Office
a room or building where people work at desks.
My office is very near the train station.
Task
a small job that you must do.
Cleaning the kitchen is my daily task.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
Introduce your job.
  • What is your job, and do you like it?
  • What time do you start and finish work every day?
🔵 Round 1 — General Discussion▲
Questions
Simple discussion about work habits.
Do you have a good work-life balance?
★ What is the most important thing for your daily balance?
Why do people want to have a fast career?
★ Do you want to change your career in the future?
How often do people in your country work overtime?
★ Did you work overtime last week?
What is your favorite method of relaxation?
★ How do you relax after a very busy day?
Does your job cause you a lot of stress?
★ What causes you the most stress at work or school?
Why are modern people always so busy?
★ Are you busy this weekend, or do you have free time?
Where do people go for their summer holiday?
★ Where did you go for your last holiday?
What are some popular free time activities in your city?
★ What free-time activities do you do with your family?
Do you prefer to work in an office or from home?
★ Is your current office comfortable for you?
How do you organize your daily task list?
★ What is your most difficult task today?

🟢 Round 2 — Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Predictions
Simple future predictions about jobs and rest.
In the future, the balance between work and personal life will disappear completely as our brains are permanently connected to the network.
★ How do you keep a good balance when your smartphone is always in your pocket?
Universal basic income will make everyone less busy with work, causing humans to lose their sense of purpose.
★ If you never had to be busy with jobs, what would you do to feel useful?
As artificial intelligence takes over every productive task, free time will become the most stressful part of human life.
★ Have you ever felt anxious during a day without any task to do?
The physical office will become obsolete, and remote work will turn our homes into 24/7 productivity hubs.
★ Do you work better when there is a clear boundary between your office and your bedroom?
Future employers will monitor stress levels using biometric brain-scans and force employees to take mandatory breaks.
★ Would you feel comfortable if your boss could track your emotional stress in real-time?
Working overtime will eventually be classified as an addictive behavior and treated with therapy.
★ Have you ever worked overtime to avoid dealing with personal life problems?
In the future, a person's worth will be judged by how they spend their free time rather than their jobs.
★ What creative activity would you pursue if you had unlimited free time?
We will see a massive global decline in retirement ages, turning every day into a lifelong holiday.
★ At what age do you hope to stop working and start your permanent holiday?
A highly successful career will become an exclusive privilege for those willing to sacrifice having a family.
★ Do you believe it is possible to have a top-tier career and a thriving family life at the same time?
In the future, we will have more relaxation hours as humanity looks back at 40-hour workweeks as historical slavery.
★ If you could redesign the workweek for better relaxation, how many hours would be ideal?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I have a busy job→I have a demanding job(Demanding is better for jobs than busy)
I am work in office→I work in an office(Simple present tense structure)
He work very hard→He works very hard(Third-person singular 's')
