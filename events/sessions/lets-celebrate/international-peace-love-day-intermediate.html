This session celebrates International Peace and Love Day, focusing on global harmony and positive connections. We will discuss the origins of this festive day and how small acts of kindness can foster a sense of community. The conversation will encourage participants to share personal stories of gratitude, compassion, and forgiveness in their daily lives. Join us for a friendly B1 conversation.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Harmony
a state of peaceful agreement and cooperation between people.
Living in harmony with your roommates makes home life much more comfortable.
Compassion
a strong feeling of sympathy and sadness for other people's suffering.
We must show real compassion to people who have lost their homes in natural disasters.
Forgiveness
the decision to stop feeling angry or resentful toward someone who hurt you.
Asking for forgiveness is often the hardest part of rebuilding a friendship.
Gratitude
the feeling of being thankful and showing appreciation.
Keeping a journal of the things you love is a great way to practice daily gratitude.
Kindness
the quality of being friendly, helpful, and caring.
A simple act of kindness, like holding the door open, can brighten a stranger's day.
Foster
to encourage or help something positive develop and grow.
Our teacher works hard to foster a safe and supportive learning environment.
Gesture
an action that communicates an emotion, idea, or physical state.
Offering a hot cup of tea is a simple gesture of welcome in our culture.
Global
relating to the entire world; worldwide.
Protecting our oceans is a global responsibility that requires every country's effort.
Festival
a special day or period of celebration, often cultural or religious.
The community organizing team set up a brilliant food festival in the local park.
Ritual
a regular action or series of actions that you perform in a set way.
Our family's Saturday morning breakfast ritual is something we never miss.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What is the most positive festival or holiday in your country?
  • Do you think there should be more international days dedicated to peace?
🔵 Round 1 — General Discussion▲
Quiz / Discussion
How does promoting harmony benefit local neighborhoods?
★ When do you feel most in harmony with your environment?
Why is showing compassion crucial during difficult social crises?
★ Have you ever received unexpected compassion from a stranger?
Why is offering forgiveness to a close family member often so difficult?
★ Is it easy for you to practice forgiveness after an argument?
How does practicing gratitude affect our long-term happiness?
★ What is one thing you feel deep gratitude for today?
Why are acts of kindness contagious?
★ What was the last simple act of kindness you performed?
How can communities foster a stronger sense of mutual aid?
★ How connected do you feel to the community where you live?
What simple gesture of friendship is universal across cultures?
★ What gestures of body language make you feel welcome?
How do global events shape our views of other countries?
★ Have you ever participated in a global project or event?
How does celebrating a festival preserve a community's culture?
★ What is your favorite traditional festival to celebrate?
Why do humans need a daily or yearly ritual to feel secure?
★ What is your most important daily ritual?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

Interactive task: Work in pairs to plan a simple peace festival for your neighborhood, highlighting one positive ritual.

🟢 Round 2 — Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Debate
In the future, we will have a unified global holiday where all businesses close for a day of peace.
★ If you had 24 hours of absolute global quiet, how would you spend that day?
The internet will eventually foster a single, unified culture, making local traditions obsolete.
★ Which of your local traditions do you want to foster and protect in a completely globalized world?
As society becomes more digital, traditional community ritual practices will disappear completely.
★ Have you already replaced any physical, offline ritual with digital or online ones?
Teaching empathy and compassion in schools will become far more important than teaching traditional academic subjects.
★ What is a lesson in compassion you wish you had been taught during your school years?
We will see fewer local conflicts and more active forgiveness as international communication and instant translation improve.
★ Do you find that forgiveness is easier to achieve online or face-to-face?
Global environmental crises will eventually force all competitive nations to live in perfect harmony.
★ Does the thought of finding harmony together to solve global challenges bring you hope?
Expressing gratitude to citizens of other countries will replace national borders and make them obsolete.
★ How does your sense of gratitude change when you travel outside your home country?
Acts of kindness and support done in virtual spaces will become just as powerful as those done in physical person.
★ Has an online act of kindness ever deeply comforted you during a difficult personal time?
A future world with a permanent peace festival and no competition will eventually become stagnant and uncreative.
★ Would a life-long peace festival feel boring without a bit of internal friction or struggle?
A simple gesture of peace will eventually replace complex modern technologies used to resolve personal conflicts.
★ Do you think a physical gesture like a handshake is the best way to find inner peace?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We must to keep the peace. → We must keep the peace. (Modal verb check: 'must' is followed by the bare infinitive 'keep' without 'to'.)
I make an act of kindness. → I do / perform an act of kindness. (Collocation: 'acts of kindness' are typically 'done' or 'performed', not 'made'.)
I am full of gratitude for you. → I am full of gratitude to you / towards you. (Preposition check: we feel/show gratitude 'to' or 'towards' a person for something they did.)
