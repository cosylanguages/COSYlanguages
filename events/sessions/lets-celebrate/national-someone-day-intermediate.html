National Someone Day is a beautiful opportunity to thank that special 'someone' who has made a difference in your life. Whether it is a helpful neighbor, a dedicated teacher, or a supportive coworker, this session is all about recognizing unsung kindness. We will discuss the positive impact of showing gratitude and how simple gestures can strengthen our daily connections. Join us for an inspiring B1 discussion.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Gratitude
the feeling of being thankful and showing appreciation.
Showing sincere gratitude can make a huge difference in your relationships.
Appreciation ≠ Indifference
the recognition of the good qualities of someone ≠ a total lack of interest or concern.
We must show deep appreciation for our teachers instead of cold indifference.
Connection
a positive relationship or link between people.
There is a strong connection between sharing meals and building a happy community.
Spontaneous
done in a sudden, natural way without planning.
Our spontaneous coffee meeting turned out to be the highlight of my week.
Gesture
an action that expresses a feeling or intention.
Holding the door open for an elderly person is a small but kind gesture.
Generosity
the quality of being kind, helpful, and giving to others.
Her generosity was clear when she offered to help us move our heavy furniture.
Impact
a powerful effect or influence on a situation or person.
A supportive coworker can have a major impact on your job satisfaction.
Genuine
real, honest, and sincere; not fake.
He offered a genuine apology for arriving late to our dinner.
Support
to give help, encouragement, or approval to someone.
My parents always support my career decisions.
Uplift
to make someone feel happier, more hopeful, or more positive.
Her funny stories always uplift my mood when I am feeling down.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Who is a special 'someone' that helped you this month?
  • Do you find it easy to say 'thank you' in public?
🔵 Round 1 — General Discussion▲
Quiz / Discussion
How do you express your sincere gratitude to a helpful coworker?
★ When was the last time you felt true gratitude for a friend's help?
Why is showing public appreciation so important in a modern company?
★ Do you feel appreciated by your colleagues or managers?
How do we build a strong social connection with neighbors?
★ Do you have a friendly relationship with the people living next door?
Why are spontaneous favors often the most memorable?
★ Have you ever received a spontaneous gift that made you smile?
What small gesture can a stranger do to make your day better?
★ What kind gesture did you do for someone recently?
Does showing generosity always require spending money?
★ How do you show generosity without using your wallet?
How does one person's kindness have a lasting impact on a whole class?
★ Who had the biggest positive impact on your childhood?
How can you identify a genuine friend from a superficial one?
★ Do you have a genuine friend you can call in the middle of the night?
How do you show support when a friend is going through a difficult time?
★ Who is the most supportive person in your life right now?
What kind of news or stories always uplift your spirits?
★ Do you actively look for stories that uplift you on social media?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

Interactive task: Design a perfect 'Thank You' card for a supportive coworker who helped you with a very hard task on a tight deadline.

🟢 Round 2 — Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Debate
In the future, technology will make it impossible to have a genuine human connection.
★ Do you think technology helps or hurts your personal connection with others?
We will rely entirely on AI models to support us with emotional guidance instead of human friends.
★ Would you ask an AI for emotional support if you had a bad day?
Future cities will require neighbors to perform a weekly kind gesture for each other by law.
★ Would you like it if a kind gesture was required by your city?
Sharing generosity online will replace traditional neighborhood charities completely.
★ Do you prefer to show generosity in person or through online apps?
Public indifference will become so strong that people will stop showing appreciation for key workers.
★ Do you actively show appreciation to delivery drivers and cleaners?
We will use special chips to immediately send our gratitude to others without speaking.
★ Would you use a chip to express gratitude, or is a verbal thank-you better?
Sincere actions will be replaced by fake, planned events, destroying all spontaneous joy.
★ Do you think modern life is losing its spontaneous character?
Our small actions will have a much bigger impact on the global environment than government policies.
★ Do you think your lifestyle choices have a genuine impact on the planet?
Smartphones will only show news that can uplift users, completely banning negative articles.
★ Would you buy a phone that only shows positive news designed to uplift you?
A world where everyone has to be nice and show appreciation all the time will become very boring.
★ Do you agree that a little friction is necessary to keep life interesting?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am very grateful of my teacher. → I am very grateful to my teacher / grateful for my teacher's help. (Preposition check: 'grateful' is followed by 'to' for people, and 'for' for actions/things.)
We discussed about our someone special. → We discussed our special someone. (Preposition error: the verb 'discuss' is transitive and does not take 'about'.)
It is very important to nurturing friendships. → It is very important to nurture friendships. (Infinitive structure: 'to' is followed by the base form of the verb 'nurture'.)
