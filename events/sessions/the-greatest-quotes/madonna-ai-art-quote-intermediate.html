Quote

"Algorithms and artificial intelligence are the opposite of taking risks and to me that is the opposite of making art."

This session debates the provocative quote by pop icon Madonna about the role of algorithms and artificial intelligence in creative industries. We will discuss whether machine-generated art lacks the vulnerability, error, and risk-taking that define true human creativity. Participants will explore the future of painting, music, and writing in an age of automated production.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Algorithm
a process or set of rules to be followed in calculations or other problem-solving operations, especially by a computer.
Streaming platforms use an advanced algorithm to suggest new songs.
Opposite
a person or thing that is totally different from or the reverse of someone or something else.
Cold is the natural opposite of hot.
Risk
a situation involving exposure to danger or loss.
Every great creative project involves some level of personal risk.
Art
the expression or application of human creative skill and imagination, typically in a visual form.
She believes that cooking delicious meals is a beautiful form of art.
Predictable
behaving or occurring in a way that is expected.
Machine-generated plots in books are often very predictable.
Vulnerability
state of being exposed to the possibility of being attacked or harmed.
Embracing vulnerability is a far more courageous path than pretending to possess absolute strength.
Automate
to make a process operate automatically by using computers or machines.
You can automate many repetitive tasks in an office easily.
Authenticity
the quality of being authentic, genuine, or true.
Listeners appreciate the raw authenticity of her early vocal recordings.
Formulaic
produced according to a formula or standard recipe; unoriginal.
The blockbuster movie felt highly formulaic and uninspiring.
Imperfection
a fault, blemish, or undesirable feature.
A tiny imperfection in a handmade vase reveals its human origin.

🟢 Round 2 — The Future: Agree or Disagree?▲
Debate
Debate these predictions about algorithms, AI, and human culture.
In the future, a highly advanced algorithm will predict the success of every piece of art before it is created. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you rely on a recommendation algorithm if it could perfectly predict your mood?
We will develop a completely opposite appreciation for machine perfection, valuing error and human limits much more. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think perfect design is less appealing than slightly flawed work?
To protect the purity of human art, future societies will require a license for anyone using generative software. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think digital artwork should be clearly labeled as AI-generated?
A future world fully run by safety rules will leave no room for the necessary risk of creative experimentation. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ What is the most creative choice you have made that felt risky at the time?
Entertainment will become so highly predictable that people will pay to experience completely random, chaotic events. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you enjoy attending a concert where you don't know the genre or artist beforehand?
Admitting our human vulnerability will be the only way to distinguish ourselves from completely resilient machines. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Is showing emotional vulnerability easy or hard for you in your professional life?
The complete pressure to automate labor will eventually free all humans to pursue pure, non-commercial hobbies. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you never had to work for money, how would you spend your days?
A person's raw authenticity will become their most valued social trait in a world filled with digital filters. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you feel you can be fully authentic on your personal social media profiles?
We will see a total decline in mainstream culture because most content will be produced using formulaic recipes. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you prefer popular mainstream media or niche independent creations?
A world governed completely by logic will have no appreciation left for the unique beauty of imperfection. — Do you agree or disagree?
★ What minor flaw in yourself or your surroundings do you actually find beautiful?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The AI can to make art→AI can make art(Can + infinitive without 'to', omit 'the' for general technology)
I am agree with Madonna→I agree with Madonna(Agree is a verb)
It depend of your preference→It depends on your preference(Depends + on, singular verb s)
