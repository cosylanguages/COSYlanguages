📜 Read Column Transcript▼

My neighbor was outside, meticulously polishing a heavy brass antique under the grey Breton sky—an heirloom she has confessed to detesting. It made me smile. In a world where we fear romantic commitment and constantly swipe for the next best option, why do we pledge such unwavering, monogamous loyalty to old traditions? I watched the smoke of my Marlboro Red dissipate into the damp air and couldn't help but wonder... Are these cultural rituals a comforting embrace, or are they just a hidden monopoly on our choices, forcing us to sleep with history while neglecting our own future?

📖 Session Vocabulary

Monogamy
the practice or state of having a relationship with only one partner at a time.
She wondered if her unwavering loyalty to old family customs was a form of cultural monogamy.
Monopoly
exclusive possession or control of the supply of or trade in a commodity or service.
The traditional wedding industry holds a near monopoly on how we define romantic success.
Devotion
love, loyalty, or enthusiasm for a person, activity, or cause.
His absolute devotion to the family reunion was both touching and slightly exhausting.
Heirloom
a valuable object that has belonged to a family for several generations.
The heavy gold watch was a precious family heirloom that carried decades of silent expectations.
Unwavering
steady or resolute; not wavering or showing hesitation.
Despite their differences, the community maintained an unwavering support for the annual harvest festival.
Arbitrary
based on random choice or personal whim, rather than any reason or system.
Many of our most beloved holidays are based on completely arbitrary calendar dates.
Preserve
to maintain something in its original or existing state.
Older generations fight desperately to preserve customs that younger people find irrelevant.
Anachronistic
belonging or appropriate to a period other than that in which it exists.
In our digital age, writing physical letters can feel delightfully anachronistic.
Reverence
deep respect for someone or something.
She treated her grandmother's recipes with a level of reverence usually reserved for sacred texts.
Customary
according to the customs or usual practices associated with a particular society.
It is customary to bring a small gift when visiting a host in traditional communities.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am agree with keeping old heirlooms. → I agree with keeping old heirlooms. (Verb usage: 'agree' is a verb, not an adjective, so do not use 'am'.)
Not only we follow traditions but also we love them. → Not only do we follow traditions, but we also love them. (Inversion rule: starting a sentence with 'not only' requires subject-auxiliary inversion.)
Traditions are passed down since centuries. → Traditions have been passed down for centuries. (Preposition choice: use 'for' to indicate the duration of an action, and use present perfect passive.)
