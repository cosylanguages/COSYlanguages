📜 Read Column Transcript▼

As a linguist, I spend my days dissecting grammar, vowels, and syntax. Recently, over a glass of dry red wine in Saint-Malo, I listened to a grand debate about inclusive French grammar—the endless battle of 'tous et toutes' and median periods. While sipping my own version of the drink from Gods, I couldn't help but wonder... We spend so much energy polishing our syllables, hoping to cure centuries of disparity with a suffix. But does rearranging our grammar genuinely foster equality, or is it merely an elegant distraction from the stubborn, cold realities of inequality?

make changes in something, typically a social, political, or economic institution.

Syllable
a unit of pronunciation having one vowel sound, with or without surrounding consonants.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We discussed about inclusive language. → We discussed inclusive language. ('Discuss' is a transitive verb in English. Do not use 'about' after it)
Language changes can make people to feel uncomfortable. → Language changes can make people feel uncomfortable. (The causative verb 'make' is followed by a bare infinitive without 'to')
She explained why linguistics are important. → She explained why linguistics is important. (The noun 'linguistics' is singular in English, despite ending in 's')

👥 Unit 1: UNDERSTAND — Vocabulary Catalyst

Let's anchor our discussion. Before we debate, review these 4 key conceptual terms. Double-click any unfamiliar word on the page to save it to your personal dictionary!

Linguistic reform
Deliberate change to the grammar, spelling, or usage of a language, often for social or political equality.
Linguistic reform can trigger fierce debates among grammarians and the public alike.
Inclusivity
The practice or policy of providing equal access, representation, and opportunities to all individuals.
Many argue that changing pronouns is a simple but vital step toward real-world inclusivity.
Tokenism
Making only a symbolic or superficial gesture to appear inclusive, without making genuine structural changes.
Critics claim that companies using gender-neutral words without promoting women is mere tokenism.
Disparity
A great difference, especially one of unfair or unequal treatment in society or economics.
Despite inclusive greetings, the gender wage disparity in executive positions remains high.

👥 Unit 3: EXPLORE — The French vs. English Debate

🔎 Linguistic Contrast

While English easily adopted the singular "they", French has faced intense resistance over gender-inclusive punctuation (like "tous·te·s") from the French Academy. Why is grammatical change so threatening to some cultures, yet natural to others?

The Academic Gatekeepers

Some believe strict preservation of grammar is vital to preserve cultural heritage and literature.

The Fluid Evolution

Others argue language is a living tool that must adapt to represent the changing identities of its speakers.

🗣️ Interactive Discussion Point:

Is language a reflection of society, or is it a tool that actively shapes society? If we change the words, do the hearts and minds follow, or are we simply rewriting the script of an old play?
