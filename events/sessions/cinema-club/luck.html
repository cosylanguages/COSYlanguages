Welcome to our specialized Cinema Club session. This session is designed to explore the sweet story of Luck. Learn simple words to talk about luck, families, and optimism. In this session, we practice Adverbs of Frequency & Probability to talk about your daily routines.

🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Superstition
belief in good or bad luck symbols.
Walking under a ladder is a common superstition.
Optimism
hoping for good things in the future.
Sam always shows optimism when she faces problems.
Family
a group of people who love each other.
Sam wants to find a warm family.
Sad days
unhappy or difficult periods.
She had some sad days in her old house.
Happy days
joyful or pleasant periods.
Sam hopes for happy days with her friends.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

Bad luck
unfortunate events that happen by chance.
Sam thinks she has bad luck every morning.
good vibe
a friendly and positive feeling.
The Land of Luck has a very good vibe.
get rich
to earn or get a lot of money.
The leprechauns do not care to get rich.
clover
a small green plant, sometimes with four leaves.
Finding a four-leaf clover brings good luck.
Movie
a story shown in moving pictures.
I love watching this funny cartoon movie.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I will resign if they skimp always on budget→I will resign if they always skimp on budgetFrequency adverbs like 'always' come before the main verb.
We are living here since two years→We have been living here for two yearsUse present perfect continuous with 'for' to express duration.
They don't allow her leave the house→They don't allow her to leave the houseUse 'allow someone to do something' construction.
