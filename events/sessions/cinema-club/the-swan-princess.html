Welcome to our specialized Cinema Club session. This session is designed to explore the sweet story of The Swan Princess. Learn simple words to talk about friends, love, and fairy tales. In this session, we practice Comparatives and Superlatives to talk about your favorite characters and places.

🎬 Theme Snapshot: The Swan Princess

In this session, we will discuss:

Protagonist Princess Odette and Prince Derek
Setting a beautiful fairy tale kingdom and a quiet lake
Core Conflict breaking a magic spell with true love
Grammar Focus Comparatives and Superlatives

🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Friend
a person you know well and like.
Odette has a very good animal friend.
Love
a strong feeling of liking someone or something.
Derek shows true love to Princess Odette.
Lake
a large area of water with land all around it.
The princess lives on a quiet lake.
Memory
the ability to remember things.
She has a sweet memory of her old home.
Promise
to say that you will definitely do something.
He made a promise to find her.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

No offense
a phrase used to say you do not want to upset someone.
No offense, but I do not like that frog.
Enemy
a person who hates or wants to harm another.
Rothbart is the main enemy in the story.
Show
to let someone see something.
I want to show you my beautiful home.
Wicked
very bad or evil.
The wicked wizard cast a bad spell.
Story
a description of imaginary people and events.
I love reading this happy story.

Screenplay Discussion

★ What is the sweetest thing about love?
Is the swan lake more beautiful than other places?
★ Do you like visiting a lake?
What is your favorite childhood memory from movies?
★ Do you remember your first film?
Why is a promise important to Prince Derek?
★ Do you always keep your promise?
When Derek says 'no offense', is he rude?
★ Do you say 'no offense' often?
Who is the worst enemy in this story?
★ Do you like bad guys in cartoons?
Can animals show smart ideas to help the princess?
★ Does your pet show smart tricks?
Is the wicked wizard the scariest person?
★ What makes a character look wicked?
Do you like this story more than other stories?
★ What is your favorite animated story?

🎬 Final Challenge — You're the Director!▲

You are the director! Change one thing in the movie:

1. The Setting 🌲 Move the story to a modern city. What changes?
2. The Ending 🔚 Make a different ending. How does it end?

Speaking Assignment: Pitch your new version to the class. Use comparative adjectives like "more exciting" or "the best"!

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I have seen that movie yesterday→I saw that movie yesterdayUse the simple past for actions completed in the past.
She is more older than him→She is older than himDo not double comparatives; 'older' is already comparative.
He gave to her a beautiful book→He gave her a beautiful bookDirect object doesn't require 'to' after 'give' in English.
