🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Desert
a large, dry area of land with sandy soil.
The woman travels across a beautiful desert.
Destiny
the things that will happen to you in the future.
The movie shows how love is her true destiny.
Friendship
a relationship between friends.
True friendship helps people overcome difficult times.
Time
the system of hours, minutes, and seconds.
The clock shows how time changes in the story.
Dreams
pictures or thoughts in your mind when you sleep.
The movie is filled with beautiful, strange dreams.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

Surreal
very strange, like something from a dream.
The flying stone statues look very surreal.
Art
beautiful things made by drawing, painting, or sculpting.
I love studying modern art in this cartoon.
Loop
a shape like a curve that starts and ends at the same place.
The woman dances inside a giant loop.
Story
a description of people and events.
This is a sweet, silent love story.
Show
to let someone see something clearly.
The director wants to show beautiful ideas.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
She is more older than him→She is older than himDo not double comparatives.
He gave to her a beautiful book→He gave her a beautiful bookDirect object doesn't require 'to' after 'give'.
I seen that movie yesterday→I saw that movie yesterdayUse 'saw' for simple past.
