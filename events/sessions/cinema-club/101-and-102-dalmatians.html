Welcome to our specialized Cinema Club session. This session is designed to explore the sweet story of 101 & 102 Dalmatians. Learn simple words to talk about dogs, pets, and clothes. In this session, we practice Demonstrative Determiners & Possessives to talk about your pets.

🎬 Theme Snapshot: 101 & 102 Dalmatians

In this session, we will discuss:

Protagonist the dalmatian puppies
Setting London streets and beautiful homes
Core Conflict saving the cute puppies from Cruella
Grammar Focus Demonstrative Determiners & Possessives

🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Puppies
very young dogs.
The dalmatian has fifteen cute puppies.
Pets
animals that live in your home.
Dogs and cats are very popular pets.
Bad person
someone who is not nice and harms others.
Cruella is a very bad person in this story.
Home
the place where you live.
The puppies love their warm home.
Friend
a person or animal you like.
Every puppy needs a good friend.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

Spots
small round marks of a different color.
Dalmatians have beautiful black spots on their fur.
Love
a strong feeling of liking someone.
The owners show great love to their dogs.
Funny
making you laugh.
The parrot in the movie is very funny.
Fur coat
a warm coat made of animal hair.
Cruella wants to buy a big fur coat.
Clothes
things you wear, like shirts and pants.
The fashion designer makes beautiful clothes.

🎥 Film Analysis Structure

🟠 Screen Hook (Warm-up)▲
  • Do you like dogs? Why?
  • How many puppies do you see in the trailer?
🔵 Part 1 — General Discussion (10 Items)▲
Screenplay Discussion
Are these puppies cuter than normal dogs?
★ Do you want to have puppies at home?
Why do people love keeping pets?
★ What is your favorite pet animal?
Why is Cruella such a bad person?
★ Do you like bad guys in comedies?
Is this warm home safe for the puppies?
★ Is your home big or small?
Who is the best animal friend in this film?
★ Do you have a pet friend?
Why do dalmatians have black spots on their fur?
★ Do you like black and white colors?
How does Derek show love to his dogs?
★ How do you show love to pets?
Which scene in the movie is the most funny?
★ What cartoon makes you laugh most?
Why does Cruella want a new fur coat?
★ Do you have a warm coat?
Does the designer make nice clothes?
★ What clothes do you like to wear?

🎬 Final Challenge — You're the Director!▲

Speaking Assignment: Pitch your new version to the class. Use demonstrative determiners like "this puppy" or "these dogs"!

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
This dogs are very cute→These dogs are very cute / This dog is very cuteUse plural determiner 'these' with plural noun.
The puppy has spots on its body→The puppy has spots on its bodyThis is already correct.
Look at that puppies over there→Look at those puppies over thereUse plural determiner 'those' with plural noun.
