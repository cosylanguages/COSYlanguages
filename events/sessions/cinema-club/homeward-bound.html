Welcome to our specialized Cinema Club session. This session is designed to explore the sweet story of Homeward Bound. Learn simple words to talk about pets, family, and survival in the wild. In this session, we practice Modal Verbs of Ability & Possibility to talk about what pets can do.

🎬 Theme Snapshot: Homeward Bound

In this session, we will discuss:

Protagonist Shadow, Chance, and Sassy
Setting the wild forest and mountains
Core Conflict traveling across the forest to find their family
Grammar Focus Modal Verbs of Ability & Possibility

🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Pet
an animal that you keep in your home.
Chance is a very playful pet dog.
Family
a group of people who are related.
The pets walk far to find their family.
Find
to discover where something is.
Can they find the right way home?
Survival
continuing to live in a difficult situation.
The forest is dangerous, but survival is possible.
Sad
unhappy or showing sorrow.
The children are sad when their pets leave.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

Buddy
a close friend.
He is my favorite buddy in this game.
Wild
a natural state, away from towns and homes.
Wild animals can be dangerous at night.
Dog
a very common domestic animal with four legs.
Shadow is a very smart old dog.
Pack
a group of animals that live and hunt together.
These three pets act like a small pack.
Silent
without making any sound.
The cat is silent when she hunts.

🎥 Film Analysis Structure

🟠 Screen Hook (Warm-up)▲
  • Have you ever traveled to the mountains?
  • What can a smart dog do to help you?
🔵 Part 1 — General Discussion (10 Items)▲
Screenplay Discussion
Can a loyal pet find the way back home?
★ What can your pet do when you are away?
How can a strong family bond help the pets?
★ Can you call your family when you travel?
How can they find water in the wild mountains?
★ Is it easy for you to find new places?
Can their smart decisions help in forest survival?
★ What can you do to survive in the forest?
Are the children sad when their pets are gone?
★ Do you feel sad when you lose things?
Can Chance help his buddy Shadow in danger?
★ Can you help your buddy with homework?
What dangerous animals live in the wild forest?
★ Have you ever seen a wild animal?
Can a smart dog run faster than a cat?
★ Do you prefer a dog or a cat?
Can this small pack fight a big mountain lion?
★ Do you like working in a group?
Why must Sassy the cat be silent when hunting?
★ Can you be silent for ten minutes?

🎬 Final Challenge — You're the Director!▲

You are the director! Change one thing in the movie:

1. The Setting 🌲 Move the story to a big city. What changes?
2. The Ending 🔚 Make a different ending. How does it end?

Speaking Assignment: Pitch your new version to the class. Use modal verbs like "can", "can't", "could", "couldn't" or "might"!

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The dog can to run fast→The dog can run fastDo not use 'to' after modal verb 'can'.
She is more older than him→She is older than himDo not double comparatives.
I will resign if they skimp always on budget→I will resign if they always skimp on budgetFrequency adverbs come before main verbs.
