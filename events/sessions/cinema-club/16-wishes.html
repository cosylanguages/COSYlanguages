🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

Desires
strong feelings of wanting something.
Abby has many desires on her special list.
Result
something that happens because of an action.
The bad result of the wish made her sad.
Growing up
becoming an adult.
Abby learns a lot about growing up on her birthday.
Friend
a person you like and spend time with.
Connor is Abby's best childhood friend.
Home
the house where you live.
She wants to change her home with a wish.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

Epic
very great, grand, or exciting.
She wants to have an epic sixteenth birthday party.
bday
short word for birthday.
Abby is excited about her big bday today.
make a wish
to hope for something good to happen.
Blow out the candles and make a wish!
glitch
a small problem or error that stops something working.
There is a magic glitch in her plan.
Secrets
things that are kept hidden from others.
The magic boxes hold many special secrets.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I wish I have a million dollars→I wish I had a million dollarsUse simple past for present wishes after 'wish'.
She is more older than him→She is older than himDo not double comparatives.
He gave to her a beautiful book→He gave her a beautiful bookDirect object doesn't require 'to' after 'give'.
