❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

thinking
Target vocabulary word meaning in 'Eleven O'Clock'.
This is an elegant example of using 'thinking'.
tonight
on the present or coming night.
We are going to attend a live music concert tonight.
Opposite: today
Dreaming
indulging in daydreams or fantasies.
He was dreaming of a warm sunny beach in California.
Opposite: Waking
Personality
the combination of characteristics that form character.
She has a bright and outgoing personality.
Opposite: Anonymity
Aftermath
the consequences or aftereffects of a significant event.
They worked hard to rebuild the town in the aftermath of the storm.
Opposite: Preparation

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Disconnect
an instance of breaking contact or connection.
There was a temporary disconnect in our phone line.
Opposite: Connect
true
in accordance with fact or reality; loyal and faithful.
A true friend will always support you through difficult times.
person
the combination of characteristics that form character.
She has a bright and outgoing personality.
Covina
a city in California, used here as a symbol of suburban life.
He spent his childhood years living in the quiet suburbs of Covina.
Opposite: Metropolis
crying
shedding tears, especially as an expression of distress or pain.
The crying child was quickly comforted by her mother.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
