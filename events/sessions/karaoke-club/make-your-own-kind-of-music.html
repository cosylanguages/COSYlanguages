📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Be yourself
to act naturally according to your own character.
It is always important to be yourself at school.
Late
after the expected or usual time.
He was late for the special music class.
Melody
a sweet tune or sequence of musical notes.
I love the soft melody of this song.
Choir
an organized group of singers.
The school choir sings beautiful songs.
Chorus
the part of a song that is repeated.
Everyone sang the chorus together loudly.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Solo
done by one person alone.
She sang a beautiful solo on the stage.
Happy
feeling or showing pleasure or contentment.
This cheerful song makes everyone feel happy.
Friend
a person you know and like well.
A good friend always listens to your ideas.
Path
a way or track for walking.
We walked along a quiet path in the forest.
Harmony
the combination of notes sung together beautifully.
They sang in perfect harmony at the concert.

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

Is it always easy to be yourself?
★ When do you find it hard to be yourself?
Are you ever late for your music lessons?
★ What do you do when a friend is late?
Do you like the sweet melody of this song?
★ What is your favorite happy melody?
Do you want to sing in a big choir?
★ Have you ever sung in a school choir?
Can we sing the chorus together?
★ What is the easiest chorus to remember?
Is singing a solo scarier than singing with others?
★ Do you like singing alone?
Does this song make you feel happy?
★ What music makes you happy?
Is a loyal friend better than a rich friend?
★ Who is your best friend?
Should we walk on a quiet path in spring?
★ Do you like walking in the park?

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Direct Imperatives

In the song Make Your Own Kind Of Music, the lyric "Make your own kind of music... sing" shows the use of Direct Imperatives. Use this to encourage your friends.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:
  • Make your own choices every day!
  • Sing your favorite song tonight!
  • Be happy with what you have!
🎭 Stage 6: Final Challenge▲
🎭

Work in pairs. Talk about what kind of music you love most and why.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement'.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration.)
