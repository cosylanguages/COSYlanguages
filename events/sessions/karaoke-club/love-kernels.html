❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Kernels
small, soft, or edible parts of a nut, seed, or corn; metaphorically, tiny bits.
He only threw her a few kernels of affection, keeping her wanting more.
Opposite: Bulk
Delusional
characterized by or holding idiosyncratic beliefs or impressions that are contradicted by reality.
He was delusional to think he could win without practicing at all.
Opposite: Realistic
Obsession
an unhealthy preoccupation with someone or something.
His obsession with cleanliness became a bit extreme.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
