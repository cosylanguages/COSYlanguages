📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Brown
of a color produced by mixing red, yellow, and black.
The autumn leaves turned a beautiful shade of brown.
Opposite: Green
ready
fully prepared or in a fit state for an activity or action.
After weeks of hard work, they were finally ready to present their project.
beautiful
pleasing the senses or mind aesthetically.
The sunset over the mountain was one of the most beautiful sights she had ever seen.
alone
having no anyone else present; on one's own.
She preferred to walk alone in the quiet forest to clear her mind.
lonely
sad because one has no friends or company.
She felt a bit lonely living in the giant, unfamiliar city.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

find
discover or perceive by chance or after deliberate search.
It takes time to find a career path that truly inspires you.
ring
a small circular band, typically of precious metal, worn on a finger.
He gave her a beautiful silver ring as a symbol of their friendship.
love
an intense feeling of deep affection.
Their shared love for classical music brought them together.
day
a period of twenty-four hours, or the time of light.
It was a beautiful day to spend outdoors in the countryside.
Loneliest
Thematic vocabulary word from 'Make Your Own Kind Of Music'.
This is an elegant example of using 'Loneliest'.

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Past Simple vs. Present Location Questions

In the song Where Is My Husband, the repetitive lyric chunk "Where is my husband? He went to..." highlights the use of Past Simple vs. Present Location Questions. This structure supports: Escalating comical, desperate inquiries.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
