📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Maternal
relating to a mother, especially during pregnancy or shortly after childbirth.
She felt a strong maternal instinct to protect her young children.
Opposite: Paternal
harrowing
acutely distressing or painful.
Rescuing the lost hikers from the steep mountain was a harrowing experience.
matriarch
a woman who is the head of a family or tribe.
The grandmother was the respected matriarch of the large family.
expectations
Thematic vocabulary word from 'Settle For Me'.
This is an elegant example of using 'Expectations'.
instinctual
relating to or of the nature of instinct.
The mother dog showed an instinctual drive to protect her new puppies.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

tireless
having or showing great effort or energy without tiring.
She was a tireless campaigner for clean energy and environmental protection.
Warm
having or showing a moderate degree of heat.
A warm bowl of soup is perfect for a cold day.
Opposite: Cold
Safe
protected from or not exposed to danger or risk.
Keep your important documents in a safe place.
Opposite: Dangerous
Expectation
Thematic vocabulary word from 'Settle For Me'.
This is an elegant example of using 'Expectations'.
Opposite: Surprise
apartment
a suite of rooms forming one residence, typically in a building.
They recently moved into a spacious apartment on the fifth floor.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
