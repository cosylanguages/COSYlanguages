❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Disguise
give a new appearance to hide identity.
She wore a wig as a clever disguise.
Opposite: Reveal
right
morally good, justified, or acceptable; correct.
She did the right thing by telling the truth to her friend.
Opposite: wrong
game
an activity that one engages in for amusement, strategy, or fun.
Chess is a classic game of skill, patience, and deep foresight.
lonely
sad because one has no friends or company.
She felt a bit lonely living in the giant, unfamiliar city.
find
discover or perceive by chance or after deliberate search.
It takes time to find a career path that truly inspires you.
