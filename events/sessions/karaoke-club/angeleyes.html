📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Hypnotised
having been put into a state of hypnosis; highly captivated.
She sat completely hypnotised by the beautiful piano performance.
Opposite: Indifferent
Paradise
an ideal or idyllic place or state; heaven.
The tropical island felt like a peaceful paradise on earth.
Opposite: Hell
Disguise
give a new appearance to hide identity.
She wore a wig as a clever disguise.
Opposite: Reveal
Shiver
shake slightly and uncontrollably as a result of being cold, frightened, or excited.
The cold winter wind made her shiver despite her warm coat.
Opposite: Sultry-glow
Game
an activity or sport played according to rules, or a deceptive trick.
He realized his friend was playing a manipulative game with his feelings.
Opposite: Reality

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Pay
give someone money that is due for work done, or suffer the consequences of an action.
If you treat people badly, you will eventually have to pay the price.
Opposite: Default
Lonely
sad because one has no friends or company.
She felt very lonely during her first week living in the big city.
Opposite: Sociable
Pain
highly distressing physical or emotional sensation.
Time eventually heals the deep pain of a broken heart.
Opposite: Comfort
Disappointment
sadness or displeasure caused by the nonfulfillment of one's hopes or expectations.
Her failing grade was a major disappointment to her parents.
Opposite: Fulfillment
Gaze
look steadily and intently.
He would gaze at the stars for hours in the backyard.
Opposite: Glance

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

The chorus warns: "Look into his angel eyes / One look and you're hypnotised." Why is charm and charisma such a powerful tool, and why does it make us ignore potential red flags when we are Hypnotised by someone?
★ Have you ever met someone whose sheer charisma completely Hypnotised or captivated you at first?
He makes his victims feel like they are in "Paradise" before breaking their hearts. Why is the very beginning of a romance or deep infatuation often so idyllic and flawless?
★ What is your personal idea of a peaceful Paradise when you need to escape from life's daily stresses?
The song warns: "And one day, you'll find out he wears a disguise." How can we distinguish between a person's authentic character and a carefully crafted social Disguise?
★ Have you ever felt the need to wear a protective social Disguise in an unfamiliar professional environment?
The singer recounts seeing him with a new girl: "And the look that he gave her made me shiver." Why do physical reactions like a Shiver or goosebumps carry such accurate emotional intuition?
★ Tell us about a time a sudden gut feeling or a cold Shiver warned you about a person or situation.
The protagonist realizes too late that "it's a Game he likes to play." Why do some individuals treat human feelings as a game or a source of control?
★ What is your favorite board Game or activity to play with friends on a relaxed evening?
The singer warns: "He took my heart and now I pay the price." What is the emotional cost of trusting too quickly, and how do we learn to Pay that price?
★ Do you prefer to Pay with mobile apps, cards, or traditional cash when buying small items?
The verse describes the painful aftermath: "Sometimes when I'm lonely, I sit and think about him." Why does being Lonely make us romanticize toxic past relationships?
★ What is your favorite self-care activity to keep from feeling Lonely when spending a weekend alone?
She wonders: "Every time when I see him, will it bring back all the pain?" How does unresolved emotional Pain affect our ability to trust new people?
★ What is a great technique or exercise that helps you release muscle tension or physical Pain after a stressful day?
Learning of his betrayal was a major Disappointment. How can we turn such painful disappointments into positive lessons for personal growth?
★ What minor Disappointment (like a canceled flight or a bad movie) did you have to laugh off recently?
She cautions: "Don't look too deep into those angel eyes." What does a person's eye contact or steady Gaze reveal about their true, unmasked intentions?
★ Do you find it natural or difficult to maintain a steady Gaze during intense conversations?
🎵 Interactive Musical Checkpoints (Karaoke Identity)
  • Before Listening: Predict based on the title 'Angeleyes': Do you expect a slow, sad, acoustic ballad, or a bright, energetic, and highly harmonized dance track?
  • After Verse 1 (Pause & Predict): Pause after 'it's a game he likes to play'. Why does she want to warn the other young girl? Should she actually walk up and say it?
  • During the Chorus (The Warning): Listen to how the upbeat, happy 'Ah-ha-ha' backup vocals contrast with the dark warning 'you must pay the price'. Why did ABBA structure the song this way?
  • After Verse 2 (The Relapse of Memory): She says 'it hurts to remember all the good times'. Why are happy memories often more painful than bad ones after a breakup?
  • After Singing/Listening (Lyrical Takeaway): Which lyric line or warning about deception will stay with you tomorrow?

🟢 Round 2 — Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

Physical attractiveness and surface charm (like 'angel eyes') are the most deceptive human traits, and we should evaluate potential partners solely based on prolonged actions. Debate.
Warning a stranger about a manipulative partner is an act of social solidarity, even if they are currently too infatuated to believe you. Do you agree?
The pain of romantic betrayal, while devastating, is a necessary and invaluable life lesson that significantly increases emotional resilience. Discuss.

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Relative Clauses with 'Who', 'Which', and 'That'

Relative clauses give us more information about a person or thing. In the song, the singer warns about a man 'who' wears a disguise and uses games 'that' he likes to play.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:
  • The look that he gave her made me shiver with disappointment.
  • Don't look too deep into the angel eyes of a guy who wears a clever disguise.
  • She remembered all the good times which eventually brought her so much pain.
