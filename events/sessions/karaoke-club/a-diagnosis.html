❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

diagnosis
the identification of the nature of an illness or other problem by examination of the symptoms.
The doctor provided a clear diagnosis after reviewing the test results.
mental
Target vocabulary word meaning in 'A Diagnosis'.
This is an elegant example of using 'mental'.
right
morally good, justified, or acceptable; correct.
She did the right thing by telling the truth to her friend.
Opposite: wrong
wrong
not correct or true; incorrect.
He gave the wrong answer to the simple physics question.
Regret
feel sad, repentant, or disappointed over.
He expressed deep regret for his past mistakes.
Opposite: Satisfaction

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Obsession
an unhealthy preoccupation with someone or something.
His obsession with cleanliness became a bit extreme.
Opposite: Indifference
Confusion
lack of understanding; uncertainty.
The sudden change in schedule caused a lot of confusion.
Opposite: Clarity
Anxiety
a feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease.
He felt a bit of anxiety before his big presentation.
Opposite: Calmness
ready
fully prepared or in a fit state for an activity or action.
After weeks of hard work, they were finally ready to present their project.
find
discover or perceive by chance or after deliberate search.
It takes time to find a career path that truly inspires you.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
