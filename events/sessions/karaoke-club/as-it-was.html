📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Change
To make or become different.
Moving to a new city can completely change your lifestyle and routines.
Opposite: Stability
Isolation
the state of being separate from others.
The remote cabin offered a perfect sense of isolation.
Opposite: Integration
Nostalgia vs. Modernity
A sentimental longing for the past contrasted with contemporary or modern times.
The singer deals with a conflict between nostalgia for his old life and the pressure of modernity.
Suburban
relating to or characteristic of a suburb.
They lived in a quiet, peaceful suburban neighborhood.
Opposite: Urban
Gravity
the force that attracts a body toward the earth.
Gravity keeps our feet firmly planted on the ground.
Opposite: Levity

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Disconnect
an instance of breaking contact or connection.
There was a temporary disconnect in our phone line.
Opposite: Connect
Melancholy
a feeling of pensive sadness.
A gentle melancholy settled over the autumn garden.
Opposite: Cheerfulness
Expectation
a strong belief that something will happen.
They met every high expectation of their teachers.
Opposite: Surprise
Routine
a sequence of actions regularly followed.
Establishing a morning routine can boost your productivity.
Opposite: Novelty
Reflection
the throwing back of light, heat, or sound.
The quiet lake offered a beautiful reflection of the trees.
Opposite: Absorption

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

The chorus repeats: "In this world, it's just us / You know it's not the same as it was." How do sudden, major life transitions or Change force us to re-evaluate who we are?
★ When was the last time you felt a strong sense of sudden Change in your own life?
The verse paints a picture of being alone: "Answer the phone / Harry, you're no good alone / Why are you sitting at home on the floor?" Is temporary Isolation helpful for healing, or does it make emotional recovery harder?
★ Do you find it easy or difficult to share your feelings of Isolation with others?
The lyrics contrast the past with modern speed: "I don't wanna talk about the way that it was." How does Nostalgia vs. Modernity create a constant mental conflict when we look at our childhood memories?
★ How has your personal understanding of Nostalgia vs. Modernity changed over the past few years?
The song references quiet, static family settings: "Your daddy lives by himself." How does a peaceful, slow Suburban lifestyle contrast with the fast-paced, high-pressure world of modern careers?
★ In your daily routine, what helps you maintain your focus on Suburban tranquility versus city chaos?
In the intro, the singer says: "Holdin' me back / Gravity's holdin' me back." In a metaphorical sense, what are the heavy obligations or memories that act like Gravity, pulling you back from moving forward in life?
★ What is the most important lesson you have learned about breaking free from the heavy Gravity of past mistakes?
The singer describes a breakdown of communication: "Nothin' to say / When everything gets in the way." Why do modern busy lifestyles cause a sudden Disconnect between close friends and partners?
★ Do you believe that modern society values Disconnect and independence too much, or is it often ignored?
The song couples a highly upbeat, danceable synth-pop rhythm with deeply sad, melancholic lyrics. Why does the artistic contrast of Melancholy themes and cheerful music feel so relatable to listeners?
★ Who is the person in your life that best represents the idea of managing their Melancholy with a positive smile?
The lyrics touch on the pressure from family and friends: "He just wants to know that you're well." How do you cope with the heavy weight of Expectation that family members place on your life?
★ If you could gift more freedom and less Expectation to someone you care about, who would it be?
The verse details static home habits: "sitting at home on the floor." Why is breaking a comfortable but unhealthy daily Routine one of the most difficult parts of moving on?
★ Do you think that establishing a daily Routine is more about personal peace or shared experiences?
The bridge notes light-speed internet and avoiding the past: "I don't wanna talk about who's doin' it first." Why is quiet Reflection more valuable for personal healing than constant social comparison?
★ What small practice or habit helps you cultivate deep Reflection in your busy life?

🟢 Round 2 — Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

The melancholic contrast between cheerful, upbeat music and sad, lonely lyrics is the most honest way to express the complexity of modern life. Debate.
The nostalgia we feel for 'the way that it was' is usually an illusion; we only remember the positive moments while forgetting the struggles of the past. Discuss.
In a hyper-connected world of light-speed internet, true isolation is no longer about physical distance, but about a deep emotional disconnect from those around us. Agree or disagree?
✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Comparative *As... as* with Past Habits

In the song As It Was, the repetitive lyric chunk "You know it's not the same as it was" highlights the use of Comparative *As... as* with Past Habits. This structure supports: Analyzing life changes and temporal transitions.

Let's Practise — Three Examples:

  • Example 2: Notice how this pattern is repeated to build rhythmic familiarity.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
