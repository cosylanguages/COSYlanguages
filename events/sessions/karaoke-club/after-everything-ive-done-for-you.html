📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

done
finished or completed.
Once the research is done, we can begin drafting the final report.
face
the front part of a person's head, or to confront.
We must face our greatest challenges with courage and determination.
right
morally good, justified, or acceptable; correct.
She did the right thing by telling the truth to her friend.
Opposite: wrong
work
activity involving mental or physical effort done in order to achieve a purpose or result.
All that hard work finally paid off with a promotion.
Aftermath
the consequences or aftereffects of a significant event.
They worked hard to rebuild the town in the aftermath of the storm.
Opposite: Preparation

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

friendly
kind and pleasant; behaving in a helpful or supporting way.
The new neighbors gave us a very warm and friendly welcome.
lies
false statements made with the intent to deceive.
Basing a friendship on lies will eventually lead to disappointment.
dirty
not clean; covered or marked with an unclean substance.
The dirty dishes were piled high in the kitchen sink.
love
an intense feeling of deep affection.
Their shared love for classical music brought them together.
everything
all things, or all things of a particular class.
They lost everything in the fire but were grateful to be safe.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
