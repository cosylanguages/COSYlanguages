❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

crowded
full of people, leaving little or no room.
The popular beach was very crowded on the warm summer weekend.
Opposite: empty
politician
a person who is professionally involved in politics, especially as a holder of an elected office.
The local politician promised to improve public parks and libraries.
smiling
having a smile on one's face, showing pleasure or affection.
The smiling teacher greeted her new class with immense enthusiasm.
place
a particular position, point, or area.
The quiet park near her house is her favorite place to read books.
everything
all things, or all things of a particular class.
They lost everything in the fire but were grateful to be safe.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)
