💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

The singer tells us to be yourself even if others are different. Why is this important?
★ Is it easy to be yourself around strangers?
You must sing your song even if it is late to start. Is it ever too late to change?
★ Are you usually late for meetings?
A sweet melody can lift our spirits. What makes a melody beautiful?
★ What is your favorite song melody?
The singer says you don't need to join the choir. Do you like singing in a group?
★ Have you ever sung in a school choir?
A catchy chorus is the heart of a pop song. Why are choruses so easy to remember?
★ Can you hum the chorus of your favorite song?
Singing a solo requires a lot of courage. Would you sing a solo on stage?
★ Do you prefer working alone or doing a solo project?
The track brings a very happy and positive vibe. What makes a song feel happy?
★ What simple thing makes you happy?
A true friend supports your unique choices. What makes a good friend?
★ Who is your oldest friend?
Everyone must find their own path in life. Is your path clear to you?
★ Did you choose your career path early?
Singing in perfect harmony is a beautiful experience. How can we live in harmony?
★ Does your home feel in harmony?

🟢 Round 2 — Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

It is always easy to be yourself when you are with family.
Arriving late is a sign of disrespect in all cultures.
A song must have a great melody to be successful.
Singing in a choir is better than singing alone.
A great chorus is the most important part of music.
Performing a solo is too stressful for most people.
We can choose to be happy every single day.
A dog is a better friend than most humans.
Following a traditional path is safer and happier.
Living in perfect harmony with everyone is impossible.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
There is a big improvement of my English → There is a big improvement in my English (Use 'in' after 'improvement' to express domain.)
It was a great relief of hearing the news → It was a great relief to hear the news (Use 'relief' + infinitive 'to hear'.)
She is in the choir since three years → She has been in the choir for three years (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration of state.)

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1 — Lyric Analysis & Discussion

The song says life is getting better every day. What makes your day better?
★ Are you feeling better than yesterday?
Finding true joy makes us happy. What is your favorite hobby to feel happy?
★ Does sunny weather make you happy?
We all want to live a peaceful life. What does a good life look like?
★ Where do you want to spend your life?
The world is full of beautiful places to see. What part of the world do you love?
★ Do you want to travel around the world?
Every single day brings a fresh start. How do you plan your day?
★ Did you have a busy day today?
Good friends support us during hard times. How do friends help you?
★ Do you have many close friends?
The track describes a deep love for life. Is love the key to happiness?
★ What do you love doing on Sundays?
The warm sun is shining in the sky. Do you like a shining sun?
★ Is the sun shining in your city today?
There is always hope for tomorrow. What are your goals for tomorrow?
★ What will you cook tomorrow?
A friendly smile can make a stranger feel welcome. Do you smile often?
★ Did someone make you smile today?

We should start every day with a short meditation.
It is better to have one true friend than many acquaintance friends.
True love always lasts forever.
A shining sun is the best cure for sadness.
We should focus on today instead of worrying about tomorrow.
A warm smile is the most powerful communication tool.
