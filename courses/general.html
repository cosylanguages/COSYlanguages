General Course 📖

All 4 skills growing together, structured CEFR-level progression.

💬 Message us on WhatsApp See prices

Who it's for

Adults who want to learn a language properly, step by step, alongside a busy life. No specific goal needed, just steady progress.

What you'll do

  • Speak from lesson one.
  • Grow listening, speaking, reading and writing together.
  • Follow a clear path from your level upward.
  • Use situations from your real adult life.

Languages

Available in English (taught since 2020), French (since 2022), Italian (since 2022), Russian (since 2022), and Greek (since 2025).

👥 Group lessons: Available in English, French, Italian, and Russian.

How lessons work

Lessons are conducted 1-to-1 (or in small group cohorts) with a strict speaking-first methodology where at least 40% of every lesson is active speaking.

Durations offered: 60 minutes or 90 minutes.
