🧭 Course Selection Compass: Is a Professional Course Necessary For You?

Confirm whether your professional goals align with our specialized industry tracks:

✅ This course is necessary if...
  • You already possess an intermediate foundation (recommended B1 or higher) and need to operate professionally in an international environment—leading meetings, presenting research, conducting code reviews, negotiating contracts, or managing upscale guest service.
  • You want lessons centered 100% on authentic workplace artifacts: your slides, code commits, academic manuscripts, contracts, or customer escalation emails.
  • You need to master diplomatic tact, professional registers, hedging, and industry jargon without generic textbook filler.
⚠️ Otherwise, choose another course if...
  • Choose the General Course if your general level is currently A1 or A2. Specialized professional terminology requires an established grammatical core first.
  • Choose the Spoken Course if you only want everyday social conversation to maintain fluency rather than formal technical communication.
  • Choose the Exam Preparation Course if your goal is an official certificate (IELTS, TOEFL, DELF, CILS) for a university or visa requirement.
  • Choose the Relocation Course if you need language for setting up daily life (housing, bank accounts, healthcare) in a new country.

🎯 Our 4 Specialized Professional Tracks

Each track has its own distinct syllabus, roleplays, lexical corpus, and communicative benchmarks:

🎓 1. Academic & Research Track Professors, PhDs, Postdocs & Students

Designed for university lecturers, research scholars, master's students, and doctoral candidates who must publish peer-reviewed articles, deliver keynote conference presentations, defend dissertations, and teach seminars in a non-native tongue.

🔬 Target Audience

  • University teachers & professors
  • PhD researchers & postdocs
  • Postgraduate / Master's candidates
  • Academic grant applicants

📚 Thematic Lexicon & Syntax

  • Academic hedging: suggests that, tentatively points to
  • Peer review response templates & rebuttal rhetoric
  • Methodological rigor and empirical citations
  • Nominalization and formal scholarly syntax

⚡ Demands & Prerequisite

  • Entry Level: Minimum CEFR B1+ / B2
  • Bringing original draft abstracts or thesis excerpts
  • Live rehearsal of conference presentations with Q&A

🎯 Key Skills & Capabilities

  • Delivering 20-minute presentations smoothly
  • Handling hostile questions with diplomatic composure
  • Writing crystal-clear scientific abstracts & reviews
💻 2. Information Technology (IT) Track Software Engineers, PMs, DevOps & QA

Engineered for developers, technical project managers, QA engineers, and engineering leads working in distributed, multicultural tech teams across agile and remote environments.

👨‍💻 Target Audience

  • Software developers (Frontend, Backend, Fullstack)
  • Product Managers & Scrum Masters
  • DevOps, Cloud Architects, and SREs
  • Technical Support & QA Engineers

📚 Thematic Lexicon & Syntax

  • Agile rituals: standups, retrospectives, grooming
  • Pull request (PR) reviews: constructive feedback
  • Architecture design documents (RFCs) & trade-offs
  • Incident management: postmortems, RCA, triage

⚡ Demands & Prerequisite

  • Entry Level: Minimum CEFR B1
  • Rehearsing concise status updates under 2 minutes
  • Explaining complex algorithms to non-tech peers

🎯 Key Skills & Capabilities

  • Articulating blockers clearly without defensive phrasing
  • Negotiating timelines and scope changes with PMs
  • Passing international tech job interviews & system design
🛎️ 3. Hospitality & Tourism Track Hotels, Restaurants, Concierge & F&B

Tailored for front-desk personnel, concierges, restaurant managers, sommeliers, and tourism professionals providing premium customer experiences to international patrons.

🏨 Target Audience

  • Hotel front-desk agents & reservation specialists
  • Head concierges & luxury guest relations managers
  • Restaurant hosts, servers, and sommeliers
  • Tour guides & event hospitality coordinators

📚 Thematic Lexicon & Syntax

  • Diplomatic politeness registers & formal honorifics
  • Detailed culinary vocabulary, allergens, and pairings
  • De-escalation templates for dissatisfied guests
  • Local cultural recommendations and VIP arrangements

⚡ Demands & Prerequisite

  • Entry Level: Minimum CEFR A2+ / B1
  • Flawless pronunciation and welcoming vocal prosody
  • Quick-thinking active listening under high-stress hours

🎯 Key Skills & Capabilities

  • Resolving billing disputes with graceful discretion
  • Presenting complex wine lists and tasting menus
  • Delivering personalized concierge advice to clientele
⚖️ 4. Juridical & Translation Track Lawyers, Corporate Counsel & Legal Translators

Created for legal practitioners, corporate compliance officers, paralegals, and professional translators who navigate bilateral commercial contracts, regulatory statutes, and cross-border disputes.

🏛️ Target Audience

  • Commercial & corporate lawyers
  • In-house legal counsels & compliance officers
  • Certified legal translators & court interpreters
  • Cross-border M&A consultants

📚 Thematic Lexicon & Syntax

  • Contractual clauses: warranties, indemnity, force majeure
  • Comparative terminology: common vs civil law
  • Precision drafting: avoiding ambiguous modal verbs
  • Statutory compliance, GDPR, and intellectual property

⚡ Demands & Prerequisite

  • Entry Level: Minimum CEFR B2
  • Zero tolerance for ambiguity or false friends (faux amis)
  • Analysis of real bilingual contracts during sessions

🎯 Key Skills & Capabilities

  • Negotiating contract modifications clause-by-clause
  • Translating statutory texts with rigorous equivalence
  • Advising boards on regulatory risks with precision

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can we use my actual company documents or academic papers in class?
Yes, absolutely. In fact, we strongly encourage it. Our teachers sign strict confidentiality non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) if required. Working directly on your upcoming slide decks, code reviews, email correspondences, or paper drafts delivers immediate return on investment.
What if my current general language level is only A2?
If you are at an A2 level, jumping directly into legal arbitration or academic peer reviews will lead to cognitive overload. We recommend taking our General Course for 2–3 months to solidify core grammar and vocabulary, before transitioning into a specialized Professional Track once you reach B1.
Do you offer corporate invoicing for employer reimbursement?
Yes. We supply formal invoices, certificates of attendance, and competency progress reports suitable for corporate training budgets, HR departments, and employee development programs.
How does the teacher know my industry's specific jargon?
Our instructors are experienced professionals with backgrounds in international business, academic research, translation, and tech environments. Furthermore, we conduct an extensive intake analysis of your specialized domain vocabulary before lesson one to tailor materials to your niche.
