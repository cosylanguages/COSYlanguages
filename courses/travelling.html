Travelling Language Course ✈️

Travel with genuine autonomy and cultural intimacy. Eliminate dependence on automated translation apps and phrasebooks. Rehearse high-stakes, real-world situations out loud: airport border control, lost luggage, hotel check-ins, authentic regional menus, transit strikes, and emergency healthcare.

🧭 Course Selection Compass: Is the Travelling Course Necessary For You?

✅ This course is necessary if:

  • You have an upcoming vacation, road trip, or international business visit (in 2 to 8 weeks) and want fast-track survival fluency without getting bogged down in grammar theory.
  • You want to explore off-the-beaten-path regions where locals, taxi drivers, and café staff speak little or no English.
  • You want to order meals with confidence, ask for local recommendations, bargain at outdoor markets, and handle unexpected mishaps (delays, lost property, pharmacy needs).

⚠️ Otherwise, choose another course:

  • Choose the Relocation Course if you are moving permanently or long-term, needing rental lease contracts, visa administration, tax registration, and social integration.
  • Choose the General Course if you want complete, balanced mastery of reading, writing, syntax, and structured CEFR level progression.
  • Choose the Professional Course if your travels involve corporate meetings, technical engineering reviews, or executive negotiation.

🗺️ Situational Travel Roadmap: From Departure to Local Connection

Our travel curriculum is structured as a chronological journey covering every realistic travel touchpoint:

🛫 Module 1: Transit, Border Control & Navigation High Stakes

Target: Navigate airports, international railway hubs, subways, and car rental agencies with zero anxiety.

🗣️ Roleplays & Scenarios

  • Customs & immigration: purpose of trip, length of stay, declaration
  • Train stations: buying tickets, validating platform stamps, requesting seat changes
  • Lost baggage claims: describing suitcase colors, materials, and contents

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Directions: straight ahead, turn right at the traffic lights, platform number
  • Transit announcements: delays, track changes, cancellations
  • Vehicle rentals: insurance coverage, fuel policies, reporting vehicle dents
🏨 Module 2: Accommodations & Resolving Hotel Issues Comfort & Service

Target: Check into hotels or guesthouses, negotiate room preferences, and assertively resolve lodging issues.

🗣️ Roleplays & Scenarios

  • Early check-in, late check-out, and luggage storage arrangements
  • Lodging complaints: non-working A/C or heating, missing towels, street noise
  • Requesting local concierge tips: quiet non-touristy restaurants, safe walking routes

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Room amenities: wi-fi password, adapter, keycard malfunction
  • Polite assertions: "Could you please check...", "The room seems to be..."
  • Payment & city taxes: tourist tax, invoice breakdown, deposit refunds
🍷 Module 3: Culinary Immersion & Restaurant Culture Cultural Delights

Target: Read authentic untranslated menus, order food with confidence, state food allergies, and understand tipping customs.

🗣️ Roleplays & Scenarios

  • Booking a table by phone and requesting an outdoor terrace seat
  • Navigating regional specialties, cooking styles, and wine pairings
  • Expressing food restrictions (vegetarian, gluten-free, nut allergies)

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Meats, cheeses, seafood, produce, and local cooking terms
  • Requesting the bill, splitting payments, and local tipping etiquette
  • Complimenting the chef and expressing authentic appreciation
🚑 Module 4: Emergencies, Healthcare & Safety Crucial Safety

Target: Communicate urgent needs to pharmacists, emergency doctors, or police officers if unexpected events occur.

🗣️ Roleplays & Scenarios

  • Pharmacy consultations: describing fever, sprains, stomach upsets, insect bites
  • Reporting a lost wallet or stolen passport at the local police station (commissariat/questura)
  • Contacting emergency services and describing exact physical locations

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Anatomical terms, pain descriptors (sharp, dull, throbbing), dosages
  • Emergency keywords: help, emergency, fire, police, ambulance, prescription
  • Official document loss statements for consular travel certificates

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I only have 3 weeks before my trip. Is that enough time?
Yes! Even 6 to 8 hours of targeted conversational rehearsal on high-frequency scenarios (ordering, asking directions, hotel check-in) completely transforms your travel experience. You will stop panicking and feel welcomed by locals.
Can my partner or travel companion join the lesson?
Yes. We welcome duos! Practicing dialogues together as travel companions makes the sessions exceptionally fun and realistic for your journey.
Will we learn about local culture and etiquette?
Absolutely. Cultural faux-pas often matter more than grammar mistakes. You will learn the cultural nuances: how to greet shopkeepers in France, coffee ordering rituals in Italy, toast customs in Russia, and hospitality etiquette (filoxenia) in Greece.
