Spoken Course 🗣️

Experience real conversation with your teacher in 15 or 30 minute focused sessions, free from rigid grammar tables and repetitive worksheets.

💬 Message us on WhatsApp See prices

Who it's for

Learners who mainly want to talk. Good if you understand more than you can say, or find grammar tables and worksheets a chore.

What you'll do

  • Have real conversations with your teacher.
  • Work in focused 15 or 30 minute sessions.
  • Practise speaking without rigid grammar tables or repetitive worksheets.

Languages

Available in English (taught since 2020), French (since 2022), Italian (since 2022), Russian (since 2022), and Greek (since 2025).

👥 Group lessons: Available in English, French, Italian, and Russian.

How lessons work

Lessons are conducted 1-to-1 in short, intense, high-focus bursts with a speaking-first approach.

Durations offered: 15 minutes or 30 minutes.
