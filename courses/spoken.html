🧭 Course Selection Compass: Is the Spoken Course Necessary For You?

Ensure the Spoken Course fits your personal study style:

✅ This course is necessary if...
  • You suffer from the "Passive-Active Gap": you can read articles, understand podcasts or movies, but when an actual human speaks to you, you freeze or take 20 seconds to construct a simple sentence.
  • You have zero interest in filling out written grammar worksheets, memorizing isolated conjugations, or doing traditional homework exercises.
  • Your schedule is packed, and you prefer 15 or 30-minute intense conversational workouts 3 to 5 days a week to build genuine automaticity.
⚠️ Otherwise, choose another course if...
  • Choose the General Course if you are a beginner with no prior grammar foundation, or if you need to develop formal writing, reading comprehension, and balanced CEFR certification skills.
  • Choose the Exam Preparation Course if you have to pass an official certification (IELTS, TOEFL, DELF, CILS) with strict written and oral rubric criteria.
  • Choose the Professional Course if you need industry-specific workplace communication for IT, Law, Academia, or Luxury Hospitality.

🗺️ Spoken Fluency Roadmap: Overcoming the Speaking Barrier

Our spoken curriculum tracks progression across distinct communicative hurdles rather than static written grammar chapters:

1
🌱 Stage 1: Breaking Silence & Sound Production (A1 – A2) Overcoming Insecurity

Target: Eliminate the fear of sounding foolish. Train facial muscles and vocal tract for authentic rhythm, correct intonation, and direct question-response automaticity without mental translation.

🎯 Conversational Skills

  • Immediate answers to personal inquiries without long pauses
  • Survival conversational repairs: asking for repetition, reformulating
  • Everyday narrative flow: describing your weekend in 3 continuous minutes

🧠 Anxiety-Reduction Techniques

  • Error tolerance protocol: teacher notes errors quietly without interrupting rhythm
  • Chunking method: learning set phrases ("I'd say that...", "Actually...")
  • Micro-monologues on high-comfort personal topics
2
🌿 Stage 2: Spontaneous Reaction & Active Debate (B1 – B2) Conversational Independence

Target: Speak without advance preparation. Defend personal opinions, navigate disagreements diplomatically, interrupt politely, and sustain 15 minutes of uninterrupted topical conversation.

🎯 Conversational Skills

  • Expressing strong agreement, partial concurrence, and polite dissent
  • Using conversational discourse fillers naturally (rather than native sounds)
  • Real-time story narration with vivid sensory vocabulary and past tenses

🧠 Anxiety-Reduction Techniques

  • Rapid-fire reaction drills: reacting spontaneously to provocative headlines
  • De-escalating the "inner perfectionist": focusing on message transmission
  • Immediate audio playback analysis during final 5 minutes of each session
3
🌳 Stage 3: Nuance, Irony & Near-Native Polish (C1 – C2) Effortless Rhetoric

Target: Grasp subtle cultural humor, sarcastic subtext, and colloquial idioms. Pivot between formal register and street slang effortlessly with native prosody and rhythm.

🎯 Conversational Skills

  • Engaging in rapid repartee, humor, sarcasm, and playful debate
  • Handling spontaneous philosophical and societal controversies without hesitation
  • Mastery of regional accents, colloquial abbreviations, and cultural metaphors

🧠 Advanced Rhetorical Polish

  • Vocal intonation modulation to convey skepticism, empathy, or excitement
  • Unfiltered conversation with native interlocutors at natural fast-talking speeds
  • Critique of cinema, literature, geopolitics, and human psychology

Why 15 or 30-Minute High-Frequency Sessions Work

Cognitive linguistics proves that daily 15 or 30-minute high-frequency oral immersion is vastly superior to a single 2-hour lesson once a week for speaking fluency:

  • Cognitive Stamina: In a 15-minute 1-to-1 session, you speak for ~12 full minutes. In a 60-minute passive classroom, you rarely speak more than 5 minutes total.
  • Neuroplasticity & Habit Formation: Daily conversational contact creates strong neural pathways, preventing the brain from slipping back into native-language default modes.
  • Zero Homework Burden: You log into the call, talk, receive real-time pronunciation and phrasing adjustments, and finish your lesson energized for your workday.

Languages Offered: English, French, Italian, Russian, and Greek.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What if I don't know what to talk about?
You will never run out of topics. Our teachers are trained conversational facilitators armed with hundreds of curated discussion prompts, recent cultural controversies, philosophical thought experiments, visual stimuli, and breaking news articles. We also build topics around your personal hobbies, career, and intellectual interests.
Will the teacher interrupt me every time I make a grammar mistake?
No! Constant interruptions destroy conversational momentum and reinforce speaking anxiety. Instead, our teachers use delayed feedback: they type notes in your shared document while you speak, and at the end of the conversation topic, you review the phrasing together, practicing the natural corrections out loud.
Can I really make progress in just 15 minutes a day?
Yes. Consistency outperforms duration. Four 15-minute sessions a week produce 60 minutes of pure, uninterrupted 1-to-1 talking time. Students consistently report a massive reduction in mental translation lag within 3 to 4 weeks.
Can absolute beginners take the Spoken Course?
Absolute beginners (true A0) are strongly advised to start with our General Course for at least 4 to 6 weeks to acquire the minimal phonetics and 200 essential words needed to hold a basic exchange. From false-beginner (A1) upward, the Spoken Course is highly effective.
