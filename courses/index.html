🎓 The CEFR Framework & Pedagogical Architecture

All COSY courses are aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Our communicative method ensures that no matter your target level, at least 40% of every lesson is dedicated to active, unscripted oral production, backed by deep grammatical competence, lexical precision, and strategic listening.

A1
🌱 Breakthrough & Discovery 0 – 100 Guided Hours

Construct simple affirmative, negative, and interrogative sentences. Form basic questions and express daily physical needs with clear phonetics.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Present simple indicative & core auxiliaries (be, have)
  • Articles, gender/number noun agreement, core pronouns
  • Basic word order (SVO) & question formation

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~600–800 words)

  • Self-introductions, family, home, food, numbers 0–1,000
  • Daily routines, telling time, basic café transactions
A2
🌿 Waystage & Survival 100 – 200 Guided Hours

Communicate in routine tasks requiring a direct exchange of information. Narrate past events and describe immediate surroundings.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Simple past / passé composé / passato prossimo / прошедшее
  • Future tenses, modal auxiliaries (can, must, should)
  • Comparatives, superlatives, direct/indirect pronouns

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~1,200–1,600 words)

  • Shopping, city navigation, vacations, weather
  • Health complaints, pharmacy visits, personal interests
B1
🌾 Threshold & Independence 200 – 400 Guided Hours

Enter unprepared into conversations on familiar topics. Express opinions, reason logically, and handle unexpected situations when traveling.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Past continuous vs past simple, imperfect vs perfective aspect
  • First & second conditionals, passive voice, relative clauses
  • Discourse connectors: cause, contrast, consequence

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~2,500–3,200 words)

  • Workplace environments, media, current affairs, social trends
  • Abstract concepts, personal ambitions, storytelling
B2
🌲 Vantage & Fluency 400 – 600 Guided Hours

Achieve operational independence. Interact with native speakers fluently without strain; understand technical articles and defend viewpoints with nuance.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Subjunctive mood (doubt, emotion, necessity), mixed conditionals
  • Advanced passive, causative structures, reported speech nuances
  • Participle clauses, gerunds vs infinitives with subtle shifts

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~4,500–6,000 words)

  • Global politics, ethics, technological disruption, art analysis
  • Idiomatic expressions, phrasal verbs, workplace negotiations
C1
🌳 Effective Operational Proficiency 600 – 800 Guided Hours

Master stylistic nuance, irony, and rhetorical elegance. Use language flexibly for academic, professional, and literary purposes.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Cleft sentences, inversion/fronting for dramatic emphasis
  • Subtle modal auxiliaries denoting nuanced speculation
  • Nominalization and advanced academic synthesis

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~8,000–10,000 words)

  • Philosophy, jurisprudence, financial markets, sociology
  • Academic critique, rhetorical oratory, cultural metaphors
C2
🏛️ Mastery & Bilingual Polish 800+ Guided Hours

Near-native linguistic intuition. Reconstruct arguments from diverse sources coherently and express fine shades of meaning effortlessly.

🧩 Grammar Inventory

  • Full mastery of historical, literary, and regional syntax variants
  • Micro-nuances of prosody, stress, intonation contouring, and cadence

📚 Vocabulary Themes (~15,000+ words)

  • Complete cultural subtext, historical idioms, regional dialects
  • Classical literature, specialized scientific papers, jurisprudence

🧭 Course Selection Decision Matrix

Not sure which course fits your current stage and goals? Use this comparison guide to find your optimal path:

Course Choose This Course If... Key Focus Areas Otherwise, Choose Another If...
📖 General You want structured, balanced mastery of all 4 language skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking) with steady CEFR progression. Grammar rules, vocabulary breadth, phonetics, structured writing, real-world conversational drills. Choose Spoken if you already understand grammar and only want conversation; choose Exam Prep if you have an exam date in <6 months.
🗣️ Spoken You have passive grammar knowledge but freeze when speaking, or you want high-frequency 15–30 min conversational workouts. Spontaneous reaction, oral fluency, idiomatic expressions, confidence building, active listening. Choose General if you need to learn grammar from scratch or require formal writing/reading credentials.
📝 Exam Prep You are registered for or preparing to sit an official test (IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge, DELF/DALF, CILS, CELI, TORFL, etc.). Exam rubrics, timed tasks, writing syntheses, oral monologue/dialogue, mock papers with scoring feedback. Choose General if your current CEFR level is too far below target exam requirements (build the foundation first).
💼 Professional You need language for career performance in Academic, IT/Tech, Hospitality, or Juridical/Legal fields. Domain-specific vocabulary, executive presentations, contract drafting/reading, tech standups, guest resolution. Choose General if your baseline language level is below B1; specialized jargon requires an intermediate foundation.
🏡 Relocation You are relocating or have recently moved abroad and need to navigate daily administration, leases, healthcare, and social life. Administrative forms, rental contracts, doctor visits, tax registrations, utility setup, expat social integration. Choose Professional if your daily living needs are settled and you strictly require workplace/corporate communication.
✈️ Travelling You are taking a vacation or business trip and want to travel independently without relying on digital translation apps. High-frequency travel vocabulary, directions, hotel issues, restaurant ordering, transit, emergency phrasing. Choose Relocation if you intend to stay permanently or sign long-term tenancy and employment contracts.
