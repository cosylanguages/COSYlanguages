Exam Preparation 📝

Authentic tasks, exact exam conditions. Focus on the oral component where it matters most.

💬 Message us on WhatsApp See prices

Who it's for

Learners preparing for a language exam who want to feel ready on the day, especially for the speaking part.

What you'll do

  • Work through authentic exam-style tasks.
  • Practise under exact exam conditions.
  • Focus on the oral component where it matters most.
  • Tell us which exam you're taking and we'll match the tasks to it.

Languages

Available in English (taught since 2020), French (since 2022), Italian (since 2022), Russian (since 2022), and Greek (since 2025).

👥 Group lessons: Available in English, French, Italian, and Russian.

How lessons work

1-to-1 intensive coaching sessions structured around target exam rubrics and timed task execution.

Durations offered: 120 minutes intensive mock sessions (or standard 60/90 minutes based on target exam requirements).
