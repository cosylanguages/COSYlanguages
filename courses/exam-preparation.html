🧭 Course Selection Compass: Is Exam Preparation Necessary For You?

✅ This course is necessary if:

  • You have a booked or impending test date for an internationally accredited certificate (IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge, DELF, DALF, TCF, CILS, CELI, TORFL, or Ellinomatheia) required for university admission, immigration, or professional licensing.
  • Your overall language level is already close to or matches the target CEFR band, but you need to master test structure, timing, task rubrics, and avoid losing points on oral or essay synthesis.
  • You need realistic full-length mock examinations with objective examiner scoring and strategic error remediation.

⚠️ Otherwise, choose another course:

  • Choose the General Course if your current language level is significantly below the exam requirement (e.g. you are currently A2 and need B2/C1; you must build foundational grammar and vocabulary first).
  • Choose the Spoken Course if you have no exam deadlines and purely want unconstrained conversational fluency without rigorous scoring rubrics.
  • Choose the Professional Course if you need language for your corporate workplace (IT, Hospitality, Legal) rather than an academic standardized test.

🏆 Official Certifications Supported Across Languages

We provide tailored test-taking preparation matching the exact testing frameworks established by national ministries of education and international testing consortia:

🇬🇧 English: IELTS, TOEFL iBT & Cambridge B1 to C2 Levels

Targeted preparation for IELTS Academic & General Training (Bands 6.0 – 8.5+), TOEFL iBT (80 – 115+), and Cambridge Qualifications (B2 First / FCE, C1 Advanced / CAE, C2 Proficiency / CPE).

🗣️ Oral / Speaking 11–15 min

  • Part 1: Personal interview and prompt familiarity (4–5 min)
  • Part 2: 2-minute individual long turn monologue with 1-min preparation
  • Part 3: Abstract two-way thematic discussion and opinion defense (4–5 min)
  • Examiner Rubric: Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Pronunciation.

✍️ Writing Component 60 min

  • Task 1 (20 min, 150 words): Synthesize data from graphs/charts/diagrams or draft formal correspondence
  • Task 2 (40 min, 250 words): Academic discursive essay arguing an issue with examples
  • Key Demands: Cohesive devices, academic register, thesis statement clarity, zero word-count deficits.

🎧 Listening Component 30–40 min

  • 4 sections of increasing complexity: everyday social dialogues to complex university lectures
  • 40 questions (multiple choice, sentence completion, diagram labeling)
  • Strategy: Predicting keywords, managing distractor options, tracking signpost language.

📖 Reading Component 60 min

  • 3 long authentic academic texts (approx. 2,150–2,750 words total)
  • True / False / Not Given, headings matching, summary completion
  • Strategy: Skimming for gist, scanning for specific data, managing strict 20-min per passage limits.
🇫🇷 French: DELF, DALF & TCF France Éducation International

Official diplomas awarded by the French Ministry of National Education: DELF (A1, A2, B1, B2), DALF (C1, C2), and TCF (Test de Connaissance du Français for university or nationality).

🗣️ Production Orale 15–30 min

  • Entretien dirigé: Spontaneous presentation of self and background
  • Exercice en interaction: Role-play resolving a dispute or negotiation
  • Monologue suivi / Exposé (B2/C1): 10-minute presentation analyzing an article followed by rigorous debate with examiners.

✍️ Production Écrite 45–90 min

  • B1/B2: Formal letter of complaint, essay arguing a societal problem (250 words)
  • C1: Synthesis of multiple technical documents (220 words) + persuasive argumentative essay (250 words)
  • Key Demands: Mastery of French subjunctive, logical connectors (cependant, en revanche, d'autant plus que).

🎧 Compréhension Orale 25–40 min

  • Radio broadcasts (France Inter, RFI), press conferences, public interviews
  • Multiple choice and short response questions
  • Technique: Handling authentic French contractions, rapid colloquial tempo, and nuanced registers.

📖 Compréhension Écrite 35–60 min

  • Articles from Le Monde, Courrier International, administrative texts
  • Extracting implicit ideological stances and identifying tone/satire.
🇮🇹 Italian: CILS, CELI & PLIDA Universities of Siena, Perugia & Dante Alighieri

Legally recognized certificates required for Italian university matriculation, citizenship (B1 Cittadinanza), and professional register enrollment.

🗣️ Produzione Orale 10–15 min

  • Dialogo con l'esaminatore: Real-world situational roleplay
  • Monologo: Visual prompt or text analysis, structured argumentation
  • Scoring focus: Natural pronunciation, congiuntivo accuracy, idiomatic Italian expressions.

✍️ Produzione Scritta 60–90 min

  • Due prove: Formal letter/email to institutions + narrative or expository essay
  • Focus: Syntax harmony, correct prepositions with verbs, rich vocabulary.

🎧 Ascolto & Comprensione 30–45 min

  • Radio recordings, authentic conversations, academic lectures
  • Testing comprehension of implicit meaning and regional accents.

🧩 Analisi delle Strutture 60 min

  • Cloze tests, morphological transformations, preposition insertion
  • In-depth test of Italian grammar mechanics and idioms.
🇷🇺 Russian: TORFL / ТРКИ (I to IV) State Testing System

Test of Russian as a Foreign Language (Тест по русскому языку как иностранному). Standard requirement for admission to Russian universities and certified translation licenses.

🗣️ Говорение (Speaking) 25–45 min

  • Participating in dialogues with specific communicative goals
  • Expressing evaluation of a heard or read text; spontaneous interview
  • Scoring: Intonation constructions (ИК-1 to ИК-7), verbs of motion, correct case usage.

✍️ Письмо (Writing) 50–80 min

  • ТРКИ-1 (B1): Drafting an informal/formal letter and reproductive summary of a text
  • ТРКИ-2/3 (B2/C1): Analytical report, official appeal, critical essay based on multiple sources.

🎧 Аудирование (Listening) 35–45 min

  • News broadcasts, film dialogues, scientific or cultural lectures
  • Evaluating intentions, emotions, and cause-and-effect relationships.

🧩 Лексика. Грамматика 60–90 min

  • Rigorous testing of Russian nominal/adjectival case inflections, aspect pairs (СВ/НСВ), participle & gerund constructions.
🇬🇷 Greek: Certificate of Attainment in Greek (Ελληνομάθεια) Centre for the Greek Language

The only globally recognized qualification for Modern Greek, administered by the Ministry of Education of Greece (Πιστοποίηση Επάρκειας της Ελληνομάθειας A1–C2).

🗣️ Παραγωγή Προφορικού Λόγου 10–15 min

  • Warm-up conversation, picture description, structured dialogue or debate with co-candidate
  • Emphasis on natural stress placement, nominal declension precision, and aspectual verb forms.

✍️ Παραγωγή Γραπτού Λόγου 45–75 min

  • Informal and formal correspondence, essay expressing personal standpoint with structured arguments.

🎧 Κατανόηση Προφορικού 30–40 min

  • Radio announcements, conversations, interviews in standard Modern Greek
  • Distinguishing key factual details from supporting opinions.

📖 Κατανόηση Γραπτού Λόγου 45–60 min

  • Newspaper articles, historical excerpts, literary selections with comprehension queries.

⚡ Demands, Rigor & Test-Taking Capabilities Built

Success in official language exams demands more than general conversation—it requires strategic execution under psychological and chronological pressure:

  • Chronological Precision: Every task is practiced under strict stopwatch conditions so you instinctively know how to allocate minutes during the live exam.
  • Rubric Alignment: You learn exactly what examiners listen and look for: lexical density, varied syntactic architecture, avoidance of repetitive vocabulary, and cohesive flow.
  • Speaking Composure: We run rigorous mock speaking interviews where you practice speaking continuously for 2 minutes without hesitation, filler words, or freezing.
  • Diagnostic Writing Evaluation: Every submitted essay or synthesis receives thorough line-by-line markups indicating task achievement, grammatical range, lexical sophistication, and cohesive structuring.

Recommended Lesson Formats: 90 or 120-minute intensive double sessions to simulate actual examination conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How far in advance should I begin exam preparation?
If your current CEFR level already matches the exam tier (e.g. you are currently B2 and need to pass DELF B2 or IELTS 6.5), we recommend 6 to 10 weeks of focused exam-technique preparation. If you need to jump one full CEFR level (e.g. from B1 to B2), you will typically need 4 to 6 months of combined general language elevation and exam drills.
Do you administer the official exams directly?
No. Official certification exams are administered by accredited examination centres (British Council, IDP, Alliance Française, Institut Français, Società Dante Alighieri, official state universities). We provide the rigorous preparatory training and mock testing so you arrive fully primed to succeed on exam day.
Will we do full-length mock exams?
Yes. Full timed mock tests are an essential milestone of our preparation. You will complete authentic past papers under exact exam timing, followed by an in-depth score debriefing and targeted remediation of weak areas.
What if I only struggle with the oral or writing part?
Because our 1-to-1 coaching is entirely bespoke, we can dedicate 80% or 100% of your lesson time exclusively to the oral interview or writing tasks while you do the reading and listening independently.
