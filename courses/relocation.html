🧭 Course Selection Compass: Is the Relocation Course Necessary For You?

Clarify whether settlement language coaching is your main priority:

✅ This course is necessary if...
  • You are moving permanently or long-term to France, the UK, the US, Italy, Greece, or Russian-speaking regions and need to manage daily life without hiring expensive translation coordinators.
  • You feel overwhelmed by local paperwork: residency permits (titre de séjour, permesso di soggiorno), tax identification numbers (tax code, codice fiscale, AFM), rental deposits, and medical reimbursements.
  • You want to build genuine local relationships: chatting with neighbors, participating in school PTA meetings, and understanding civic culture.
⚠️ Otherwise, choose another course if...
  • Choose the Travelling Course if you are only taking a short holiday or visiting for a few weeks without permanent administrative obligations.
  • Choose the Professional Course if your relocation life is already organized and you specifically need corporate language for software development, law, or academic research.
  • Choose the Exam Preparation Course if you need an official certificate (e.g. B1 citizenship exam or university matriculation diploma).

🗺️ The 4 Pillars of Expat Settlement Roadmap

We systematically address the real-world obstacles every newcomer encounters during the first 12 months abroad:

1
📑 Pillar 1: Housing, Leases & Utilities Setup The First Month

Target: Communicate directly with real estate agents, decipher rental agreements, report maintenance issues, and register household electricity, water, and internet contracts.

🏡 Practical Competencies

  • Inquiring about listings, scheduling viewings, asking about landlord deposits and fees
  • Reading clauses: notice periods, utility charges (charges comprises / spese condominiali)
  • Setting up accounts with telecom providers and utility companies

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Housing terms: guarantor, security deposit, inventory check (état des lieux)
  • Assertive issue reporting: water leaks, mold, radiator bleeding, locksmith services
2
🏛️ Pillar 2: Municipal Bureaucracy & Banking Official Status

Target: Attend municipal appointments, open bank accounts, register for tax IDs, and handle residency permit renewals with calm composure.

🏛️ Practical Competencies

  • Opening checking accounts, understanding international transfer fees and card limits
  • Visiting prefecture/town hall (mairie / comune) for civil registration
  • Interpreting official postal notices, tax declarations, and certified apostilles

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Bureaucratic terms: proof of address (justificatif de domicile), certified translation, fiscal code
  • Clarification: "Which additional supporting document is required for this file?"
3
🩺 Pillar 3: Healthcare, Pharmacies & Insurance Vital Wellbeing

Target: Choose a general practitioner (GP), communicate medical histories, understand prescription instructions, and navigate national health insurance reimbursement schemes.

🩺 Practical Competencies

  • Booking doctor appointments, describing chronic ailments and allergic reactions
  • Understanding prescription dosages and pharmacy label warnings
  • Navigating health cards (Carte Vitale, Tessera Sanitaria, AMKA)

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Symptom terminology: chronic vs acute pain, specialist referrals, lab bloodwork orders
  • Insurance claims: co-payments, deductions, hospital admission procedures
4
🤝 Pillar 4: Neighborhood Community & Family Life Thriving Locally

Target: Overcome social isolation. Chat with neighborhood merchants, participate in local community events, converse with teachers during school conferences, and make local friends.

🤝 Practical Competencies

  • Parent-teacher meetings: discussing adaptation, grades, and school clubs
  • Neighborly etiquette: building renovation notices, package reception
  • Joining sports clubs, local associations (associations de quartier)

📚 Key Vocabulary & Phrasing

  • Warm conversational openers, local community slang, polite dinner acceptances
  • Expressing gratitude, small talk about local festivities, regional traditions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can we practice my actual upcoming administrative appointment?
Yes, 100%. If you have an upcoming appointment at the prefecture, the police station, the bank, or a rental viewing, we simulate the exact dialogue in advance. You will walk into the room knowing the exact questions you will be asked and how to answer them without hesitation.
Can the teacher help me understand letters received from the government?
Yes! Official letters are notoriously full of dense legalese and administrative jargon. During your lessons, you and your teacher can read the correspondence together, identifying the required deadlines and action items.
I have children who need to enroll in local school. Can this course help?
Absolutely. We cover school registration forms, canteen meal selections, after-school care sign-ups, and the essential vocabulary needed for parent-teacher conferences.
