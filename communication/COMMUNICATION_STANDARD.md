# COSYlanguages Communication & Speech Standard v1.0

This document defines the strict, unified standards for all communication, pragmatics, and speech materials across COSYlanguages (Book 3). Every unit, guide, and interactive scenario must adhere 100% to this specification.

---

## 1. Core Pedagogical Principles

### Principle 1: Pragmatic Intent Over Mechanical Translation
Communication materials must teach functional goals (e.g., *softening a refusal*, *buying time*, *asking for clarification*) rather than direct word-for-word translation.

### Principle 2: Standardized Unit Rhythm
Every communication unit page in `communication-manual/` must follow a predictable, intuitive visual sequence:

1. **Unit Title & Functional Goal:** Clear statement of communicative goal (e.g., *How to politely decline an invitation*).
2. **Context & Register Bar:** Visual tags indicating register (Casual, Neutral, Formal) and typical setting (Social, Workplace, Digital).
3. **Dialogue Flow Diagram / Visual Blueprint:** A step-by-step CSS flowchart showing opening, core exchange, and closing sequence.
4. **Key Expressions & Nuance Cards:** Functional phrases categorized by register and social intention.
5. **👀 Notice (Guided Pragmatic Observation):** An inductive question highlighting tone, politeness, or intonation patterns.
6. **🗣️ Natural Alternatives & Fillers:** Native speech shortcuts, hesitation strategies, and natural reaction tokens.
7. **⚠ Social Pitfalls & Misunderstandings:** Contrast between blunt/awkward phrasing and natural/polite alternatives.
8. **💡 Cultural / Pragmatic Memory Trick:** Actionable tip for remembering social rules or conversation flow.
9. **🔗 Cross-Manual Connections:** Links to matching structural topics in Book 1 (Grammar) and lexical topics in Book 2 (Vocabulary).
10. **📍 Can you use this in real life?:** A communication self-check checklist.
11. **🎉 Interactive Scenario / Practice:** Quizzes or roleplay prompts reinforcing real-time application.

## 2. Visual Style & Color Language

| Element | Color | Semantic Purpose |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Casual / Friendly** | Green (`#1c9483` / `var(--brand-green)`) | Informal speech with friends and family |
| **Neutral / Standard** | Blue (`#3562e0` / `var(--brand-blue)`) | Everyday public and workplace speech |
| **Formal / Polite** | Purple (`#7c4fd6` / `var(--brand-purple)`) | Official, customer service, or senior contexts |
| **Social Pitfall / Warning** | Red (`#a3195b` / `var(--brand-red)`) | Blunt, rude, or easily misunderstood phrasing |
